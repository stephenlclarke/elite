# Navigation and trade practice runtime translation

This is schema-2 runtime content translated from the approved original L337 family design. It is currently unavailable in the public mission list because the original-world catalogue has no installed native endpoint binding. Installing this directory does not make predecessor worlds original, supply a binding or certify the tutorial as playable.

At a genuinely bound training berth, select a reachable destination on the chart and a stocked legal tonne commodity on the market. Mission Contacts confirms actual fitting, capacity, stock and route and reserves one tonne of hold capacity. Use the normal single-tonne market controls to buy the identified tonne, H to perform the actual admitted journey, then sell it through the market at the selected destination. Buy actual fuel as needed and return to the issuing training berth for confirmed reception. Shared Confirm and Esc controls apply; closing a dialogue transfers nothing.

The market determines real prices and finite stock. A loss qualifies; reception pays no additional credits. The contact shows actual buy/sale differences, fuel units used and fuel credits paid. Cancel explicitly through the contact to release surviving cargo without recreating stock or refunding money. Actual cargo loss cannot be repaired by substituting another tonne. The first verified success records this family's permanent zero-mass clue; repeating the family cannot replace another source.

Native proof, custody, save combinations and validation limits are described in [the issue notes](../../ISSUE-navigation-trade-practice.md). The source-level translation and native fixtures do not establish original-world integration or full gameplay acceptance.
