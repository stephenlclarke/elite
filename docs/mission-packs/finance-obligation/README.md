# Fixed-principal finance demonstration

This original optional example offers 200.0 CR from a workshop lender at Galaxy 1, system 237's original main orbital station. It is not a historical GSE, Iron Raven or Bank of the Black Monks adaptation. Source and regression definitions are present; compilation, native execution and full gates remain with the integrator.

Install the complete folder as described in the [schema 2 guide](../../SCRIPT-MISSION-PACKS.md), bind a writable save, and inspect the entry in Mission Gallery. Try starts a separate commander at the declared system with 100.0 CR. Its initial dock does not prove a physical arrival. Launch, return through the actual original main-station doorway, and open Station Contracts. The native Finance row shows the exact principal and real balance. Borrow requires explicit Confirm; Back changes nothing and Decline ends the offer. A full credit balance refuses Borrow but still allows Decline.

Repay collects `min(real balance, outstanding principal)`. A partial payment carries the remainder; zero balance leaves the debt open. No interest, deadline, automatic collection, arbitrary payment editor or virtual XYZZY balance applies. Spending some of the loan through the real market lets you demonstrate a partial payment; later real income can settle the remainder. Each payment requires a fresh review. Reading, Help, scrolling and painting do not transfer money.

After borrowing, open Mission Contacts to finish the demonstration. Its accepted liability remains independently available in Station Contracts, including if the parent content becomes unavailable. Save/load retains receipts; writer failure preserves cash, debt and the previous save generation. Gallery Restart replaces the trial's own setup, and Return restores the live commander and writer without copying trial debt or credits.

The manifest's `[[loans]]` fields are `id`, `profile = "fixed-principal-v1"`, stable `creditor`, human `title`, integer `principal` in tenths of a credit, `galaxy` (0-7) and `system` (0-256, including PLUGH). The handler command `{op="finance.offer",id="workshop"}` registers an exact offer once at its declared active contact; it cannot issue money. `w.finance.workshop` exposes read-only `status`, `principal`, `remaining` and receipt count. Native Finance controls own acceptance and repayment independently of Lua callbacks. Record limits are 128 offers and 1,024 total transfer/decline receipts; offers and closed history are retained rather than recycled to grant another principal.

The selected historical GSE profile separately requires 500,000 CR principal, 550,000 CR nominal settlement, calendar-day timing and eligible Galaxy 8 collection. Its short or zero payment closes the debt. Those historical rules, facilities, escrow and complete P04/P19 adaptation remain outstanding; this generic carried-principal product must not substitute for them. An independent [original capped simple-interest product](../finance-interest/README.md) is also authored, with central compilation/runtime qualification pending. This product remains explicitly interest-free.
