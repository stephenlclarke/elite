# Supplied freight jobs

This installable G03 variant supplies issuer-owned Food, Textiles or Machinery at the origin main station. It complements the separate procurement pack, whose goods the player buys. Neither pack is activated by default.

| Difficulty | Supplied cargo | Time allowed | Final reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 tonne | 2 mission days | 750 CR + 300 CR per required hop |
| 2 | 5 tonnes | 2 mission days | 1500 CR + 300 CR per required hop |
| 3 | 10 tonnes | 3 mission days | 2500 CR + 300 CR per required hop |

These are frozen project balance estimates. Each offer names a different reachable main-station destination, at most three shortest-route hops away. Its native route and commodity draws are frozen; acceptance does not reroll them. A tier that exceeds the ship's actual total hold is omitted from that board. Confirmation also requires room for the full supplied quantity alongside all existing cargo and occupied space. XYZzy unlimited capacity does not waive this requirement.

Acceptance charges no fee and issues uniquely owned one-tonne units. They appear separately in inventory information and count once toward the hold. Ordinary same-commodity goods cannot replace them, and market sales cannot consume them. A compatible hull exchange preserves the units; a hull or refit without enough actual room is refused.

At the destination main station, open the receiving contact. Up/Down selects **Deliver one tonne** or **Deliver all remaining freight**. Left/Right changes Back/Confirm; Return performs the selected action. Back and choice browsing change no saved cargo. Partial receipts remain recorded. Payment happens once, only after every original unit has been received by the real destination contact. Confirmation at the exact deadline is allowed; later confirmation is refused.

The original issuer's contact can abandon the job for 0 CR, returning only outstanding freight. Expiry, escape and ship destruction also settle for 0 CR and remove only outstanding owned units. Earlier deliveries remain recorded. Ordinary goods, equipment and unrelated assignments keep their normal rules. A failed final publication preserves the previous units and receipts for retry; a real fatal loss retains a fixed pending loss so it cannot become a delivery during retry.

The Gallery trial is freshly initialized at Avenor (`G01-S007`, galaxy 0/system 7), whose original TL12 ordinary shop supplies its paid fit. The declared 100000-tenths budget pays 86000 for Targeting Basic, Advanced and Docking, Pulse Shield Booster and Extra Energy Unit, leaving 14000. The trial then starts in genuine flight. The player must fly, dock at an actual main station, review an offer and Confirm before any freight is issued. No prior voyage, station arrival or acceptance is invented. Restart buys the same fit again with a fresh trial owner. Trial saves stay separate from the live commander.
