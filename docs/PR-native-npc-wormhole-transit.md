# Native authored NPC wormhole transit provider

## Native authority and lifecycle

The new provider accepts a stable instance/actor/request/destination leg, then resolves the exact installed original-world records, approved catalogue fingerprint and native `distanceLY` through G1's `Universe.originalWorlds`. Both live and retained routes rejoin that cache and the actual StarSystem records at the current endpoint. No name lookup, selected-system shortcut, copied save authority, BBC half-Y conversion or extra catalogue cache is used. Ordinary original worlds require their exact `G01-S000` style keys. Unknown keys and PLUGH without a separate authentic policy cannot produce a route.

A closed NPC drive declaration joins a surviving fitted component UUID and energy to explicit range, timed activation, mass-lock scale and the NPC's own `ScriptNPCState.fuel` reservoir in native light-years. Existing `NPCResources` fuel and commander fuel are separate reservoirs and are not spent for an NPC. The provider reuses `HyperspaceDrivePolicy`, actual actor-relative blockers, native EMP/electrical state and current source/owner/fit validation. Physical spool observations interrupt on module, power, fuel, binding or clearance loss. Calendar-only catch-up cannot replace loaded physical activation. Healthy waiting does not consume the finite refusal budget.

Opening records a bounded, rendered, oriented spatial aperture and registered actual native exit, with deterministic identity and 120 calendar-second expiry. Opening and steering confer no departure. Actual normal acceleration and turning carry the ship through the aperture; its measured enclosing hull must fit. The existing continuous native hull sweep and geometry cache validate scene clearance. Rotating obstacles and the player additionally require conservative measured-radius separation; missing/new/unproven scene motion refuses entry. Transit-controlled motion is admitted as a whole cohort before becoming evidence. Internal return/clearance waypoints do not emit generic `actor.arrived`. Writer refusal requires a retained source and another actual permitted crossing after further motion; it cannot publish a debit or detachment partially.

An accepted crossing freezes the actual complete body/native NPC condition after exactly one NPC fuel debit, detaches its source binding without a destruction/removal event and retains the stable logical actor UUID. It records native departure, route duration and due time. Due unloaded, blocked or unavailable destinations remain `arrivalPending`; a native visit/allocator/geometry/whole-actor admission transaction supplies the new binding before `actor.transit.arrived`. The immutable encounter declaration keeps its original source location; only a successful native arrival authorizes per-actor relocation. Source restoration excludes departed actors, and current scene descriptors/save consistency follow the proved relocated actor. Uncommanded arrivals clear the actual exit through existing flight limits. Repeated request/load/receipt cannot recreate the ship or recharge its resources. Native EMP/pod/salvage custody that the current mission admission seam cannot carry is refused rather than discarded. Existing mission EMP policy remains authoritative; electrical depletion unit coverage does not claim an EMP producer can currently disable mission actors.

Player entry requires the actual live opening, attained swept crossing, its own fitted working drive/range/fuel and native mass-lock/EMP guards. It uses the common actual native arrival path and registered native exit, with one fuel debit and one commander passage receipt. Native commander arrivals increment calendar and global `transitSeconds` once; concurrent NPC journeys never add to either clock. Transit timers dispatch only from that actual commander delta. Flight/local clocks receive no travel-hours adjustment. System/transit arrival supplies no station reception, escort settlement, clue or payment; those require their separate real authorities.

## Content, persistence and bounded work

`npc-wormhole-transit-v2` requires profiled/story/durable/calendar capabilities and an actual declared drive component. Closed manifest validation keeps courier capabilities/events and mission-calendar boards restricted. `actor.transit` and `actor.transit.retry` retain the exact leg; retry refreshes a bounded refusal/admission budget without a new identity or free fuel. The saved owner-bound ledger has at most 128 retained legs, at most 128 pre-departure refusals and 128 arrival attempts per loaded visit, with two-second backoff. New destination visits permit fresh pending admission; explicit retry permits another finite budget. Immutable geometry uses the existing bounded native cache; actual motion samples are retained only while a current opening needs observation. No Game/Commander storage owner or per-ship actor is introduced.

Current-format state preserves spool/native bindings, aperture expiry, route facts, full departure condition, due time, pending state and actual arrival history. Validation rejoins the owner, logical actor, native address bounds, exact fuel arithmetic, working component, route and calendar facts. In-transit actor queries expose no position. Native query reads are pure. No predecessor migration, compatibility alias or legacy world/save adapter is added.
