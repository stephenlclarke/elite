# Wire physical equipment ownership into actual purchases and fitting

The current game represents fittings mainly as names and counters. The agreed physical inventory, purchase history and component progression primitives exist, but they do not yet govern the actual shop, compatible recovered fitting, hull exchange or save migration. A high fitted tier must not fabricate paid predecessors or specialist modules, and repeated fit/load/exchange must not duplicate a physical device or heal/refill it.

Integrate the existing inventory and progression APIs at the actor-owned mutation boundaries. Persist one owner and active hull identity in the inventory, retain portable item identities and their original provenance, and validate the full capability context after load. Keep paid purchase history separate from earned/reward/XYZZY/recovered acquisition and explicit grandfathered legacy entitlement. Apply cash, source-item consumption, equipment, history and stat changes atomically on a candidate, with resource fractions preserved. Standard Basic shield, navigation and energy hardware are free foundations. Getters must never allocate identities or mutate ownership.

Acceptance includes migration of actual old values and held replaced devices, idempotent round-trip, all sequential shop prerequisites, real independent Mining/Salvage ownership, exact stale item/owned/source revision refusal, failed save/load/fit rollback, compatible hull transfer without identity/provenance changes, and matching compact shop/inventory/help displays. Required validation includes focused transaction regressions, actual native interactions and the full integrated quality gates. See the [implementation handoff](PR-equipment-runtime.md).

The next bounded gap is ordinary recovered feature devices: the salvage registry describes Mining/Salvage targeting modules, scoops, ECM and cutter devices, but the Inventory fitting path previously accepted only scalar upgrades. Wire these five definitions into existing canonical equipment and supported vacant physical slots, requiring an actual fitted targeting core for either specialty. Preserve the complete source lot through the existing permanent settlement receipt, including quality, condition and issued loot. An explicit action may fit in space; collecting must not install automatically. Refuse stale or altered painted actions, occupied/unsupported fittings, duplicate source ownership and missing receipt evidence without publishing partial state. Keep paid history, resource charges and scalar progression separate. No new condition threshold, health model, service price, missile/hardpoint or alien fitting belongs to this slice.
