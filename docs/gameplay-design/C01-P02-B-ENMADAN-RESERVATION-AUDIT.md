## Explicit new L337 source decisions

These identities, quantities and timings fill missing authoring inputs; they are not recovered historical values. Create the source once, atomically with the actual Venn commission, **before** any Renas scan or Navidor recorder admission. Its real native creation/operation times and sequences are recorded then, never backdated. Initialization requires that genuine fresh native commission, no prior source, no scan reading and no surveyor/admission/capture/loss. A source cannot be added later to make previously unbound evidence match. Existing unbound claims cannot be silently hydrated after recovery; no old-format/import/migration obligation is introduced.
