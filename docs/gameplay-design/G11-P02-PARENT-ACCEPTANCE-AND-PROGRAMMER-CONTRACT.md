## Accepted facts and explicit new decisions

The accepted “External isolation work” family requires one uniquely loaned one-tonne programmer, two named unmanned external modules, both physical isolations, same-issuer programmer return, and `760 + 105L + 80H` CR at genuine completion. Required berths, deposit and voluntary contribution are zero. Losses remain real; cancellation/failure pays no reward or clue. The existing family clue is `isolation-work-family-trace`, zero mass and once per family. The shared design requires explicit consent, stable offers, at most three concurrent Original/Wayfarer contracts, and no unquoted deadlines.

The approved P01 source fixes Lerr's actual issuer/receiver at **Avenor G01-S007**, the two original feeder modules at **Renas G01-S185**, their finite native resource effects, and the stable source/owner identities. It does not provide a parent acceptance or equipment issue authority.

**New L337 decisions, now Root-approved:** the initial quoted itinerary is the complete direct round trip `[G01-S007, G01-S185, G01-S007]`; **L = 2**, including return of the programmer. Its declared pricing tier is **H = 0**, with no new government-to-hazard mapping or safety promise. Therefore the frozen reward is **970 CR / 9700 native tenths**. There is no acceptance fee, deposit, bond, clock, offer expiry, deadline, late fee or failure reward. Existing schema encodes `offer.duration = 0` and `job.deadline = nil`, not a deadline equal to acceptance time. Physical supply exhaustion and actual source/tool loss remain possible under P01; these are not hidden job timers.

## One typed parent authority; one atomic issue

MC1 owns the single **`NativeIsolationWorkParentAuthority`** type already drafted in `NativeIsolationWork.swift`; its raw initializer remains private. Keep it non-Codable. G3 supplies typed primitive historical acceptance/issue proof to MC1's factory, not another authority or dictionary/Boolean grant. There cannot be a genuine post-issue authority before the actual issue has occurred.

Reuse `ScriptInstance.contract` for frozen offer/job terms, accepted/terminal outcome and settlement; reuse `gearIssues`, canonical inventory and existing equipment transaction machinery for the entire loan. G3 adds only the typed optional **`savedIsolationWorkParent`** record: parent/pack/owner, P01 source reservation, Lerr principal, genuine acceptance/consent operation/sequence/dock, hash of existing immutable contract terms, and sealed proof of the actual issue operation/source/original serial. An actual return/loss cutoff records its causal operation, physical time and acknowledged sequence when one occurs. These are proof links, not another item list, loan status, cargo budget or reward balance. Root reserves conditional **133/134** for this parent; provider **131/132** remains unchanged.

The authority projects parent ID/digest, authorization operation, owner, deterministic Lerr issuer, historical genuine acceptance dock, **`issueOperation: UUID` from the actual equipment issue receipt**, and the **current** carrier/inventory revision. Authentication rejoins the canonical parent, primitive proof, immutable contract and original source. Each runtime consumer also checks its lifecycle: work requires an active parent and fresh `NativeIsolationProgrammerCustody.resolve`; unpaid return may use a failed parent; terminal replay reads its committed receipt. Missing operation proof is unavailable, never reconstructed from a label, resource ID or source UUID.

Retain its actual **`receipt.command`** in the sealed parent issue proof. The same authority's later runtime factory resolves from canonical records and fresh state; it does not restore a live quote nonce or trust decoded permission fields alone. Frozen acceptance carrier/revision are historical facts; legitimate hull transfer requires valid current custody and a fresh work arm, not a changed job/serial. **Historical validation order:** (1) G3's primitive parent-proof validator checks accepted terms, native acceptance/issue provenance, gear-source references and any actual return/loss cutoff without calling `Commander.valid`, the provider validator or full parent settlement validation; (2) P01's historical codec consumes those primitives and validates its source, work and verification history; (3) the receiving/terminal validator joins both validated isolation receipts to the actual return/settlement. Genuine returned/completed sources must load with original openings retained; historical decoding must not require a currently held programmer. Measured endpoints cannot exceed the real cutoff's physical time; published work/opening/verification operations must precede its acknowledged sequence. Ongoing passive resource accounting does not become new tool work. Runtime work still requires the current issued original and active parent. This is a one-way validation dependency, not circular whole-Commander/provider/parent recursion.

## Return, settlement and failure

Show two separate actions at the actual Lerr/Avenor receiver: **Complete isolation / return original** when completion conditions hold, and **Return original unpaid / abandon job** otherwise. Each requires a current actual native dock, a fresh explicit review/Confirm and the exact canonical original/obligation. Nothing is accepted merely by opening a screen or docking.

Successful reception requires an active valid parent; two distinct **published P01 verification receipts**, one per original source role, each bound to this parent/authorization and programmer issue; genuine current source states without a contrary later terminal loss; and the same original programmer still in canonical issued custody. Source receipt substitution, partial work, opening without later observation, another source, Enron repairs, W04 installation, ordinary kills or a journal marker cannot qualify. Published valid P01 receipts remain valid across visits/loading; no extra repeat measurement is imposed solely because the player returned home.

The successful receiving dock must be the original Avenor station/principal, with a later native doorway receipt than acceptance and physical time no earlier than either P01 verification. Loading/born-berthing or replaying the acceptance dock cannot manufacture that return. No Renas docking stage or extra travel-receipt framework is added: actual Renas native module work and later actual Avenor arrival are the relevant physical evidence.

In one outer publication candidate, G3's return bridge calls existing **`settleScriptGear(..., release:false, ...)`** on the exact original, yielding returned status and removing only its inventory item/one tonne. It does not use `gear.release` or erase issue history. MC1 verifies that native return result, credits the frozen **9700 tenths** through the existing checked settlement path, records the once-only contract terminal/completion receipt and grants the existing zero-mass `isolation-work-family-trace` via its native first-success hook. The parent production clue pin must bind its actual digest and successful receiving cause; the provider child is never a second clue source. Writer refusal leaves tool, obligation, reward, terminal and clue all uncommitted. Repeating the exact successful receiving command or reloading returns its existing result without adding cash or another clue.

Unpaid return is allowed at the same actual issuer before completion, including the acceptance dock if the player has not launched, or after a genuine job failure. Return that same original, record **zero** reward and no clue, preserve all source balances/openings/losses, close the contract and release its active slot. A player may explicitly abandon away from the issuer: end the job unpaid and release its slot, but keep the still-issued programmer obligation and counted tonne until actual Lerr return. Do not free its cargo by marking a loan released. Missing/lost originals cannot be returned; existing issue/physical-loss authority owns that loss. Provider destruction, actual programmer loss or invalidated source authorization fail the job without refund, replacement, payment or clue. Pause, Back, closing a screen or an interrupted work arm is not abandonment.

**Known initial-stock limit:** P01 supplies one pair and PR73's lifetime original guard remains true after return/loss. This packet therefore cannot promise a second accepted attempt after terminal failure or completion. Keep the family and honest loss/return explanation discoverable. The broader shared requirement for availability after a failed job needs an actual newly funded source/original recovery policy before repeatability can be claimed; do not satisfy it by clearing history, changing parent UUID or cloning stock. That remains an explicit G11 limit, not a replacement-stock feature silently added here.
