# Ship design quality and remaining player experience requests

## Motivation

Catalogue coverage did not satisfy the requested design quality. The generated Alliance Challenger had only 52 vertices and 42 faces, with no intricate surface detail; many unrelated ships reused the same primary template. The requested family resemblance, coordinated palettes and smooth alien forms remained outstanding. The Liberator also needed three nacelles, a smooth two-hemisphere engine and a gold collector that was not hidden by intersecting grey hull faces.

The player's audit identified additional missing behaviour: reachable profit rankings, restored-game startup credits/title, compact chart separation and consistent controls/help. These requests need a single evidence-backed delivery rather than treating received instructions or an audit as completed implementation.

## Acceptance

Revise every existing project-authored hull with appropriate primary structures and real surface geometry, retain intentional clean/organic surfaces, preserve all source geometry/gameplay/reference profiles, and store actual final designs/materials in individual TOMLs. Add the purchasable Liberator and correct its engine/collector. Preserve source colour/texture/UV facts without pretending to render unbundled textures.

Integrate the audited startup, profit, chart and control fixes. Verify actual native browsing, exchange, flight, docking and saved progress; regenerated full catalogue/README media; coverage, sanitizers and leaks; protected-main publication, exact-main Sonar and a verified signed/notarized download. The separate scriptable mission-engine branch must retain its honest capability boundary.
