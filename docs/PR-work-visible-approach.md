# fix(flight): retain the visible approach to an oblique work surface

Acquiring a visible surface no longer automatically changes the approach to that face's geometric normal. Guidance projects the current ship-to-surface direction into the actual canonical target's local coordinates, verifies that its proposed stand-off ray still reaches the real mesh, and passes that immutable steering direction to the relative work controller. When no verified direction is supplied, existing normal-based steering remains available. Scoop guidance is unchanged. The optional scoop collection offset is checked using the same chosen approach direction and its corresponding facing basis.

The controller transforms this target-local direction through the actual moving pose each step and applies the existing target-velocity, angular-velocity and centripetal feed-forward. A zero, nonfinite or back-facing supplied direction refuses before requesting motion. The geometric `surfaceNormal` is retained on the target and in the sealed work surface; face authorization and actual cutter contact continue to use that normal. The direction is transient navigation intent, not a collision certificate, authority cache or persisted state.

The required gap remains `max(120, 2 * playerRadius)`, the work margin remains 1, and cutter range, acceleration, speed, pitch/yaw/roll rates and readiness thresholds remain unchanged. The exact triangle/dual-mesh validator, continuous envelopes, budgets, separate braking proof and accepted-endpoint integration are untouched. The correction avoids the SH127 corner-cutting trajectory rather than granting a brake or hold that its geometry refused. It is not a general obstacle path planner or a promise that every visible surface has a safe approach corridor.
