# feat(flight): add a pure persistent orbit formation controller

## Result and implementation

`OrbitFormationController.step(state:input:)` returns a next value state and ordered steering directives. Each live member has a stable logical UUID and fixed slot, independent of scene actor identity. Slot goals share one analytic circle in the saved planet's world basis, with exact tangent velocities. Polar radial, plane and angular-error correction guides capture around that circle instead of pursuing a leader's model offset or taking a chord toward its position. Caller-provided slot order can place any leader or special member last; the kernel contains no campaign, galaxy or ship-name rules.

The common angular speed reserves half the weakest hull's acceleration for centripetal demand, uses 75 percent speed/turn headroom, and slews with a quarter-acceleration tangential budget. A newly weak member receives an explicit blocked result while the common rate slows into its bounds. Steering requests bound acceleration and heading from actual current velocity/forward direction. Existing overspeed is braked gradually; the controller never clips live velocity or assigns an orbit pose. Desired circular goals and velocity are intentions, not collision authorization.

The immutable world anchor includes its identity, centre, right-handed orthonormal basis, planet radius, orbit radius and surface clearance. Every live hull must fit outside the planet. Pairwise ideal-slot chord separation includes the wraparound gap. A conservative requested-motion sphere sweep checks planet clearance over each physical step of at most 0.1 seconds; blocked results contain no steering. Actual fleet/obstacle collisions and committed movement still belong to the adapter. Finite input whose derived geometry or steering cannot be represented is refused.

Up to 64 remembered UUID/slot bindings belong to one saved incarnation. Removing a member leaves its slot and phase relationship intact; another ID cannot reuse that slot within the incarnation. Missing snapshots receive no command. An unused slot may admit a new logical member. Capture time accumulates only while not tracking and never resets within the incarnation, including after absence or reload; tracking pauses the consumed budget. Empty input freezes the state. A legitimate new provider incarnation gets a new state/identity rather than clearing old bindings or resetting progress. Saved slot counts are bounded before child decoding.
