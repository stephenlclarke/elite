# Resolve future EMP balance from the actual fitted energy grade

`EMPPulseProfile` validates existing equipment and the fitted energy component against the authoritative definition, then reads its saved canonical grade order. It does not migrate, promote, allocate IDs, modify Commander, authorize a shot or introduce save fields. Existing grade progression gives additive factors 1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5, with pulse strengths 32/48/64/80 electrical-energy units and ranges 3,000/4,500/6,000/7,500 native distance units.

The additive 50% power progression and increasing reach reflect the user's requests. Base power 32, the matching 50% range progression, splash radius one third of reach and linear distance falloff are explicit project balance defaults ratified during coordination. Cost 8, heat 18 and cooldown 1.5 seconds remain distinct from pulse strength; warm-up is 10 simulation seconds. Splash clips the attenuated pulse against the actual remaining reserve, returns zero at/outside the radius and rejects negative/nonfinite distance or reserve. Geometry and victim selection remain the future runtime producer's responsibility; this profile does not invent a contact or exclude player collateral.

Authored tests cover real fitted grants, detached hardware, legacy labels, existing explicit migration, typed save/reload, actual quoted component upgrades and canonical promotion while retaining the original physical item. They distinguish a subgrade capacity/recharge improvement from an earned grade change, reject forged/underpowered/nonfinite fitted components, and test exact falloff and reserve-clipping boundaries. These are source-level equipment/profile tests, not proof of paid service accounting, emitted pulses, charging, collateral, player disablement or recovery.
