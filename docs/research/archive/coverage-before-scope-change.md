## Research coverage and fidelity requirements

Updated 13 October 2026. This pass closes several named gaps with primary source inspection and complete factual lookup tables. It does not establish faithful replication of every mission on every platform: some executable profiles, server rules, assets and original-game outcome fixtures remain unavailable or unverified. Necessary project estimates are now authorized and explicitly labelled; recovered historical values remain separate. See [estimate policy](../project-estimates.md).

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | Core state machine and important numerical parameters established for an identified source profile; original assets, engine/AI behaviour, timing and binary parity remain separate work. |
| R2 | Sequence or major parameters established; required thresholds, dates, rewards, positions, branches or runtime outcomes remain unresolved. |
| R3 | Discovery inventory or design requirements; insufficient verified mechanics for faithful replication. |
| U | Reported unfinished historical material; do not claim a complete retail mission without executable reachability/outcome evidence. |

R1 means the documented core can be implemented; it does not mean the whole encounter has passed an original-game parity test. Counts of script records, log objectives, packages and narrative missions are different measures and must not be added together as a mission total.

## Required evidence for closing a gap

Record build/binary hash; start save; prerequisites; system/station IDs and coordinates; RNG seed; trigger event; objective order; enemy identity/spawns/AI; timer domain and boundary; success and failure; balances/equipment/reputation before and after; final save. Include successful and failed runs. A modern playthrough cannot prove a different historical port's behaviour.

Verify save/reload at every transition that consumes cargo, changes a planet/station, replaces a ship, carries passengers or advances a global story. Preserve source quirks separately from deliberate adaptations. For generated contracts, save the generated instance and validate its objective graph; do not give every random instance a new canonical mission identity.

## Completion criterion

For historical fidelity, every required field of a selected mission profile needs a source or measured original-game fixture. For the owner-authorized project adaptation, an individually labelled necessary estimate can instead fill a missing parameter; its source gap must remain visible. R2/R3/U entries must not be called faithful reproductions merely because a plausible implementation can be written. Exact replication of every edition and evolving online/community mission remains unverified; the backlog above names the specific evidence still needed.
