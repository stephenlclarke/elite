# Rescue Stations: portable solar laboratory

P30 scenario2b is ready as `PROJECT-P30-SOLAR-2B-v1`: check out one portable laboratory, deploy it in solar orbit, recover it after its reaction completes and return the same lab to the accepting orbital RRS station. This completes the third variant of [RRS family2](rescue-courier-mission-specifications.md). Apply [scope v4](implementation-scope.md), [crew/cargo/equipment custody](crew-and-passenger-specifications.md), [shipyard storage](player-shipyard-design.md) and the owner's [headquarters contract-board extension](rrs-contract-board-specifications.md). It adds no surface operation, on-foot deployment or Frontier mission.

## Processing and recovered heating rules

The distribution unconditionally sets reacted and calls reactionComplete at every sample after the first. The selected narrow repair removes only that unconditional pair and retains the subsequent source bands and existing guarded completion. When current normalized temperature<=previous and current<0.75, report too cold/stalled. When current>0.9, report thermal overload. Otherwise current>0.75 completes the reaction once, so the successful sampled band is strict0.75<temperature<=0.9. At exactly0.75 there is no completion; exactly0.9 is eligible. A lab that already reacted remains reacted while cooling or overheating; another sample cannot award another completion. No invented cumulative chemical dose, arbitrary processing quota or second timer is added.

Pinned O08 native raw minimum temperature is60, maximum256 and solar external constant1250. Let d be distance from sun centre, R its radius, lab insulation I=3 and E=1250*R*R/(d*d), multiplied100 for nova. With raw lab temperature T and simulation delta dt:

1. If E>I*100 and E>T, add `(E-T)*dt*0.00175/I` to T.
2. Otherwise, if T>60, add `(E-I*100-T)*dt*0.1/I`, then clamp T to at least60.
3. If T>256, subtract `dt*T` from hull energy. No energy regeneration repairs this25-energy instrument. Zero or negative energy destroys the actual lab and fails once.

JS temperature is T/256, not rawT. Source initial new lab temperature60 is0.234375 normalized; the source respawn setter0.65 means166.4raw, not0.65raw. The repaired profile never grants that warm reset or restores hull25 merely by revisiting, scooping or redeploying.

Use a deterministic1/60-second thermal substep and the original float constants as the selected project discretization. Persist any fractional remainder; process sampled5-second events in chronological order and split an accelerated elapsed interval at events/destruction. This fixed cadence is a necessary labelled project choice, not proof of the original variable-frame outcome. Validate finite positive d/R and actual system/star identity; the star's physical collision boundary remains effective rather than permitting an inside-sun heating exploit. At a stationary distance2R, starting raw60 and already aligned, the repaired band's first successful five-second sample is at1270seconds, T192.1295234750926, normalized0.7505059510745805, with hull25 remaining. This is a reproducible selected-profile example, not a universal fixed processing time.

The same deployed lab continues this deterministic thermal/sampling integration during elapsed simulation time when the player visits another system. That is an explicit persistent-world repair replacing the source's approximate respawn. Do not add real-world offline time just because a save is loaded. Save lab system/star/position, event/RNG and health; apply the same elapsed simulation history whether it is rendered or not. After pickup, the stowed instrument is protected from direct solar exposure and cools under the same selected cooling expression with E=0 as a labelled stowage adapter. It retains energy, reaction result and identity; stowage grants no repair, chemical reward or duplicate instrument. Redeployment uses that retained state.

## Return, deposit and failure

On-time success requires the paid lease, same living reacted lab aboard the player, correct accepting galaxy/system/station and arrivalTime<deadline. Headquarters-accepted work returns to that exact headquarters through the owner's adapter; a different same-system waystation does not count. Save arrival time before showing the debrief, so on-time docking is not made late by reading the screen. Confirm atomically returns the lab, consumes its pylon/clearance reservations, refunds the recorded80credit escrow, pays75, adds reputation4 and increments scenario2b completion once. Net earnings are75; the155credit terminal transfer includes the player's own80deposit. No free equipment/ammunition or new hull is a reward.

At or after the deadline mark the assignment overdue; do not destroy or magically transport a still-existing lab. Returning the actual intact paid-for lab late refunds only its80deposit, pays no75reward, applies reputation-10 and increments one failed counter. Returning an unprocessed lab late has the same deposit-only failure. Before the deadline, an early unprocessed return simply remains active unless the player explicitly abandons and returns it. Never reproduce the source's80credit late refund for no checkout, no paid receipt or a missing/destroyed instrument. Repeat handoff/receipt cannot refund twice.

Failure from destruction/loss, new-galaxy travel, original accepting station destruction or explicit abandonment pays no mission reward and applies reputation-10/failed counter once. Paid deposit is refunded only when the actual surviving instrument is simultaneously returned at the original station; otherwise it is forfeited. No checkout means no debit and no refund. The slot is then free for another eligible contract, while late callbacks from the old lease cannot alter it. Source ordinary same-galaxy system departure is not by itself a failure; do not copy courier2/2a departure rules into2b.

An actual surviving but unreturned failed lab retains RETURN_ONLY identity and condition; it is not deleted or made free personal equipment. If aboard, its pylon/intake custody still constrains fit until disposal. If left in space after terminal failure, release the active player's recovery reservations while preserving the world's retired lab; reacquiring it requires current pylon/intake fit and does not reactivate the mission. After forfeit, later return/disposal pays0 and changes no reputation. The original station handles that return-only cleanup; if destroyed, a surviving RRS orbital station, or an explicit orbital authority fallback when none is available, may dispose of it with zero reward/refund. This cleanup never reverses station-destruction failure or grants success elsewhere. Another lease cannot use its IDs or claim its escrow.

Stop contract-owned callbacks/markers/availability flags on terminal cleanup, retain immutable death/return/forfeit receipts and make a retired lab reject processing/mission callbacks. Its ordinary physical heat/destruction can continue without another reputation penalty. The source solarlabavailable flag must not survive into a different attempt or authorize unlimited mission gear purchases.
