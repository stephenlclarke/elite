# Aquatics: Kraken IV

O07 P02, Aquatics 2.31, contains one named mission: stop the hijacked AHC Kraken IV before it leaves Aqualina, Galaxy 3/system 99. The main source is Scripts/aquatics_populator.js, with target/police/equipment scripts, aquatics_krakenAI.plist and configuration files pinned in [package provenance](data/oolite-pack-sources.json). [Seven callback cases](data/secondary-oxp-source-fixtures.json) check its briefing transitions, player/NPC target destruction and player departure. Other Aquatics scripts supply ships, platforms, weapons and world traffic, not twenty-four separate missions.

## Offer and graph

Dock at the aquatics_HQ station in galaxyNumber 2/system 99. Score >511 qualifies. First contact sets IMMEDIATE_OFFER or UNAVAILABLE and records clock.days as aquatics_timer. Subsequent screen opportunity changes IMMEDIATE_OFFER to MAKE_OFFER. An initially unqualified commander upgrades UNAVAILABLE to MAKE_OFFER when docking with sufficient score. Negative Aquatics reputation causes expulsion and CASE_CLOSED.

Accept choice AQUATICS_1_ACCEPTED sets BRIEFING_1 and shows the first briefing. The next opportunity changes to BRIEFING_2 and shows the model briefing; the next changes BEGUN. First refusal opens a second chance; final refusal AQUATICS_3_REJECTED sets KICK_OUT, then reputation -10 and expulsion/CASE_CLOSED. Launching before briefings finish resets them to MAKE_OFFER. Back or interrupted briefing must not be treated as final acceptance in the project.

Launching from HQ in BEGUN spawns one Kraken if none exists. While BEGUN, **any** attributed or environmental destruction sets SUCCEEDED; player kill is not required for mission success. Kraken leaving or the player entering witchspace sets FAILED. Returning to HQ in either outcome ends CASE_CLOSED. Success sets reputation exactly 1; failure pays no completion reward. The target's dictionary bounty is 5,000 credits, awarded by the engine for a qualifying player kill, not again by the debrief. If police kill it, the mission can succeed without the player receiving that bounty.

## Encounter and escape

Kraken appears along the planet-to-witchpoint route at fraction 0.4+random*0.4. Add ceil(random*3)+1 police pursuers, each at fraction 0.05+random*0.9. Nominally this is two to four pursuers; random exactly zero yields one. Its AI heads for the witchpoint, desired range 5,000, updates every ten seconds and attempts hyperspace on arrival. It can divert into combat or flee to range 25,600. Therefore escape is a physical travel/AI outcome, not a fixed countdown.

The hijacked Hammerhead has energy 3,500, recharge 2.5, speed 100, thrust 100, roll 0.3, pitch 0.2, cargo 100 and four escorts. Its parent front weapon is absent/unarmed, but the combined ship has 34 subentities, including sixteen plasma turrets and sixteen laser mounts on its two carried platforms. Turret placements, orientations, energy 25 and range 7,500 remain in [expansion resources](data/oolite-expansion-resources.json); mount definitions supply their durability/weapons. Do not replace this with one harmless hull merely because its own front gun is blank. Escort and police role weights must use their actual dictionaries, including Barracuda/Manta variants, rather than assuming every pursuer has the same energy or missiles.

Every 4.75 seconds from spawn, Kraken scans radius 25,600 and deactivates active weapons having one of these exact roles: EQ_QC_MINE, EQ_RMB_CASCADE_MISSILE, EQ_RMB_LAW_MISSILE, EQ_RMB_OVERRIDE_MISSILE, energy-bomb, EQ_NUKE2_MISSILE, EQ_HARPOON_NUKE2_MISSILE, EQ_PHOTON_MISSILE, RANDOM_HITS_MINE. It assigns aquatics_deactivatedAI, changes display name and colours. Ordinary lasers/missiles are not blanket-disarmed. Keep this distinction in the project combat adapter.

Destruction drops ceil(random*6)-1 large fragments, ceil(random*16)-1 small fragments, ceil(random*30)+30 burning-fuel entities and a surviving fragment when a separate draw >0.33. The first two counts can be -1 at random zero, but spawn only if >0; fuel's exact lower endpoint is 30. Original wreck/fuel scripts govern secondary explosions; persist dangerous debris if saving an active encounter.

## Station adaptation, equipment and persistence

The HQ is already in space. Its original welcome and reward text also mentions surface shipyards, and PlanetFall integration enables a surface yard. Project mapping P02-STATION-1 keeps all briefing/debrief states at the orbital HQ and routes any advertised service to that station. Disable PlanetFall hooks and the aquatics_ASCYards surface role. No landing, planetary service dependency or new ground objective is needed. This follows the owner's [handoff rule](station-handoff-specifications.md), with original text retained only for reference.

The equipment reward is availability, not free equipment. With reputation 1, Galaxy 3/system 99 main-station offers include experimental Guardian Defense at 5,000 credits/TL index 14, jammer mine at 1,000/TL14 and Trident missile at 500/TL14. After strictly >50 days from aquatics_timer, a witchspace exit upgrades reputation 1 to 2, enabling general main-station versions at TL index 12 and the same prices. Reputation 0 also upgrades after strictly >150 days; absent/null and numeric zero need explicit initialization in the project, since the original equality can treat them alike. Negative reputation has a >0.5 arrival chance to rise by one.

Cloak purchase costs 50,000 credits, TL14, at the system main station with positive reputation, completed native cloak mission and aquatics_cloakRandom >0.75. That random value rerolls on startup/HQ docking in the source; persist its station-visit offer seed in the project. Purchase converts the wrapper item to EQ_CLOAKING_DEVICE and makes an otherwise TL99 cloak repairable at index 14. Guardian purchase initializes two drones; hostile red alert deploys the remaining count, leaving red alert/docking/jumping recalls survivors, and exhausted drones damage the controller. Store controller condition and drone identities/count across hull swaps rather than minting two new drones on every selection.

Persist mission/briefing stage, reputation, equipment timer, source offer seed, Kraken identity/condition/subentity damage, pursuers and escape route progress. Reconstruct the active encounter without duplicating it. Save timers by remaining duration and stop scans/debris handlers on removal. Verify score 511/512, both refusals, launch at each briefing, player/NPC/environmental kill, both escape paths, exactly-zero random draws, bounty once, day thresholds and all station handoff/storage transactions. Original native combat and equipment-event parity still require implementation tests.
