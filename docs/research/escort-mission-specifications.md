# Escort Contracts

O07 P10 is package 1.7.2; main/mother scripts still identify themselves as 1.7.1. The [maintained page](https://wiki.alioth.net/index.php/Escort_Contracts_OXP) lists 1.7.2 as a PNG colour-profile update. The cached package was already 1.7.2; an older script banner does not establish an older download. Its exact scripts, seven AIs, equipment and ship dictionaries are hashed in [package provenance](data/oolite-pack-sources.json). [Thirty source cases](data/escort-source-fixtures.json) check generation gates, deadlines, aegis outcomes, payment, friendly fire and synchronized flight using O08-based script-object scoping and mocked APIs. Native flight/AI parity is separate. This is a reusable space escort generator, not thirty different missions.

## License and offers

EQ_ESCORTCONTRACTS costs 500 credits, has damage_probability 0 and transfers between ships. Equipment data specifies TL index 4 and requires a clean legal record. Its condition script permits purchase in government >2, or any scripted award context. The wiki's lower tech description differs; use the inspected equipment profile with the O08 TL reader, not a merged threshold.

At the main station, require the working license, bounty exactly zero, no current contract and ec_runcontractsetup. Offer generation passes when random <0.4*(1+reputation/20). Reputation is bounded -10..20, so the endpoint probabilities are 0.2 and 0.8, with 0.4 at neutral. The source supports configurable old-style selection, but its current default is new-style: systems within seven light years, government <=5, not post-nova; independently accept each with probability 0.5*(1-government/10). Old-style instead requires government lower than the origin and uses probability 0.5. An empty target set produces no interface.

For each target, freeze its name/government/economy/tech, a random trader name, base price 75+75*(7-government), contract pay round(basePrice*(0.51+random+reputation/20)), and kill bonus round(40*(0.51+random+reputation/20)). Use separate random draws for the last two values and positive JavaScript Math.round semantics. Reputation is escort-specific, not Oolite passenger reputation. Never recalculate accepted pay after reputation changes.

Viewing offers or a chart does not accept. The default selection is a navigation choice. Acceptance stores target/pay/bonus/name, resets kills/payment, sets active true and departure expiry clock.minutes+180. Returning from declined screens retains offers for 60+round(random*660) game minutes; only a later GUI change with time strictly greater than that threshold clears them in the source. Project repair: persist the generated offer set and expiry per station visit, and evaluate expiry directly instead of depending on a GUI event or rerolling through reloads.

## Departure, transit and escort

Launch must occur strictly before departure expiry. Spawn one ec_mother within radius 4,000 of mainStation.position-mainStation.forward*14,000. Source also fails departure if an ec_mother already exists; project repair: identify the mission's own mother rather than count unrelated role users. Late departure costs one reputation, floor -10, and ends the contract.

Mother waits, initially checking ten seconds after launch and every thirty seconds thereafter. Player distance <=9,000 starts jump preparation. Distance greater than four mother scanner ranges also permits departure without the player; intervening distances prompt approach. Mother moves toward mainStation.position-mainStation.forward*36,000, uses fuel 7 and attempts the chosen jump every twenty seconds until unblocked. Its successful jump starts a follow timer 50+motherMass/4000 seconds. The player may follow its wormhole or jump independently to the same system. Arrival at a wrong system fails and costs one reputation. A missing mother at the correct destination does not fail immediately, allowing differing wormhole arrival times; docking before success still fails.

Use the source's core mother role entries for Anaconda, Boa, Python and Boa Cruiser, with their dictionary weights 1,1,0.5,1. The separate python-trader entry has weight 1 but requires its named parent. Optional retexture/add-on entries only participate when their parents/models exist. All mothers override front/aft beam lasers, ECM and injectors, no built-in escorts, M beacon, rock scan class and yellow/magenta scanner colours. Their original core energy/speed/cargo/missiles are inherited, not identical across hulls. [Expansion resources](data/oolite-expansion-resources.json) preserves all own/inherited values and unresolved optional parents. Default project profile excludes missing-parent variants rather than guessing their mission durability.

Cruise speed is min(mother.maxSpeed,0.95*player.maxSpeed); injector speed is min(mother.maxSpeed*7,player.maxSpeed*6.65). These caps apply to travel rather than combat. Destination AI cruises toward the main-station beacon, desired range 25,000, with course/aegis checks every ten seconds. Attack states fight or flee, flee distance 25,600, respond to missiles after a random 0.25..0.75 seconds, and resume the travel AI after the threat is lost. Native fightOrFleeHostiles remains an engine policy; the project may bind its existing trader combat policy explicitly, without claiming original AI parity.

## Synchronized drive, separation and friendly fire

Synchronized travel checks once per second, removes the previous velocity callback, and requires both ships free of applicable mass lock, mother not injecting, player distance >500 and <=2,500, heading dot product >0.98. The mother gains velocity heading*mother.maxSpeed*32 and a frame callback matches the player velocity. At 500 exactly or heading dot 0.98 exactly synchronization fails. Reset both to their normal thrust vectors when it stops. Mother being CLASS_ROCK prevents her from mass-locking the player; do not remove collision detection or allow either ship through obstacles.

If the player is beyond mother scanner range, each travel check rolls random*200 <14-2*government; if no pirate is already within that scanner range, spawn 1+round(random*2) pirates within radius 1,000 of mother. Thus governments 0 and 7 have nominal per-check probabilities 7% and 0%; group sizes 1/2/3 have 25%/50%/25% weights. Ordinary ambient traffic/combat still operates. Save encounter seeds and check cadence to prevent reload farming.

While mass-locked and player nearby with working injectors and fuel >1 LY, mother starts injectors if 1+random*4 < frustration. Otherwise frustration increments. On starting, refill mother's fuel to 7, reset frustration/counter, and set max iterations round(scannerRange*2.5/(maxSpeed*7)). Stop when both ships cease being mass-locked or the iteration counter is strictly greater than that limit, checking every second. Hostiles interrupt travel/injectors to defend the player or mother.

Player damage accumulates across hits, including scrapes. The first hit warns. Once a subsequent check sees total damage >50% of maxEnergy, issue the second warning. A later check after >75% turns mother hostile, ends the contract and costs two reputation, floor -10. Equality alone does not cross either threshold. A single large first hit does not immediately trigger all warnings because the source's state changes occur in order. Hired Guns escorts are treated as friendly by the intercept AI until the mother's player-warning state reaches five. Mother calls for help at energy <67%, then <33%, and resets those notices on full recharge.

## Success, failure, payment and saving

At destination aegis, active true becomes success only if neither mother nor player has hostiles. Otherwise keep escorting until mother actually docks. The original shipWillDock callback sets success without checking station/system; explicit project repair restricts it to the intended destination's main station. Docking the player while still active in the destination fails for -2 reputation; docking elsewhere fails for -1. Mother destruction while active costs -2. Source death after success deletes the claim without payment or a further reputation change; preserve the selected project's decision explicitly rather than paying twice.

Docking the player at the target main station after success pays contractPay+kills*killBonus, increments reputation by one up to 20, clears the obligation and removes any remaining mother. Credit only player shipTargetDestroyed events for a target with positive bounty, not rock/cargo, while mother is valid and player distance <3*mother.scannerRange. Three ranges is the actual rule despite an older two-range comment. Source truthiness also permits kills during pending success; project profile freezes kills at success to prevent extra farming and labels that repair.

With no active contract, each witchspace exit has probability 0.1 of reputation = round(reputation*0.8), gradually returning positive/negative reputation toward zero. Preserve JS rounding for negative halves. If a successful claim is carried away to a system without mother, the source deletes it; keep the source outcome or choose a clearly labelled project persistence repair.

Save active/success status, starting/target system, mother identity/hull/condition, follow/departure deadlines, reputation, generated pay/bonus, kills, reward-paid ID, AI travel/combat phase, frustration, warning damage and synchronization state. Original save variables cover only part of this; reconstructing a new full-health mother on every reload is not faithful. Stop velocity callbacks and timers on failure/death/docking/unload. Hull changes cannot duplicate the portable license or reset accepted encounter progress.

Verify all strict threshold boundaries above, empty/no-route/post-nova offers, missing optional ships, early/late/wrong-system arrival, mother death before/after success, two payment attempts, valid/invalid bonus kills, hostile aegis, both docking orders, save/reload in each travel phase, slower/faster player ships and blocked synchronized flight. Source cases cover selected callback boundaries; full native AI and combat outcomes still require implementation tests.
