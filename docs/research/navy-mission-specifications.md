At Navy SecCom Station, reserve enlistment requires score>=128; lower rating bands show rejection text. Accept sets STATUS_ENLISTED. No Academy completion flag is actually checked, despite briefing flavour. Initial player sortie count0 and OFFER_OPEN; reserve participation score0. Enlisted service remains voluntary. Opening reserve duty when OFFER_OPEN creates a job, starts clock.seconds, chooses destination/type and marks it; Back does not reroll an already assigned job. Destination selection begins uniformly within direct30LY, tries a route distance<=40LY, then scans candidates in source order if invalid. Project repair uses a nonempty reachable filtered list, permits current system only if it is explicitly selected and valid, and offers nothing when no candidate qualifies.

A source job expires only at witchspace arrival when elapsed>1209600seconds (14days), not at equality; docked expiry is not continuously polled. Project checks this same deadline before encounter activation, handoff or completion so docking cannot evade it. Galaxy departure cancels accepted duty and transfers enlisted status. There is no ordinary mission cash salary; retained ship bounties and accumulated sortie bonuses are the rewards.

## Participation and battle completion

Two independent conditions govern a sortie. Personal contribution reaches3 points from qualifying mission targets: ordinary Thargoid1/cruiser2/battleship3; pirate variants mostly1, Python Blackdog2 and pirate BoaII2. The exact score is script_info.score per resource; e.g a BoaII plus one1-point fighter suffices. Killer attribution accepts player, player-owned weapon, aquatics_guardianPlayer, listed anti-Thargoid/combat/kamikaze drone, CT_thargon and the two hired-gun roles. The project requires actual commander ownership for hired ships/drones rather than accepting any unrelated entity sharing a role. One personal-threshold crossing increments player_sortie once.

Separately, every mission target death decreases a battle counter. Source initial counters are20/5/20/8 for Invasion/Invasion2/Intercept/Police despite actual random roster sizes. The first three battle-over conditions are counter<8; Police requires counter<5 AND countShipsWithRole(GN_sortie_target)==0. Thus Invasion2 can declare battle over after its first death. Preserve this documented source defect; the repaired project initializes the counter from the actual unique hostile roster and uses the same <8/<5 thresholds with Police requiring all its marked targets gone, including the hideout. Never decrement for unrelated ambient ships or components.

State meanings are OFFER_ACCEPTED (neither), OFFER_COMPLETED (personal quota only), OBJECTIVES_COMPLETE (battle ended only) and MISSION_ACCOMPLISHED (both). Either threshold may happen first; reaching3 personal points after battle-over still grants the single sortie. Source cleanup on docking/jumping any of the latter three states clears markers/job data, so earning personal quota permits departure before all enemies are dead; battle-only completion gives no personal sortie. Leaving a still OFFER_ACCEPTED mission before either condition does not abandon it, but revisits may respawn/reset its whole roster. Project repair persists the encounter/death set across visits and refuses duplication. Freeze the earned participation award and record why the job was cleared.

At a SecCom visit, bonuses are one-time at every ten sorties:10 pays1000credits;20/30/40 pay5000;50and higher multiples of10 except100multiples pay10000; every100multiple pays25000. The source pays only if the count is exactly a multiple of10 at that visit; project repair retains a milestone ledger so missed visits do not lose rewards or recreate deleted old bonus flags. Ordinary rescued Navy officers pay random rank insurance100/250/500/750/1000/2500 with source bucket counts1/1/2/2/2/2 out of10. A random draw exactly0 gives ceil(random*10)==0 and undefined payout; project repair selects1+floor(random*10). The officer script itself is marked untested in the distribution: use explicit unique pod custody and station unloading, with one cargo unit while carried and no repeated rescue payment.

## P14 Admiral Kurtz: complete space sequence

Offer at a Galaxy6 SecCom with seccom_station DOING_BUSINESS, after at least ten sorties and no prior Kurtz state. Declining sets HOFD_WAIT and future qualifying visits have10% reoffer chance. Accept advances HOFD_IN_ROUTE, pays1500credits immediately, marks system214 and directs the commander there. No mission deadline is defined.

While en route, arrivals at Galaxy6 systems3/84/236/111 spawn five hofd-headhunters on the witchpoint-planet route. The Headhunter is a Krait-form target: speed300, energy250, recharge2.5, pitch1.5/roll2.75, thrust35, beam forward, zero missiles, fuel70sourceunits, injectors, shield booster chance0.5/enhancer0.25. This is a mission loadout, not the owner's eventual player-refit baseline.

At system214 spawn Behemoth-Nellie at psu[0,0,0.15], three picket-frigates, five picket-transports, two minesweepers and one picket-Atlas within5000. Nellie spawning changes IN_ROUTE to ARRIVED. Docking on Nellie presents Kurtz's choice: accept service→HOFD_SERVICE and bounty+100; refuse→HOFD_MEETING_OVER. A subsequent mission opportunity launches the player. Source launch respawns Nellie at wpu0.2 after this meeting while arrival uses psu0.15; project keeps the instance's actual persisted location rather than recreating two capitals. Service acceptance does not have a separate implemented payment/objective chain. The player can still capture Kurtz; his crew is hostile through the source group/legality AI.

Nellie's first ENERGY_LOW event launches one hofd-shuttle, deletes that event and its direct ship-death outcome, and sets KURTZ_ESCAPED on launch. Killing Nellie before shuttle launch sets KURTZ_KILLED by any killer. The shuttle has speed150, energy300, thrust12, pitch1/roll2, military forward laser, no missiles and a Kurtz escape pod. Shuttle abandon/death launches that pod once; the shipDied backup must not duplicate it. Player scoop sets KURTZ_CAPTURED and clears bounty immediately; pod death by any cause sets KURTZ_KILLED; another scooper leaves KURTZ_ESCAPED. These are mutually exclusive outcomes bound to the same character identity.

**Station handoff mapping P14-KURTZ-ESCAPE:** original shuttle AI flies to/lands on the planet. Replace that endpoint with the system214 main orbital station. It flies there at its source maximum speed, checks navigation every10seconds, and docking hands Kurtz into station custody, closing the escaped branch. No surface landing or player ground chase is included. Intercepting the shuttle, recovering the unique pod and taking him to a SecCom remain space gameplay. If no main orbital exists, apply the mission-station fallback contract. Captured passengers/pods cannot be stored as personal spare equipment; reserve capacity or block a ship change.

Return captured Kurtz to any Galaxy6 SecCom while DOING_BUSINESS: pay25000credits once, clear bounty, clear instructions and set HOFD_PREP_UPDATE. One witchspace arrival sets HOFD_UPDATE; next Galaxy6 main-station docking shows captured news and terminates. Killed/escaped outcomes change to PREP_UPDATE2/3 at witchspace arrival, then their Galaxy6 main-station news terminates with no25000payment. Source comment suggests any-galaxy news, but the actual mission-screen branch checks galaxyNumber5; retain this distinction and use a labelled project repair to allow pending news at any main orbital station so galaxy travel cannot strand completion. Clear all214markers on every terminal outcome. Offer/meeting Back reopens the pending choice without cash/state changes instead of leaving HOFD_OFFER/MEETING softlocked.
