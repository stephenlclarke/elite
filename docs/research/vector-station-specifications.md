### Trade, quantity and price rules

Accept at stage1 registers illegal Dephrac Crystals: buy at Intisoar180 main orbital, sell at Qubiisis76 exact Specialist station; source galaxy list[5], priceclass60, offset1.5, refresh0.4, decrease0.1, initial available0 and times=currentDays-10, oneShot true. The source treats these as non-contract special goods, displays grams and gives no fixed delivery quota/deadline. Any positive quantity sold calls the story callback. The callback ignores amount even for zero/negative input in direct invocation; project validates strictly positive integer grams, ownership and station/galaxy before advancing3. One gram is the minimal legitimate delivery, not an invented 10-tonne quota. No source acceptance fee.

CCL uses a separately saved market random value clamped0.6..0.81. Let d=clamp(currentDays-times,0,1000), k=ln(1+0.1*d), p=0.4*d, pTotal=p/(k>1?1/k:1), and `u = d ? ((p?p:0.1)/(pTotal?pTotal:1)*(d-k)/(k>1?k:1))/1.5/ln(18)*0.4 :0` for this source available0 profile. Available grams=floor(u), subject to the saved blend cooldown. The raw availability u can decrease over part of this interval even when the floored stock remains unchanged; do not silently replace it with constant weekly tonnes.

Buy quote = one-decimal rounding of `((60-sqrt(60*rnd))/(ln(u+3)*rnd)/(k>1?k:1)/0.1*(1/0.4))/sqrt(1.5)`. Source's `Math.random()>0,5 ?` comma expression always takes this minus branch; it is not an actual50/50 price sign. Sell quote = one-decimal rounding of buyQuote*(1.5-0.1*rnd), recomputed from local/current market state. Examples from executed source at rnd0.6: d0 stock0 buy1672.2/sell2408.0; d10 stock0 buy1360.5/sell1959.1; d15 stock1 buy1259.6/sell1813.8; d100 stock1 buy504.5/sell726.5; d1000 stock4 buy200.1/sell288.1 credits per gram. Prices are source reader results, not historically verified retail/server prices.

Buying creates a blend cooldown currentDays+ceil(ln60)=+5days. Source availability is time-derived rather than decremented from a physical manifest stock; retain the saved quote/stock/cooldown for each completed purchase and do not reroll during Back/load. A quote is valid for its transaction snapshot; any changed day/stock/wallet revalidates before confirmation. Reject non-finite, fractional, zero or negative quantities, insufficient stock or cash. Source keyboard has negative/NaN and whole-hold checks not present in the buy-all path; those are defects, not alternative legitimate game rules.

Source checks grams numerically against cargoSpaceAvailable (tonnes) and stores quantities outside the ordinary manifest without a lasting space reservation. Necessary physical-fit adaptation: reserve `ceil(totalCrystalGrams/1000000)` tonnes, minimum1 when positive, in one hazardous-cargo locker; native units remain grams and mission prices remain per gram. This is a labelled common-engine integer-tonne packaging reservation, not proof one gram masses one tonne. Ordinary cargo, fitted cabins/equipment and other mission reservations are subtracted first. No passenger berth or free secret hold is awarded, no extra equipment prerequisite is invented, and no whole-hold seal is required because source block=false. A hull swap must carry that real reserved locker or remain blocked.

The first valid sale advances3 and records a once-only story delivery receipt. Partial sales keep remaining grams/lot identities and an endpoint service until the balance is sold or explicitly surrendered; do not let source clearing state.trade orphan remaining cargo on load. Source oneShot removes the trade item on sell-all or after a quantity sale reaches zero. The project removes procurement after first story delivery and allows lawful settlement of existing remainder at the recorded buyer; later sales cannot regress the story from4/6 to3 or replay its bonus. Sell only existing unique grams, transfer custody and money atomically, preserve any other cargo. The source callback itself grants no fixed cash; it is the market sale that pays quantity*sellQuote.
