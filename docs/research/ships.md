# Deduplicated ship and vessel catalogue

Each row is one canonical model/name. Aliases and appearances are attached to it rather than counted again. Distinct marks, Bushmaster designs and prototype hulls remain separate. A shared name across centuries of the setting does not establish identical geometry, equipment or flight physics.

Version identifiers are in [versions](versions.md), and evidence IDs in [sources](sources.md). OOLITE-OXP means optional expansion content; it does not establish base-game presence. BBC-PROTOTYPE is unpublished BBC Elite II material, not Frontier: Elite II. ED-ANNOUNCED is not a confirmed playable release. ARC-GOLD follows ARC's game rather than a new hull inventory. Unlisted editions are unknown unless a cited comparison explicitly establishes absence.

S32 establishes exact 6502 blueprint families. S01 overrides the generic rock-hermit category because the BBC disc does not encounter rock hermits. S19 supplies supplementary cross-platform mappings and contains inconsistencies: its Asp Mk II claim for Frontier is not adopted; the separate Asp Explorer is retained. Modern ED names are mapped from D06, with Live and Legacy kept distinct.

## Aliases and identity decisions

- Commies Minnow/escapee/RRS miner share SH168. Police Ray/Thought Police/Convict Miner Ray/Sunray/RRS guard share SH169 and one selected mesh, with separate fitting/stat profiles. Military Stingray/Ray Mk II has its own mesh and SH170; neither is an Aquatics Manta Ray or Commies Giant Ray carrier. Thirteen dictionaries are three hulls.
- Cobra Mk 3 and Cobra Mk III use the latter spelling; the same applies to other Arabic/Roman numeral variants.
- Moray, Moray Starboat and Moray Star Boat are one model name.
- Panther/Puma Clipper and Tiger Trader retain their full names.
- Sidewinder Mk I is grouped with Sidewinder by name; historical and ED models still need separate capability profiles.
- Eagle Long Range Fighter Mk II and Eagle Mk II are one catalogue name; Imperial Eagle remains distinct.
- Griffin is also called Griffin Mk II in a legacy cross-reference; do not confuse it with unpublished prototype geometry.
- Thargoid warship is a generic family across classic Elite and FFE. Its individual designs must not be merged into a single mesh or player capability record.
- A pirate Cobra/Python is a role variant, not an extra hull. Role variants can have different bounty, missile and cargo statistics.
- Stations and rock hermits are included as encounter objects but are not counted as ordinary spacecraft. Commodity containers, missiles, asteroids and debris are excluded from hull totals.

## Named mission vessels and specialised classes

Argent's Quest is the named Turner-class reward vessel. Turner's Quest is Mic Turner's wreck in FFE's plot, not automatically an additional ordinary purchasable model. Military defence satellites and the INRA Command Ship are mission objects; the latter is associated with an unfinished branch and a modified Imperial Explorer. Fleet carriers, megaships and generation ships are known categories, but a complete class/individual-name inventory was not established. Their omission from the model table is not a claim that they do not exist. [S14, S17, S21, D06]

Deposed's Bien Interceptor is a Sidewinder variant, its Peldorian Asp is an Asp variant, and its named Tarantula uses the Boa Mk II template. Three Black Widow targets share one hull identity; the factory is a station object. Their inherited and custom numerical fields are in the expansion/combat supplements. Love Cats hunters/Romeo are Asp Mk II variants; Taranis and Military Fiasco use distinct Wolf Mk II profiles. Ionics Link/RLF vessels share the Huntsman model, and its leader/fighter roles share Redback/Whitetail models. Military Fiasco pirate/defender versions share their underlying hulls; detachable teeth/spikes, turrets and carrier bridge/dock are subentities, not duplicate whole ships. Its Basilisk/Hydra human ships are unrelated to Dangerous Thargoid Interceptors of those names. P41's satellite is an artificial object and its rotating dish is a component, not another race. P18 Link Base, P38 Taranis HQ and P42 Erehwon station are mission encounter objects described by their package resource keys.

Torjan Mino's Mamba and Maynard Zantor's Cobra Mk I are named Oric instances of existing models. P23 Qubeenian rebels and P33 Baron minions are Krait derivatives; P35's spy vessel is an Imperial Courier mission variant. P36's Stealth Barracuda has its own model and statistics. Their mission parameters are in the port/expansion supplements, with package hashes in O07. These names do not create duplicate general hull rows.

## Numerical specifications and future refits

The [capability supplement](ship-specifications.md) now supplies pinned Oolite core/player/equipment definitions, fifteen Elite-A player profiles in both source orders, 38 GBA resource records and numerical model envelopes. The [project adaptation dataset](data/player-ship-adaptation.json) keeps the requested equipment minimum and estimated player cargo separate from original NPC data. The [shipyard design](player-shipyard-design.md) defines linked three-axis comparison rotation and transfer/storage. The canonical model list itself remains an identity catalogue; these numerical profiles do not establish every edition or every optional hull.

## Appearance and geometry gaps

ZX48/ZX128 tape sides, CPC, PC-CGA/PC-PLUS/PC98, EINSTEIN, PLUS4, TNK/GBA and ORIC1337 have not all been mapped model-by-model. Do not expand a BBC-family or Spectrum-family claim to those editions. Oolite has a much larger optional ship ecosystem than the historical cross-reference. Dragon appears in the legacy index without an established version and is retained here as unresolved rather than a confirmed hull. Mirage II and Dart need proof beyond manual/story references.

The [Z80 resource tables](z80-port-profiles.md) establish all MSX disk object-header fields and the identified Spectrum tape hull headers. The [Oric resource table](data/oric-ship-resources.json) and [Oolite combat table](data/oolite-combat-resources.json) supply further raw encounter fields and inheritance limits.

For faithful mission combat, resolve each target's hull, shields, speed, rotation, laser damage, missile count, ECM, cloak, spawn position, tactics, bounty and cargo drop from the selected edition. S32 provides official 6502 numerical comparisons and blueprint links; the name catalogue alone is not a ship simulation specification.

Vector/Arn/player SE share vector.dat and one canonical hull SH165; NPC, mission and player statistics remain separate variants. The native SE player refit is now filled in the adaptation dataset, with measured base hull, explicit O08 defaults and labelled equipment capacity. Other expansion hull identities remain outstanding.

Arafura general/mission/independent/player definitions share one hull SH166. Inspection craft SH167 has its own model and is separate from Vector SE. The RRS EVA probe is a pylon payload, not an invented crewed ship. Their crew/capacity limits and necessary conversion choices are in the [dedicated profiles](rescue-inspector-ship-specifications.md).
