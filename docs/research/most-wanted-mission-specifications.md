# GalCop's Most Wanted: persistent bounty hunts

P16 version0.16 is a repeatable space bounty-tracking generator, not a fixed narrative campaign or306 unique ships. Its [author page](https://wiki.alioth.net/index.php/GalCop%27s_Most_Wanted) calls it experimental. The package's306 shipdata records are escort-suppressing/core-hull wrappers in [legacy resources](data/legacy-expansion-resources.json); do not add them to the canonical hull count. Source member hashes are in [legacy provenance](data/legacy-source-recovery.json).

Required Bounty System minimum0.4.4 is named in the manifest. The currently published [Bounty System0.19](https://wiki.alioth.net/index.php/Bounty_System) has been separately recovered in [dependency provenance and selected contracts](data/most-wanted-dependency-source.json). This project profile explicitly pairs P16 0.16 with that0.19 dependency; it does not claim historical minimum-version parity. GNN supplies names and incidental phrasing, not objectives, fleets or payments; use independently written project text. Optional Station Dock Control and Smugglers are replaced by the explicit dock ledger below, so neither is an implementation dependency.

[31 selected source checks](data/most-wanted-source-fixtures.json) cover all11 generated archetypes, all eight galaxy lane/milk-run tables, licence boundaries, hidden-bounty consumption, escort attrition and observed defects. The JSON also preserves44 complete lane arrays and242 milk-run pairs across eight galaxies, plus factual generated records for each archetype. Those deterministic sample records are examples, not replacement constants for the generator.

## Objective, tracking and success contract

An orbital GalCop security office/kiosk displays the wanted list. There is no mandatory accept/decline contract, fixed kill total, mission score gate or active deadline. The source mission list is a watchlist of target IDs; selecting a target tracks its whereabouts without creating a second target. Project exposes one repeatable family and one distinct gallery instance per watched target. Milestones: watch target, locate/intercept its persistent ship, optional warrant scan to reveal hidden ordinary bounty, destroy the unique target with credited player attribution, claim the separate bonus at orbital GalCop HQ. Do not make optional scanning mandatory for the bonus: this is a declared project repair policy because the recovered completion hook is absent, while the author describes a bonus for each listed pilot removed.

The source worldscript removes a target from its lists on death by any killer and on escape-pod launch. Neither P16 nor the inspected Bounty System0.19 scripts append a completed target to _completed, although the dependency reads/claims that array. Thus the bonus screen alone does not demonstrate a working success event. Project repair snapshots the target record before removal and creates exactly one bonus receipt only on a unique qualifying player kill. NPC/environment destruction removes the target and marks the watched job Unavailable/Failed without a player receipt. A duplicate death, despawn, wrapper replacement, escort death or hull swap cannot complete the main target again.

An escape-pod launch is not the pilot's death. Preserve pilot identity and transfer outstanding ordinary bounty to the pod/custody ledger, clear it from the abandoned hull and suppress any second hull payout. If the pilot escapes or a third party takes the pod, resolve the watchlist as Escaped/Unavailable. If the player delivers the live escapee to an orbital GalCop station, settle the unique ordinary warrant once; no additional Most Wanted bonus is invented for this capture route, since the published mission's bonus describes destruction. This is an explicit anti-double-payment/custody repair; the original worldscript simply drops the target on ejection. All transfers use the station contract and saved pilot ID.

## Population and eleven archetypes

Initialization makes floor(30*r)+10 attempts, so10-39, not an assured count; unavailable roles/routes can skip attempts. Each day add one attempt with probability10% if the list has fewer than30, and remove a random non-live target with probability4% if more than10. Daily limits do not retroactively cap a larger initial list. Generate a saved seeded world roster, not fresh target copies per dock/reload.

Choose r: <=0.3 trader branch, (0.3,0.7] pirate branch, (0.7,0.95] assassin branch, >0.95 hunter branch. Trader: a separate r>0.5 enters tier2, then r>0.8 tier3; otherwise a separate r>0.7 selects courier11. Pirate: r>0.5 tier5, then r>=0.7 tier6, then r>0.9 tier7. Assassin r>0.7 selects9 instead of8. ## Routes, schedules and visible intelligence

Use the eight pinned lane/milk-run tables. Exclude nova milk-run endpoints. Safe/dangerous spokes connect a non-nova government>=5 hub to government<=2 neighbours within7LY. Pirates start at the hub, assassins/hunters at the first dangerous neighbour. Couriers select opposite corners in Oolite map coordinates: x<31/y<16 versus x>71/y>36, and x<31/y>36 versus x>71/y<16. Attempt at most five reachable endpoint pairs, with >=2-node custom paths. Do not apply these cutoffs to unscaled byte coordinates. Unreachable G7 components are legitimate, not permission to teleport.

Source route time is the pinned O08 sum of squared per-hop LY distances, in hours; jumps minimize 1792 per hop plus distance, time routes minimize squared-distance sum. Each hop respects7LY. Reference: [Universe routeFromSystem](https://github.com/OoliteProject/oolite/blob/574ca848c91a067178416216523e8d09c1e9282a/src/Core/Universe.m#L7762). Initial dockTime=now-(3600+21600*r), departureTime=now+(3600+21600*r). Later dockTime=previousDeparture+routeHours*3600+1800+U(1800); next departure=dockTime+21600+21600*r. Milk runs alternate endpoints; lanes follow the next node and reverse at ends; spoke routes alternate hub/random spoke. At a spoke endpoint r>0.98 attempts a new hub no farther than30LY by custom path, then resumes spoke movement. Courier custom paths choose a new opposite-corner destination at the end. Save the route's node ordinal because some lane arrays repeat system IDs.

Source type1 computes a temporary swapped endpoint but never assigns it back to wanted.system: repair the alternate-endpoint transition. Source type3 searches with dest==null OR attempts<5 and can loop indefinitely; repair to dest==null AND attempts<5 and fall back to the existing hub route. Guard every null route, missing hub and empty candidate list. Source type99 removes invalid records: project instead marks unavailable, retains watched history and never pays. Bound large-time catch-up work and resume from saved schedules without changing the seeded itinerary.

Source updates visible sightings only when r>updateChance, retaining three previous reports. Initial report timestamp=now-14days*r+1day*r. The truth ledger is separate from the stale public list. Constant updates default true; when disabled display refresh is3 days. Main-station sightings publish destination/departure; hide those premium fields without a current hunter licence. Source pending windows are arrival in [-1.5h,+1.5h), or arrival more than4h ago treated as docked, with arrival+8h pending expiry. A witchpoint target only materializes within3*player scanner range, at a random direction2000-5000m from the origin, reflecting risky negative-z directions to avoid buoy collision.

The source random spawn test uses absolute time difference/3600 and chance increasing0.05 per repopulation (~20 seconds), paradoxically giving zero arrival chance at the exact scheduled instant. No-SDC docking also references undefined chance instead of waitperiod, while its comment disagrees with the computed delay. Project repair uses deterministic scheduled dock/departure/arrival events, one serialized actor per target/escort, orbital station queues and exact saved timestamps. With no explicit timetable left after a live docking, set the next departure to now+3600+21600*r (same source initial dwell distribution, explicitly borrowed repair). Never duplicate a docked/witchpoint actor through overlapping pending windows; late arrivals resume their real route phase. Station choice: assassins main, pirates80% first rock hermit, traders/hunters rock hermit if r>0.5-(7-government)/7; fall back to main when no hermit exists.

Tips have type spotted, incoming or docked, a named source and five-day age expiry. Generate10-29 tipsters with source reliability70-100; the source compares0-1 draws directly against70-100, making them always accurate. Project normalizes reliability/100, preserving the intended misleading-tip branch. False sightings select a system within7LY or alternate station/incoming detail. Limit three current tips per target/type, including ordinary sightings (source only bounds some branches), and do not display knowledge the public/tip ledger has not revealed. Optional dockmaster flags at most3 targets and charges floor(0.1*realBounty) per service; keep as an orbital dockside contact, no station interiors. Wait menu advances1-12 hours, then processes schedules/licence expiry; it is a meaningful world-clock action, unlike pausing the gallery.

## Warrant scanner, licence and rewards

Borrowed Bounty System0.19 warrant scanner costs2570CR, source tech index6, equipment space/weight1 tonne,10-second scan at0.6*scannerRange (15360m for25600 scanner); police8 seconds/0.8. Target must remain valid, piloted, nonstation, non-Thargoid and not an escape-capsule. Neither ship may cloak; scanner source cannot use injectors/torus. Out-of-range scanning pauses; target switch stops it, retaining per-target progress for later resumption. Successful official scan moves hidden bounty into visible bounty once and clears hidden amount; repeated scans cannot inflate it. Ordinary bounty payout is distinct from the Most Wanted bonus and follows credited killer/selected core legal rules. Destroying a clean-looking unscanned trader can therefore incur legal consequences.

Licence source price800CR, tech index10,900-second installation, clean-only, government>3 and GalCop-aligned station; no NPC purchase. Renewal400CR, tech index8,900 seconds, allowed at floor(ageDays)25-40 inclusive; fresh full licence denied at day1-40, permitted after40. Expiry age>=30 removes licence; warning at25-<30. Renewal resets purchase time to now, not old expiry+30. Docking with bounty>0 at GalCop station/government>4/tech index>9 queues revocation and clears purchase date. Scripted award bypass is a source API exception, not a shop loophole. Licence is commander authorization: ship swaps/storage cannot renew its date or evade revocation.

Once-only bonus = floor(realBounty*(roleBase+accuracy/50)), roleBase trader/courier/smuggler0.05, hunter0.10, pirate0.20, assassin0.15. Freeze the target's real bounty and accuracy at credited death. Security-office claim requires current orbital GalCop HQ: zero-based systems by galaxies1-8 are7,33,26,49,102,85,202,7 in Bounty System0.19. HQ bonuses survive galaxy changes; no surcharge or licence requirement is introduced for an already earned receipt. The source handler checks location only in menu enablement: project rechecks authority/location in the atomic payout itself. Clear/mark exact receipt IDs and save balance with claims; visiting HQ again cannot repay them. Ordinary warrant payments and captured-pilot custody share a unique settlement ledger so scan/ejection/hull destruction never pay the same underlying warrant twice.
