Collect a whole-hold medical consignment at a high-tech industrial system's main orbital station, then deliver it to an RRS hospital operating at an orbital mining outpost in a disease system. It replaces one eligible base6 offer. Apply [scope v4](implementation-scope.md), the [RRS board/HQ adapter](rrs-contract-board-specifications.md), [native coordinates](oolite-coordinate-contract.md), [crew and capacity](crew-and-passenger-specifications.md) and [shipyard/storage contract](player-shipyard-design.md).
