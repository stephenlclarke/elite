# Rescue Stations: derelict escort and two variants

This packet closes three retained P30 profiles from Rescue Stations 1.5.4: `P30-ESCORT-1`, `P30-ESCORT-1A-ASTEROIDS` and `P30-ESCORT-1B-THARGOID`. The original objective is to escort an RRS Arafura to a disabled vessel, protect ship-to-ship repairs, then bring both ships safely back to the accepting orbital station. It contains no playable surface stage or on-foot operation. Other P30 scenarios remain separate research work; these three profiles do not claim to complete the whole expansion. Apply [scope v4](implementation-scope.md), [station handoffs](station-handoff-specifications.md), [crew/cabin rules](crew-and-passenger-specifications.md) and [shipyard persistence](player-shipyard-design.md).
