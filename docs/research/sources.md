## Evidence disagreements

Use S01/S04/S05/S06 for official 6502 mission inventory rather than S08's older conjectures. S06 describes more than one C64 cash threshold; S34/S35 resolve the apparent disagreement as different byte predicates, including C64 wraparound. S07 reports observed rather than code-derived point triggers. S19 repeats Imperial Courier and omits several utility ships present in S20. S17 explicitly labels its cut-mission execution suggestions as guesses. D05 is not enough to reproduce exact Operation stages. Official ED website links were intermittently inaccessible; community directory claims must not be described as freshly verified official release status.
