## Benus escort and PAD

### Availability and encounter

`Scripts/vector_pad.js:shipExitedWitchspace` offers the encounter after the updater has established `vector_version`, score at least 300, a normal system with a non-nova sun and PAD not yet unlocked. It samples up to ten arrival ship counts; threshold is `max(ceil(mean(samples)),55)`. It returns when `(clock.seconds & 1)` is nonzero. If the current ship count is below the threshold and no `vector_benus` exists, spawn one at 0.75 along the witchpoint-to-planet route. Bitwise clock parity uses integer truncation; it is an arrival opportunity, not an every-second spawn timer. Project repair: initialize the version marker on a new game, use one persisted encounter ID per opportunity and do not regenerate a fleet on reload. Preserve the source threshold rule; a gallery preview can use a fixed seed and low ambient count.

`vector_benus.js` uses an Anaconda refit with four Sidewinder escorts. Its special script disables itself if instantiated with primary role `trader`; preserve the mission role until initialization. The statement `this.ship.primaryRole==='trader'` is a source no-op comparison, not an assignment. Probability `random > 0.5` spawns two Thargoid warships within 25,000 units of Benus and creates a visual position marker. Otherwise the encounter relies on ambient danger. Do not invent a fixed kill quota or reward per Thargoid. Use the pinned O08 Thargoid/Sidewinder loadout profile for this selected project edition; their generic engine AI is a borrowed dependency, not a new hull.

Hostile-area predicate selects valid ships within 25,600 of Benus, excluding the player, cargo, rocks, derelicts and suspended AI, where bounty is positive, primary role is pirate, or the ship targets Benus while outside his group. On full energy, target destroyed or target lost, Benus chooses the first matching hostile and attacks; no remaining hostile clears his combat flag and enables expanded comms. When a non-player attacks and the player is strictly within 23,000, the first distress contact changes chatter from 1 to 2, marks him as Benus and opens comms. After threats clear with the player still within 23,000, chatter becomes 3. Merely killing two scripted Thargoids is neither necessary nor sufficient if the encounter was not engaged or other hostiles remain.

Benus normally heads toward the main orbital station. `vector_benusAI.plist` preserves route avoidance/waypoints, 30,000 desired range on the initial planet-directed navigation, 10,000 approach range and subsequent 5,000 station approach updates. These are flight-navigation cues, not surface landing. Speed factors are 0.65 initial, 0.85 cruising, 0.8 approach, 0.95 after friendly progress and 2.95 for speed-up. The last is injector-request behaviour; do not multiply the published maximum cruise speed permanently. Source AI update pause is 10 seconds. Generic waypoint, interception and docking AI use the selected O08 engine profile.

### Comms, completion and failure

Comms actions are hail, direction and safety, followed by help and speed-up when expanded. Actions require distance at most 25,000 and no player-attack flag. Help can redirect Benus to the player's wanted target when the player is threatened, the target is not Benus and target bounty is nonzero. Speed-up requires player maximum speed strictly above 340, Benus not fighting and Benus farther than 25,000 from the main station; if Benus has zero fuel it adds one fuel unit and sends the speed-up message. These options do not change the mission reward.

Success is Benus reaching docking while the player is still in flight, strictly within 23,000 of Benus and chatter greater than 1. The player then docks and confirms the pending remote delivery. Source gift makes PAD effective tech index 1 and adds a journal entry; it gives no cash and does not automatically award a player Vector. PAD is a zero-price service token: purchase removes the equipment immediately and opens the journal. Project adaptation: persist a commander-wide PAD service unlock and expose it directly as a docked interface, independent of currently fitted hull equipment. This avoids pretending the journal requires a cargo slot.

Docking before Benus, staying too far away, failing to engage his distress, his death or his escape-pod launch yields no gift for that encounter. If the player attacks Benus, source chatter becomes 0, comms/marker are removed and `pflag` suppresses cooperation. Project persistence must keep that flag through reload. Source death/escape does not rescue the encounter or unlock PAD; another eligible arrival can generate a fresh opportunity. Leaving the system before docking ends that encounter without reward or a permanent campaign lock. No source deadline, cash fee, passenger berth or cargo reservation exists for escorting Benus.

### Questionnaire, punishment and injury
