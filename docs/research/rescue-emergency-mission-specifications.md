# RRS emergency freighter escort mission specification

P30 Rescue Stations1.5.4 scenario5 is complete as bounded project profile PROJECT-P30-EMERGENCY-5-v1. Protect one actual freighter during its emergency approach to the accepting orbital station, then dock there for payment. Scope ELITE-SPACE-MISSIONS-v4 applies; no planetary docking or on-foot stage exists in this objective. Variant5a is a distinct returning-Arafura mission and remains open. Research readiness and delivery do not prove playable implementation.

## Emergency approach and docking

The legacy docking table is retained in full. GLOBAL sets speed factor1, drops DOCKING_ABORTED/FACING_DESTINATION messages, chooses nearest station, requests coordinates and sets a10-second next-think pause. APPROACH/BACK_OFF flies toward station with desired range3000; TRY_AGAIN_LATER/HOLD_POSITION waits5. APPROACH_COORDINATES enters DOCKING_CORRIDOR. Its UPDATE requests getDC every3seconds and throws sparks; getDC supplies target.position+target.vectorForward*3000 and synchronously sends CORRIDOR_POSITION. That handler flies at full maximum speed with desired range1. Once reached, getDB supplies the station center and DOCKING_BAY faces it, pausing3seconds, then flies at full speed after FACING_DESTINATION. At desired range it returns GLOBAL. DOCKED/NO_STATION_FOUND exits through dockEscorts/exitAI. pauseAI delays subsequent thinking, not the remaining actions in the same list.

Necessary labelled identity repair substitutes exact accepted-home targeting for the native repeated nearest-station selection, and binds getDC/getDB to that home. Otherwise another station can receive the freighter while its any-station callback authorizes a false payment. Preserve the existing source approach speed/ranges and actual rotating station/craft geometry. Do not count reaching3000 or1 units, clearance approval, scene removal or an AI DOCKED message as an independent physical docking receipt. O08 enterDock dispatches both shipWillDockWithStation and shipDockedWithStation, notes the docked ship and removes the entity as one operation; the project must commit the comparable actual successful dock transaction once.

The waystation has9000energy/15recharge and real separate receiving/launch ports: receiving rrs_dock_oct at(0,0,280) allows docking but not launch; aft rrs_dock_oct_launch at(0,0,-280), rotated180degrees, allows launch but not docking. The dock mesh envelope is225.61138 by225.61138 by140; the four freighter base meshes have bounds Python73.9416x40.3051x142.7642, Boa62.8826x62.6144x112.6544, Cruiser67.7584x59.8272x113.7957 and Anaconda77.5304x62.5616x166.5346. These are source-unit AABBs, not proof of port clearance, internal volume or full collision shape. Use the real receiving port, orientation and swept collision/admission rules. HQ support requires actual compatible receiving geometry and the saved HQ identity; no general HQ market or new station role is granted.

The station's ordinary defense AI can also respond to real attacks: max_defense_ships6, max_police0 and defense_ship_role rrs-defender. It is not a mission order spawning six extra fighters immediately. Bound RRS defense weights are the same as rrs-escort. Native launchDefenseShip requires a valid aggressor, cap room, a valid fitting craft and a queue operation, can abort dockings, and chooses modern defense AI only if the newly created craft is already police/hermit class.
