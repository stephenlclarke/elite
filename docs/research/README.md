## How to use the data

Choose a version profile first. A common mission/hull name does not prove identical rules, geometry or statistics across ports. Source mission NPCs remain distinct from player-refit adaptations. A known player hold of zero is not replaced by a guessed positive cargo value. Cargo capacity, salvage count, equipment fit, missile capacity and current stock remain separate.

All 161 current whole-craft catalogue rows have filled common project flight/refit parameters. Missing geometry uses an explicitly provisional named surrogate. That does not establish accurate silhouettes, original player capabilities or every optional expansion hull. Shipyard changes preserve unique equipment ownership and store everything that cannot fit; reselecting a hull must not replenish ammunition or duplicate the mandatory baseline.

The [archive](archive/README.md) retains excluded FFE, Guardian surface and Operations material solely for provenance. Do not implement archived missions, excluded generated variants or P01 adventure pages. Frontier-derived ship data remains available because the owner removed missions without requesting deletion of ships.
