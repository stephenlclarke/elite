## Hunting-lodge challenges

Outside the current tournament home, the lodge offers up to three candidate opponents per visit. At most six launched challenges are allowed between jumps (source stops when count>5). Generate knight/lord/prince with probabilities65%/25%/10%, internal score600..899/900..1899/1500..3499. Rating formulas are respectively score*375*300*2*25/15, score*360*750*4*32/25, score*400*1100*4*50/35. Those constants are legacy odds inputs, not reliable current hull durability: the inspected knight dictionary has energy1500, for example.

Odds = min(10,round(rating*100/(50*player.maxSpeed*player.maxEnergy*player.thrust*player.missileCapacity))/100). Stakes100/1000/10000 credits require sufficient cash, are deducted on acceptance, and frozen return is round(stake*odds). Victory repays stake+return; forfeit loses stake. Source acceptance thresholds differ on the first candidate page (0.5 warning versus0.2 gate); repaired profile consistently requires odds>0.2 and validates turret eligibility before accepting, with a finite positive denominator. Do not recompute the stake return after a hull change.

Opponent spawns wsm[0,120100,0], begins combat when player distance<5,000 and yields at the native ENERGY_LOW event, producing a flare and VICTORY. Destroying an opponent while RUNNING also produces VICTORY; destroying the yielded opponent while already VICTORY changes FORFEIT. Player flare activation yields/FORFEIT. Docking early at lodge/main station or leaving the system forfeits; leaving after victory discards an unpaid claim in the source. No invented time limit is supplied.

During challenge, disable the player's cloak/energy bomb and store personal missiles, fitting one quest yield flare. The project uses unique persistent storage/custody, not source-only transient missile arrays or recreated conditionless modules. Restore only owned compatible items after resolution; surplus remains stored. Baseline missile capacity still exists even while tournament rules temporarily remove live rounds. Spent rounds are not replenished by swapping hulls or restarting a challenge.

## Six-stage tournament

Rank acceptance initializes a jump counter. In eligible galaxies, after >50 jumps with no tournament, announce a selected house and entry deadline currentDay+30. Entry at its hunting lodge removes the deadline; equality at the entry deadline remains valid. Decline/expiry resets the tournament. There is no scripted entry fee. Each event may be deferred before launch; docking at the tournament lodge evaluates the event and advances or terminates it.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Shield shooting | Ten feudal-shield targets within23,000 of wsm[0,122000,0]; missiles stored. Shield death asks buoy to check after two seconds. | At least8 scored checks. Score only when player is then<5,000 from buoy; attribution is not checked in original. |
| 2 Drone ladder | One feudal-drone-1 within5,000 of buoy; each death spawns next level through5. Missiles/bomb disabled. | At least4 kills, even though five stages can exist. Source drone speeds250/300/400/500/600, energy100; use individual thrust/rates from resources. |
| 3 Collection quartet | One scoopable drone within2,000 of buoy plus one knight, prince and lord within5,000 of player. Player injectors disabled. | Scoop at least1 drone, then return. Rivals collect it or fight; their fuel set0. |
| 4 Cascade field | Away gate wsu[0,0,-2.05]; home gate within20,000 of buoy. Around wsu[0,0,-2.01]:224 asteroids,48 mines and10 shield pods. | Destroy pods to release small shields; scoop at least8. |

Gates teleport at player distance<200 to the other gate's position+[0,0,500] and copy its orientation. This is in-system teleportation, not a galactic jump or planetside stage. Mines activate when player distance<4,000 and spawn30 splinters on death. Source field component placements and subentity geometry remain numerical resource data. Repaired scoring uses mission-owned unique targets/traversals and disallows NPC/environmental score farming. At event4, use the original shield-pod-to-small-shield custody rather than granting eight shields for docking.

Source event3 role test is always true because it ORs nonempty strings; repair explicit role comparisons. Its injector restoration tests a status string's truthiness and can grant injectors that were never owned; restore only the stored original item. Capture the actual final survivor's house/role rather than rely on the buoy's error-prone house-name array (including an overwritten index). All exit/death/failure paths restore equipment and stop timers; original code omits some restoration on jump. Event6 source completion is lethal, distinct from the ordinary yielding challenge.
