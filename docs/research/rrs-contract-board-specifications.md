The project therefore enables the RRS contract board at both waystations and headquarters through `PROJECT-RRS-BOARD-v1`. This is an explicit project extension: source Rescue Stations1.5.4 gives rescue_station-role waystations the board, while rrs_headquarters has different roles and does not install it. Preserve that source distinction in [mechanics](data/rescue-family1-mechanics.json); do not rewrite native roles or claim historical headquarters support. [Board profile data](data/rrs-contract-board-profile.json) controls the project adapter.

Apply [scope v4](implementation-scope.md). Only individually completed, implemented and enabled retained space profiles can appear as actionable live offers. ## Access and station capability

While docked at either an RRS waystation or headquarters, show **RRS Contract Board** in station services. Opening it does not launch the ship, change equipment, pay money or accept a mission. In flight, this docked service is unavailable; an accepted mission remains visible in the ordinary mission journal. Other station types gain this board only through a separately specified station capability, not by a name containing RRS or sharing a model.

Use explicit station capability `rrs_contract_board`, with origin source for waystations and project_extension for headquarters. Keep native roles rescue_station and rrs_galactic_hq unchanged, so ordinary population, markets, legal status, NPC scripts and docking do not accidentally inherit one another's behaviour. All eight source HQ systems also have a distinct generated waystation; the two stations need different stable IDs and separate board snapshots even when they occupy the same system.

Each accepted contract stores original offering station ID, galaxy/system, station class, mission/profile revision and board-adapter revision. Source callbacks that search only rescue_station by role must use that accepted station identity in the project adapter. Headquarters-accepted escort ships return to that exact headquarters; a separate same-system waystation cannot substitute. Main-station couriers still deliver to the original system's main orbital station, regardless of whether the start was a headquarters or waystation. Source station-destruction failure remains effective; neither automatic respawning nor another RRS board cancels that failure.

For escort launches at headquarters, prefer the selected source final fleet offset only when it clears the inspected assembled station/ship envelopes. Otherwise use a labelled headquarters-extension launch point on its stable launch axis beyond the assembled station boundary plus1000 source units, with the source formation offsets applied once. Save the chosen point. Unknown assembled clearance blocks activation of that HQ escort offer until the renderer/collision adapter supplies a safe envelope; a visible preview can explain that limitation without pretending the mission is playable. Do not spawn into station geometry or silently replace the mission with an easier courier. The waystation source profile retains its established launch coordinates.

## Board and offer details

The board identifies the docked station, current RRS reputation and any active RRS assignment. Each offer row shows mission title, source variant, destination, reward, time limit and relevant ship requirements. Selecting a row opens its briefing and full requirements. Avoid showing simulation/debug keys as customer-facing labels. Show source or project provenance on request, including the headquarters extension and any labelled mission estimate.

Offer generation uses the selected RRS base/variant gates, a saved seed and the current whole simulation-hour index. Save board contents per station/hour and preserve their decisions when returning from a briefing, launching/redocking or loading. A variant replaces its eligible base slot rather than adding an unrelated extra job. Preserve first-eligible priority and used-replacement guards: each variant occurs at most once per saved board, including4a/4b. Source comparator-based shuffling is engine-dependent; the selected labelled scheduler adapter uses a saved-seed Fisher-Yates order without claiming the exact historical distribution. An earlier station's board cannot overwrite another station's accepted contract or regenerate its target/reward. A preview has no active mission, expiry, custody or equipment side effects.

Show an explicit empty state when no contracts qualify, stating the relevant known reason where useful, such as reputation, legal status, unavailable destination, occupied RRS assignment or ship capacity. Required unresolved implementation/geometry should be labelled unavailable rather than offered as a working mission. Do not invent lower thresholds, erase failed-attempt quotas or reroll repeatedly until a desired job appears. A full party must fit the current ship; a single spare seat cannot satisfy a larger party.

Offer details include actual cargo/equipment/pylon/cabin shortfall, fitted versus hypothetical cargo extension, compatible passenger life support, required operating crew and reserved quarters. Reuse the [crew/passenger fit rules](crew-and-passenger-specifications.md): initial charter berths are zero; each fitted common berth costs5tonnes and1equipmentunit; crew quarters are already outside known usable hold; passengers/mission cargo never go into equipment storage. Pure escorts and document couriers require no new passenger berths or fictional crate tonnage. Future passenger/recovery jobs use their individually completed custody requirements.

Distinguish a generated offer from permission to accept it. A completed and implemented job can remain visible with **Ship requirements unmet** and a disabled Accept button when current fit is insufficient; show the precise missing capacity and let the player return after fitting the ship. This does not authorize acceptance or reserve anything. Unimplemented or research-open jobs never enter the live offer generator; their reference entries belong in the research/gallery catalogue and cannot be accepted. Unresolved safe launch geometry can disable an otherwise eligible offer with a clear reason. Evaluate fit again after shipyard changes without rerolling the saved offer or pretending that equipment in storage is installed.

The quoted time limit is a duration until acceptance. Merely reading a briefing, switching between board rows or leaving the board open does not start its mission deadline. At atomic acceptance bind the selected profile's duration to the actual simulation timestamp, retaining any individually specified source-clock exception. This is the labelled common acceptance-clock repair already selected by the completed mission profiles. If the whole-hour board revision changes while the briefing is open, Accept refuses the stale revision and returns to the current board without accepting, charging or reserving. Accepted contracts retain their original deadlines across board refresh, docking and save/load; only normal simulation time advances them.

## Accept, decline and active work

The briefing has **Accept contract**, **Decline offer** and **Back to board**. Accept first validates the selected profile's current station/route/ship fit, legal/reputation gates, exclusive active RRS slot, resources and unchanged offer revision. Then atomically reserves unique custody/capacity, starts the selected acceptance clock and records one acceptance receipt. Repeated clicks or callback replay return that same accepted state. A stale offer explains what changed and leaves state unchanged. Closing or Back never accepts, declines, launches, deducts a deposit or fits equipment.

Decline removes that offer for the current saved board snapshot without a mission failure or reputation penalty. Accepted abandonment is a different action on the active contract, with that profile's stated consequences and a reviewable confirmation. It cannot delete another mission, erase an occupied cabin or turn leased mission equipment into free personal gear. The journal/active-contract card shows destination, completed objectives, timers, protected ships/people/cargo and the profile's return/hand-in requirement.

Use each mission's established launch policy. Urgent escort acceptance may expose its explicit once-only launch confirmation; the nonurgent courier remains docked until normal launch. Headquarters board access does not grant automatic full fuel, equipment, ammo, escorts or money outside the mission's own defined receipt. A required equipment adjustment is a visible shipyard proposal with compatible transfer and persistent storage; unknown/leftover personal gear remains owned. Occupied mission accommodation and custody must fit before a hull change can confirm.

Successful delivery still needs the correct mission handoff and once-only reward/counter/reputation receipt. Opening a board or visiting another station cannot pay an active mission. An accepted headquarters escort uses headquarters docking receipts; main-station couriers use their main-station receipt. Returning from a completed debrief updates the local board without recreating the completed instance. An active RRS contract at one station is visible at the other RRS boards as active work, not accepted a second time.

## Equipment and headquarters boundaries

The requested headquarters extension covers the contract board. It does not copy every waystation equipment/market service to headquarters, raise the source HQ technology index0, grant police powers or bypass fit restrictions. Native equipment prices and roles remain distinct. The selected source waystation factor2 quotes formation-discount400CR, targeting-waystation3000CR and targeting-discount1200CR; base prices alone are not final station quotes.

When a completed mission requires leased or supplied mission equipment, its contract must define an explicit docked checkout/return service at the accepted station, including headquarters when offered there. This mission-scoped capability can bypass an unrelated general-market technology/role gate only for that contract's unique item; it cannot unlock a whole equipment catalogue or duplicate personal modules. The completed [solar lab2b profile](rescue-solar-mission-specifications.md) now defines its unique80credit escrow,1400second checkout, thermal/recovery custody and original-station return at waystations or headquarters. Implementation and ship-fit readiness still gate its live offer; this does not activate other research-open jobs.
