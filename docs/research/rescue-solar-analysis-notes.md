## Native lab and thermal readers

Source lab is unpiloted CLASS_CARGO, CARGO_SCRIPTED_ITEM, speed0, roll/pitch0.5, thrust18, hull25/regen0, heat_insulation3, no weapons or missiles, model rrs_solarprobe.dat. This is a mission instrument, not a new crewed hull or passenger berth. Its model is already source-hashed outside the game repository.

Pinned O08 ShipEntity.h constants: minimum cabin temperature60, maximum256, solar temperature1250, insulation factor0.00175, cooling factor0.1. ShipEntity.m thermal update uses external E=1250*sunRadius^2/distanceToSunCentre^2, multiplied100 for nova. Heat threshold is insulation*100=300. If E>300 and E>T, T+=(E-T)*dt*0.00175/3. Otherwise if T>60, T+=(E-300-T)*dt*0.1/3, then clamp at least60. When T>256, take heat damage dt*T directly from hull energy; lab has no regeneration. OOJSShip.temperature exposes T/256 and assigns normalized input*256. Source replacement lab startsT60; forced re-population0.65 meansT166.4, not raw0.65. Fixed project integration/substep choice still needs an explicit label rather than claiming exact variable-frame original outcomes.

ShipEntity.canScoop requires working cargo scoop, free cargo capacity, target CLASS_CARGO/nonstation/scoopable, forward hemisphere and for player underside hemisphere. Source scripted-item scoop runs shipWasScooped, and when no commodity is declared adds zero commodity cargo. Thus the lab returns to a pylon, but a full/zero-capacity hold blocks the ordinary intake path. Proposed common project adapter needs a reserved free intake clearance, distinct from an invented physical tonne of lab mass, plus the reserved leased pylon; declare any one-tonne clearance conversion as an estimate. Do not silently admit a known-zero player hold or replace an owned missile by deleting it. Compatible personal ammunition may be stored through a visible atomic fit proposal.
