# Crew complements and usable passenger space

The owner requested sourced crew sizes for all ships, with necessary labelled estimates based on hull size, equipment capacity and cargo space, so passenger missions require real spare accommodation. [Per-hull profiles](data/ship-crew-profiles.json) cover all 170 current canonical records: 161 enabled whole-craft refits, an explicit estimate for unrefitted Black Widow, and eight non-player objects marked not applicable rather than falsely claiming zero native staff. Expansion hull identity work remains open; this is complete for the current catalogue, not a claim that every optional Oolite hull has already been discovered. Apply the same method to each newly deduplicated whole craft before handing it over.

[Source evidence](data/crew-source-evidence.json) retains 173 edition-specific facts and 68 raw FFE structural rows, including placeholders that are not human crew. Different editions stay separate. Crew means operating personnel, including the commander, unless a field explicitly says otherwise. Required crew, optional flight seats, total accommodation, native passenger capacity and fitted project passenger berths are distinct quantities.

## Selected project crew

The common refit is a project adaptation. Prefer its selected edition's verified operating requirement. For D23-based ED refits use one commander as the project minimum; optional multicrew seats remain separately recorded. For O08/classic refits adopt the minimum documented classic complement, visibly labelled as a borrowed project requirement, because the original single-player engine did not enforce hiring. Cobra I uses its explicit single-pilot narrative and retains the conflicting table. For remaining FFE-derived hulls use the recovered FFE total including the commander, with Frontier's manual only when a selected FFE count is unavailable. Do not substitute an ED seat count for required crew on an unrelated edition.

For missing operating counts use `max(1, 1 + floor(0.5 * max(0, sqrt(volumeRatio) - 1) + max(equipmentCapacity / 24, cargoTonnes / 200)))`. All coefficients are necessary labelled project estimates, not historical facts or a physical spacecraft-design law. Equipment capacity and cargo are correlated in the existing refit generator, so the maximum avoids adding the same complexity twice. Store the full input values, resulting count and provenance on each hull; do not recalculate silently after balance changes.

Use an inspected same-scale O08/model envelope relative to the O08 Cobra III's 369874.0268262 cubic model units, or an explicitly scaled O08 surrogate. Do not compare raw GBA/classic integer vertices to metres. When measured comparable geometry is unavailable, use an estimated equivalent size ratio `max(0.05, (cargoTonnes + 5 * equipmentCapacity) / 80)` and label it as a capacity-based proxy, not measured hull volume. The common cargo/equipment fields are themselves labelled where estimated. Use source capacity over a volume guess when known, and preserve known-zero cargo.

Native alien complements and drone autonomy are separate from a humanoid player conversion. Classic Thargoid warship's documented 150 are not automatically 150 human-compatible crew cabins. The player conversion uses the explicit estimate method and humanoid environmental/refit assumptions; source alien data remains attached. An escape capsule's occupant is not a working crew roster for an autonomous missile. Stations, satellites and fixed platforms do not acquire a false human crew of zero through the player formula.

`required_crew_including_commander` and `reserved_crew_accommodation` are now filled on all 161 project refits, linked to their individual crew records. The reserved operating complement is intrinsic accommodation outside the published usable cargo and generic equipment budgets. Do not subtract it again from a known cargo capacity: that would incorrectly turn crew requirements into an invented reduction of a source hold. No crew hiring, salaries, skills, NPC dialogue or management screen is requested by this specification. For this phase assume each selected hull's normal operating complement is assigned; account for its reserved quarters rather than introducing a new recruitment blocker.

## Passenger fit and mission admission

The existing common passenger module reserves **5 tonnes of hold and 1 equipment unit per person**, including life support and a dedicated berth. These are explicit common-project choices, not native ED economy-cabin seat numbers. Native ED module layouts and capacities stay in D23; a future native adapter must use its own slot/class/seat rules rather than applying this common conversion and the native module reservation simultaneously.

First charter passenger berths default to zero. SH169 Sunray is the named verified source-standard exception: one actually fitted, uniquely owned berth from its selected native player yard, costing5tonnes/1equipmentunit once on genuine first acquisition. Its10-tonne base hold therefore has5tonnes remaining with that berth. Transfer/storage/reload/reselection cannot create another module. Published crew seats, spare bunks or a theoretical native passenger maximum do not silently install charter cabins. A source-specific mission/hull may separately authorize a fitted native accommodation adapter; it must identify capacity and life support explicitly. Classic Viper's ten-passenger and Transporter's hundred-passenger claims are historical records, not free common-refit module grants. This keeps known-zero cargo honest while allowing a later explicitly specified purpose-built passenger interior.

For the current common profile:

1. Determine standard usable hold plus an actually fitted cargo extension. Subtract cargo manifest, reserved mission cargo and all fitted module cargo footprints, including 5 tonnes for each installed passenger berth. The result must be nonnegative. Do not include hypothetical cargo expansion, stored equipment or parked-hull space.
2. Subtract all fitted transferable-equipment costs, including 1 equipment unit per berth, from this hull's equipment capacity. The result must be nonnegative. Intrinsic required systems/crew are already outside that budget; do not count them twice. A berth is a fitted owned module, not a zero-cost screen flag.
3. Available passenger places are `installedBerths - regularPassengers - missionPassengers - optionalExtraCrewUsingThoseBerths`, with each person/cabin reservation counted once. Required operating crew occupy their intrinsic quarters and do not consume these fitted charter berths.
4. Accept a passenger mission only when both fit budgets are valid and the entire requested party fits available compatible berths. Apply any established cabin class/species/environment condition. Generic common berths are humanoid-compatible; an established incompatible species requires a separately specified adapter. Refuse with the actual shortfall; do not infer seats from an empty cargo hold.
5. A source reserved/unregistered passenger token, including Arn and Aldrin, still consumes its specified berth in this project. A sealed rescue/escape pod instead uses its stated hold/special-pod-bay custody and does not become a seated passenger until an explicit station transfer. Arn's rescue route's one-tonne pod estimate and passage route's one berth are different paths.

Changing equipment after acceptance must preserve passenger life support and berth reservations. Do not fit cargo, missiles, extra crew or other modules into occupied passenger accommodation. Converting an empty berth back to hold is allowed only docked, with no passenger/reservation in it; the berth module goes into persistent equipment storage. No person goes into equipment storage and no standing passenger is silently disembarked, reassigned or marked delivered to make a proposal fit.

Per-hull empty-hold berth ceilings in JSON are upper bounds with **no transferable equipment fitted**. Actual source-standard equipment also consumes capacity. A new Vector SE carries nine equipment units of listed extras under the common costs, leaving one of its ten units; it can initially fit one berth using five of its twelve hold tonnes. If the owner intentionally stores compatible nonessential extras, its empty-standard-hold theoretical limit is two berths; source 4-tonne expansion raises the hold to sixteen and the otherwise unequipped theoretical limit to three. These are proposed fit states, not automatic rewards or duplicated gear.

## Shipyard, comparison, save and gallery

Show **Required crew**, **Reserved crew quarters**, **Fitted passenger berths**, **Passengers aboard**, **Free passenger places**, **Hold used by cabins** and **Usable cargo after fit** in the shipyard and aligned comparison panels. Include source/estimate details on demand. A ship with a bigger hull can still have fewer free passenger places after its actual equipment/cargo layout. Linked comparison rotation remains unchanged: both ships share orientation, plane and direction in all axes.

The transfer proposal moves each uniquely owned berth module like other equipment when compatible and empty. An occupied berth is a mission/passenger custody requirement: reserve compatible accommodation on the destination hull before confirmation. If insufficient, block the hull swap and show missing places or space; retain the current hull/state. Unoccupied surplus cabin modules go to persistent storage with their identity/condition; they create no free passengers or repeated equipment grants. The required operating complement remains assigned to its respective hull in this abstract phase; it does not consume passenger seats or require a new crew-hiring flow when choosing another hull.

Save hull/crew profile version, fitted berth IDs/condition, person/party IDs, cabin/berth reservation IDs, life-support compatibility, cargo/equipment reservations and accepted mission references atomically. Validate on load and before accepting, launching, refitting, disembarking or changing hull. A legacy save with passengers but no berth accounting migrates to an explicit protected occupied-cabin reservation, preserving the people and mission; flag any cargo/equipment overcapacity and require a docked correction before launch. Never delete a person, invent a delivery reward or silently grant a second cabin. Completed mission and gallery save isolation rules still apply.

Mission Gallery passenger scenarios must show the actual party size, required berths, crew reservation and cargo/equipment fit. Include a successful capacity example, full-berth refusal, insufficient-hold refusal, equipment-limited refusal, occupied-cabin swap block and sealed-pod alternative.
