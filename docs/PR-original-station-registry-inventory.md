# Bind native station inventory to the installed original source

NativeStationLedger derives its record limit from the existing immutable NativeStationSource.bindings rather than the predecessor 372-entry inventory. An independent maximum of eight times 257 source slots bounds source initialization, ledger validation and early current-save decoding. NativeStationWorld still requires the exact complete installed count, authentic source membership, unique identities, matching poses and all existing custody/resource/clock checks. The station producer, government rules, physical actor/admission/geometry budgets, random sequence and legal rules are unchanged. No source record is dropped, station is invented or predecessor save conversion is added.

Meaningful native regressions launch ordinary commanders at actual original systems 0, 7 and 237 without new equipment grants, require all 382 source records and authentic membership, and roundtrip the current station world. Missing and duplicate current-source records must still be rejected. This source repair is the concrete prerequisite for previously blocked mission launch and native debris scenes; it does not establish their later docking, physics or reward outcomes. Strict formatting, Markdown and whitespace checks are author validation only; compiler/native tests remain unrun. See the [issue](ISSUE-original-station-registry-inventory.md).
