# Equipment headings have inconsistent alignment and spacing

The equipment column heading starts at the indented item position, while category headings start at the left edge. The fuel category uses `Fuel & Storage`, whose ampersand appears as a blank glyph in the supplied screenshot and leaves an excessive gap.

Align the equipment heading with category headings and use `Fuel Storage` with exactly one space. See [the correction](PR-equipment-heading-alignment.md).
