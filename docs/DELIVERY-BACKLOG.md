Protected publication and exact-main Sonar remain enforced by the [main workflow](../.github/workflows/ci.yml) and [live quality dashboard](https://sonarcloud.io/summary/new_code?id=stephenlclarke_elite). Version 1.3.1 also makes the chart-footer and celestial-bearing conditions explicit to resolve the three readability findings from the first main analysis, preserving their behaviour.
