Station Gossip now opens a current-galaxy wanted board with up to ten actual eligible NPC careers plus a competing wanted player. Rows show rank/callsign and craft/reward on separate compact lines so reward stays visible. Return/Details opens a scrollable dossier, Escape/Back returns, arrows/click select, G/Gossip retains the complete PLUGH rumour text and K documents controls. ## Career and observation authority

A saved optional WantedCareerLedger holds ordinary NPC logical identities, their last actual physical binding, craft/legal/bounty state, literal recorded ship kills, last sensor report and retained native snapshot. The optional Commander field defaults to empty with earlier history Unknown; existing rank points are never converted. Registry callsigns are explicitly assigned identity labels such as Pilot G1-S7-V1-A4, not invented personal biographies or claims that authored history was observed.

Only successful existing NPC persistence captures create/update ordinary careers. Mission/protected and provider-owned actors (including actual joined native Crimbo members) retain their own persistence and reward paths; this registry does not park or respawn them. New registry records require an actual encounter within the existing perception radius; subsequent parked state can update without fabricating a new sighting. Location and heading come from that actual captured system-space pose at its physical timestamp. A destination is reported only from an explicit generated/recorded different-system route; otherwise it is Unknown. Zero recorded kills means none recorded by this ledger, with earlier history explicitly Unknown. It is not a zero-lifetime-kills claim.

The registry is bounded at4096 careers,16384 actual physical-actor aliases and8192 settled ship-death identities. Actual observed aliases retain attribution for a source that docks or disappears before its already-launched projectile kills a ship; they never join a different live craft for purple presentation. Reaching either limit is explicitly reported as unrecorded facts Unknown. Death retirement still removes an unavailable target when history capacity is exhausted. No fabricated replacement history or eviction supplies a fresh target. Career snapshots, binding/ship/legal consistency, observation times, global actor high-water marks and visit/time bounds participate in saved Commander validation.

On a real revisit, eligible parked snapshots are restored only to their original system's existing NPCPersistenceLedger, preserving spent resources, canonical position and stable logical identity. Stale departed-ledger reference proofs are discarded; the existing ordinary reference rebind policy handles player/station/surviving actors. Existing off-scene coasting runs before admission; the maintained bounded persistence spawn requests, ordinary population limits and real receipts allocate any new current actor. There is no spawn-on-read, player-arrival placement, new encounter provider, off-scene combat/transit simulation, or move to the reported destination. Cull/docking/EMP transfer/terminal reconciliation does not count a kill or pay a reward.

## Ranking, settlement and colour

Eligibility requires an active retained encounterable career, positive actual native bounty and wanted legal state. Order is bounty descending, recorded literal kills descending, legal severity descending, then stable identity. The wanted player uses the existing ordinary pirate schedule of50 tenths (5 CR); clean players are excluded regardless of rank. The same standard bounty supplies the player's native LegalRecord. All NPC bounties, including zero-reward wanted records and larger authored rewards, remain exactly their existing native values; zero-reward records do not qualify. No new bounty curve or board multiplier exists.

Accepted native DeathSettlement is the only new history input. Only attributed ship deaths count; self, projectile and duplicate settlement observations do not. Player rank/cash continue through the existing creditCombatDeath operation unchanged. NPC-attributed ship deaths can update their recorded count and retire the victim without player credit. A listed retired career produces one native text/voice closure notice after the once-only history admission. The board adds no payment, bonus, alternate emission or mission entitlement. Legal amnesty removes player eligibility immediately without deleting recorded history; normal hull transfer retains the Commander career payload.

The shared classifier adds purple for exact currently joined top-ten craft. It refuses stale/foreign/currently unlisted bindings, nonships, protected actors, pods, payloads, ordnance and salvage. Qualified disabled/recoverable objects retain orange priority; incoming missiles retain red. Hostile danger derives from actual hostility and active energy independently of purple presentation. Existing scanner, target marker/panel and occluded four-view world glow consume this shared cue. The panel includes WANTED rank/reward even below identification tier. No range, clipping, depth or planet/station occlusion bypass is added.
