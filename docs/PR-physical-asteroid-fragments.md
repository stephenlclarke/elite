# feat: retain asteroid breakup mass and finite salvage

## Phase one implementation

Canonical ring rocks expose birth mass from their authored commodity quantity. One mineral/alloy unit is 1,000 kg; one gold/platinum unit is 1 kg. Radius remains native collision geometry, with existing ring presence, colours, size and ordinary mining yields unchanged. This is an explicit authored mass basis, not a claim that collision units are metres or that each content type has the same physical density.

MiningLedger stores at most 256 root breakup records. Each record retains its original immutable root, commodity, birth mass and allowance, canonical actual motion/time, current binding, partial work, issued/collected counters, descendant materialization, rubble flags and observation times. The allowance is `(originalUnits - 1) / 20`, which is strictly below five percent even for 1, 20 and 40 units. A small rock can therefore have zero collectible units. Ordinary re-shattering never resets work or allowance. Normal Mining Laser extraction of an intact rock remains distinct and retains its existing finite yield.

A single narrow GameplayCombat call routes canonical ring hits to the new adapter. After the actual accepted hit the existing parent actor stays alive as a solid aggregate of unresolved fragments/dust, with its actual velocity and world position. No actor is invented and no replacement admission can erase its mass. Capacity/invalid-motion refusal leaves the parent and ledger intact. Current source observation and native mining activation persist and restore actual aggregate motion, including coasting between observations, using the existing allocator and receipts after cull/revisit. The original conservative mesh envelope remains shared with native raycasts and ordnance contacts.

Mining work issues at most the one finite root allowance as ordinary typed ore proposals. All units inherit actual parent velocity, so residual plus issued momentum equals incoming momentum. `aggregateMassKg` is the residual allocation after issued reservations; `asteroidDebrisMassKg` additionally includes never-materialized reservations still physically held by the parent. Only a real successful admission receipt materializes a canister and releases that reserved physical mass. Refusal keeps pending custody and the physical parent. Previously materialized but culled descendants retain their own canonical poses rather than returning to the parent.

The local native ore-destruction adapter keeps the actual ore actor, converts it to an asteroid hazard and retains its commodity mass, position and velocity. Rubble is not collectible, and repeat destruction does not issue more ore. Pending rubble uses ordinary bounded asteroid admission after unloading. Breakup descendants retain custody across departure; existing unrelated mined-ore expiry remains unchanged. A successful real collection records the spent root allowance and transfers the typed commodity to cargo exactly once. Current-format decode bounds root records and validates mass, counters, pending descendants, ownership joins, canonical quantity and motion. No predecessor converter or migration is introduced.

The follow-up to independent review requires materialization markers for every currently bound child and for each issued child no longer pending after collection. A sibling's marker cannot cover another child; genuinely culled materialized children remain valid. Focused malformed-current-save regressions remove the selected marker from bound and collected states, retain a legitimate culled roundtrip, and require decode refusal.

Pending offers now reserve the requested population independently: cargo uses the cargo quota and rubble the asteroid quota, with both consuming the shared ordinary budget. Outstanding offers are counted in their own population. Focused cases fill cargo while allowing rubble, fill asteroids while allowing cargo, retain refused offers for retry, prevent duplicate offers and check mixed outstanding reservations.
