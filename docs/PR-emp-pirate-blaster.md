# Add EMP Pirate Blaster, finite crew custody and target cues

The EMP Pirate Blaster is a separately owned forward utility fitting selected with W and operated with held Return. Its 3,000 CR / TL9 shop offer uses existing real purchases; XYZZY grants one while retaining four Naval lasers. A real, occluded 3,000-unit mesh hit consumes 8 energy and 18 heat with a 1.5-second cooldown. It records aggression without applying damage or granting kills, bounty or loot. Drive steering and jump initiation refuse disabled actors while linear/angular motion continues.

Each accepted disablement retains the original actor, traffic/security proof, actual combat incident, weapon ownership, human crew profile, cargo and legal state. Actual occupied escape pods are admitted individually through existing population/safety checks. Capacity refusal keeps remaining crew aboard. The ledger bounds lifetime custody to 32 hulls and 256 people, with explicit refusal before further disablement. Existing accommodation profiles are the crew source, including their established estimate basis; no new humanoid count is invented for unsupported alien/uncrewed hulls.

After every crew member has a real pod, the same hull becomes abandoned salvage. Cutter access requires the existing Salvage Module, cutter and scoops. Its known original cargo feeds the finite salvage plan; death and re-entry do not create fresh cargo or reroll settled work. Actual protected pods cannot be cut, damaged or scooped. A later ordinary lethal attack retains each unejected ordinal explicitly as deceased with its hull; earlier pods remain independent live custody. It stops future ejection and preserves the genuine later death origin and ordinary once-only cargo/reward path. EMP itself never records a lethal outcome. Stable source identities retain people and hulls independently of ordinary traffic culling.

Earlier version readers reject actual EMP fields at their native commander/world paths, while innocent names and mission data remain readable. Raw commander loading refuses EMP state because it cannot retain its physical world. Save validation crosslinks physical members, current bindings, source custody and terminal traffic transfer.

Whole-segment foreground envelopes conservatively suppress glow around thin intervening objects, covering gaps between sampled rays and the halo width; envelopes are prepared once per frame.
