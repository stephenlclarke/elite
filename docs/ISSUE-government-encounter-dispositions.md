# Apply government hostility and anarchy wanted rates per generated ship

FR-030 requests separate 90% hostile and 90% wanted ordinary generated encounters in Anarchy. FR-033 extends initial hostility across governments in order: Anarchy 90%, Feudal 70%, Multi-government 50%, Dictatorship 40%, Communist 30%, Confederacy 20%, Democracy 10%, Corporate State 0%. Other governments retain their existing criminal-status policy. The old geography-weighted pirate decision and larger pack sizes cannot express per-ship disposition probabilities.

Sample actual ambient members independently of occurrence and group size, record their real NPC engagement/legal facts, preserve retained facts and leave real provocation, police, authored missions, admission and crime authority intact.
