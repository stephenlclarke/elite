# Define normal hyperspace drive tiers without inventing immediate travel

FR-026 requires Basic, Intermediate and Advanced drives with activation times of 10, 5 and 1 seconds. FR-027 supersedes the earlier mass-lock wording with distance factors of 100%, 80% and 60%. Ordinary purchases must follow the real sequential prerequisite chain. Existing earned navigation spool effects must remain effective.

The existing runtime takes a positive ShipPerformance.spool and tests fixed mass-lock distances. Immediate alien activation needs a distinct typed branch; setting spool to zero or an epsilon would violate normal performance validation or fake the requested behavior. Alien activation requires actual salvage and authenticated PLUGH fitting, which this source-only foundation cannot authorize. See [implementation and central hooks](PR-hyperspace-drive-profiles.md).
