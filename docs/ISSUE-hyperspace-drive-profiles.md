# Install owned hyperspace drives without losing existing equipment

FR-026 requires real Basic, Intermediate and Advanced drives with activation times of
10, 5 and 1 seconds. FR-027 sets mass-lock distance factors to 100%, 80% and 60%.
Purchases follow the fitted and paid prerequisite chain. Existing navigation effects
remain effective and the original ship catalogue stays intact.

The runtime now registers the drive family, real stock item and shop offers. Existing
versions migrate atomically through finite ammunition and the stock drive, preserving
all original identities and history, including a full ordinary 256-item inventory.
Actual travel, save and regression validation remain pending. Alien immediate travel
still requires its actual salvage and authenticated PLUGH integration.

See [implementation and validation scope](PR-hyperspace-drive-profiles.md).
