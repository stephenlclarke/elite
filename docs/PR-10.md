Its executable, bundle metadata, runtime resources and original secure timestamp/signature are byte-for-byte preserved; its source checkpoint remains `402f42db4c36bd6eac5691d98ad1901944f2bf7e`. The app still supports Apple silicon and Intel with macOS 14 or later.

Apple accepted submission `fb6ded2b-0e06-4009-b0f8-34387a613b5f` with no issues, covering the nested resource bundle and both executable architectures.
