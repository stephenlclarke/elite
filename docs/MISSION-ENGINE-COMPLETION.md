## Proposed first vertical slice: persistent monetary obligations

R06 is the first proposed reusable capability: a shared financial-liability ledger. The [P19 finance specification](research/iron-raven-finance-specifications.md) explicitly requires a shared GSE loan; P04 Bank of the Black Monks also needs actual financial liabilities. Current `credits` commands and per-instance action receipts can debit/pay, but there is no native shared debt principal, creditor record or repayment service. Equipment loan ownership is already implemented and stays separate.

Limit the first slice to a declared fixed-principal loan and partial repayments at a proved original-main station contact. Bind borrower, creditor, offer, principal and revisions; require explicit native Confirm; retain the outstanding liability after mission completion/abandonment; freeze exact quotes and record each accepted payment once. Reuse painted-page proofs, current resource checks, private candidate transactions, persistence and isolated Gallery namespaces. Do not add another inline Commander-bearing review slot. Interest, instalment scheduling, collection NPCs, secondary financial stations and complete P04/P19 mission adaptation are later slices.
