# Native wreck inspection and cutting still use ordinary lifetime pools

The existing schema-27 collector, ownership store and PLUGH fitting paths preserve imported alien custody, but a fresh native wreck still enters the ordinary 4096-wreck map and 256-plan/work/pending pools before it can be inspected or cut. An imported section is permanently retired from the ordinary cutter, so its original partial work cannot continue there. The old native single-budget plan also retains issuance proof in a field that must not pay for every later eight-fragment batch.

## Intended behaviour

- Admit genuinely generated Thargoid wrecks before the ordinary lifetime gates, with exact original site, seed, owner, physical actor, pose, velocity, rotation, health and permanent death evidence.
- Preserve every immutable original inspection, including EMPTY and old unknown timestamps. Link one current versioned work cycle to the fresh, original native or imported legacy inspection.
- Accumulate work for one actual hit-accessible section. Each successful batch of at most eight original emissions consumes a complete budget and resets current work and damage. Retain the original serial allocator, structural quantities and discarded technology.
- Publish released fragments through the actor's real native writer before the common movement stage. A failed writer retains completed work, emits nothing and charges no second cutting budget on retry.
- Flush work and actual source motion on save, pause, target/tool change, cull, docking, jump and death. Retain physical binding census, independent released-fragment custody, complete old ordinary history and no-write reload behaviour.
