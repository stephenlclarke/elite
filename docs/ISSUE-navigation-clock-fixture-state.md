Checkpoint 190 reported three failures in the existing screen-navigation, navigation-save and mission-clock methods. The two consumable fixtures inserted only legacy equipment names after launch had established canonical owned fittings. The clock fixture removed every body from a retained flight scene before saving, which violates the native scene/save contract.

Retain the real native flight scene in the clock fixture rather than deleting its bindings. Versioned restoration keeps the flight scene and clock, while the loader creates fresh unpaused input state: `ScriptWorldSave` has no paused field. Continue that restored flight without a fictitious relaunch or pause toggle. Preserve all panel/paused clock freezes, saved elapsed time, bounded completion and exact credit assertions.
