# Design decisions and review closure

| Review finding | Resolution in this design | Implementation evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 162 specified hulls used only 83 numeric profiles | Explicit per-ID rows replace the repeated role templates. Functional equality excludes price, so price-only changes cannot pass the validator. Family marks exchange speed/handling for capacity, endurance and fitting rather than improving everything. | Real flight/quote/equipment consumers read each record; component checks compare actual selected ships. |
| Specialist names lacked specialist functions | Per-hull utility mounts, equipment budgets, compatible devices, recommended kits and cabin layouts determine the actual job. Command is sensor/ECM support; interdictor uses the existing EMP; no new invisible buff is implied. | Installing the required real tool enables the activity; absent or damaged equipment refuses it. |
| Carrier names implied an absent launch system | Osprey Carrier becomes Osprey Support, with a 64t hold, four utility mounts and a support loadout. Carabao Carrier becomes Carabao Freighter. No unrequested fighter-launch subsystem is introduced. | Names, quotes, help and mission labels agree; no launch action is advertised. |
| Medusa duplicated three ordinary Larvaal ships | Separate four configurations under the latest human clarification: stock base, beatable limited mission fit, paid PLUGH stock purchase and actually paid/upgraded player maximum. Restore stock speed50.3/energy410/shields300/range9, one commander and 13 equipment units. The earlier high numbers and 32-unit expansion are candidate paid endgame targets only. | Prove the limited encounter is beatable/capturable and the actual fully upgraded player loadout leads overall power against other fully upgraded ships. Neither a purchase nor the mission grants a free maximum fit. Stock-only comparisons are insufficient. |
| Alien origin was mistaken for a job | Preserve the accepted `alien` classification and Larvaal origin, add a separate per-hull `primaryUse`: defender, interceptor, surveyor, support, recovery, harvesting and capital roles. | Showroom filters can use both origin and use without rewriting classification. |
| Floating cockpit/glazing sections | Every human cockpit must be structurally attached. Buffalo, Wildebeest and Tapir receive explicit repair recipes; inspect all other hulls against the same rule. | Actual mesh connectivity/contact, opaque support and multi-angle renderer views; a bounding-box overlap alone is insufficient. |
| Crew and accommodation were only E01 estimates | Replace the cargo-derived E01 rule with stored per-hull layout targets, crew places, equipment budgets and utility limits. These are new estimates to be laid out and measured, not recovered historical values. | Physical reserved crew space, distinct living crew, compatible passenger places and hold/equipment accounting. |
| Generic role shapes and weak family variants | Keep common family construction but make each variant's use visible through the specific module/armour/engine/bridge changes listed in GEOMETRY.md. | Family contact sheets at the same physical scale and three-quarter/top/side views. |
| Lammergeier had no performance or fabrication policy | SH163 receives complete salvage-raider specifications and normal earned-blueprint fabrication. This is the proposed resolution of the old U05 blocker, not a silent bypass in current code. | Root accepts the resolution, then the full exact-ID loader/fit/access/physical contract is implemented. |
| Rogue AI styling was ambiguous | Only the Tithe Network gets blocky autonomous conversions. Human contractors, ordinary military ships, station AIs and Larvaal ships keep their own identities. | Tithe ownership plus the explicit conversion ID selects the mesh; an NPC AI controller alone never selects it. |

## Protected game rules

Keep W to change weapon/tool, Return to operate/fire, E for ECM, Q to release the hold, Tab for targeting and F for filters. Keep default mining-laser/cutter selection, the player's W override, actual target-relative hold and finite salvage. Normal-laser asteroid destruction leaves strictly less than 5% collectible mass.

Unknown hulls remain silhouettes; encountered hulls show wireframes and discovery location; commander-attributed EMP disable plus authorised physical cutting/intake earns blueprints; qualifying AI receiving/fabrication enables paid purchase. Stations are catalogued by docking. Capitals remain unpurchasable. Native Larvaal Drone and Tithe conversions can be uncrewed without inventing human casualties. Medusa remains absent until the full 64-clue PLUGH trail, its actual EMP disable, physical lattice recovery and later PLUGH receiving/reconstruction.

Keep the accepted Larvaal Drone's flattened jade shell, three deeply cut circumferential openings and luminous gold interior. Rings lie in the hull's local travel plane and never spin independently. Keep the selected organic Medusa, its eight pods, continuous ellipse, unchanged pod angles, inward-facing green organs and curved dotted projections. Do not restore the superseded crystal, three-nacelle design or former names.
