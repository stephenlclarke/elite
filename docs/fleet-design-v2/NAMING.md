# Naming and themes

Names identify an animal family; classification identifies the ship class; `primaryUse` describes the actual job. These are separate fields. A fighter remains a fighter. This proposal does not rename classifications to animal groups.

## Human names

Use one word for the animal stem. A meaningful variant suffix is allowed: `Mk II`, `Scout`, `Freighter`, `Escort`, `Miner`, `Explorer`. Do not mistake a two-word display name such as `Mole Miner` for a two-word animal name. Avoid multi-word species stems, unexplained numbers/X/Custom, synonyms that name the same animal as another unrelated family, and domestic breed names where a clear wild-animal name is available.

| Use | Theme | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fighters, interceptors and hunters | Predators, primarily birds of prey | Existing approved predator mammals remain valid. No literal feathers, beaks or animal-body imitation is required in geometry. |
| Traders and transports | Prey, pack animals, bovines and boar | Keep meaningful differences between compact traders, bulk cargo carriers and passenger transports. |
| Pirates | Scavengers | Vulture, Raven, Crow, Jackal, Hyena, Condor, Griffon, Magpie and Lammergeier are coherent. Griffon means the vulture, not a mythological creature. |
| Miners | Burrowing animals | Mole, Wombat, Badger, Gopher, Marmot, Aardvark and Armadillo remain coherent. |
| Leisure, passenger and exploration | Prey animals | A racer is distinguished by its actual light fit and speed, not an animal-shaped mesh. |
| Stations and platforms | Existing fortress predator/amphibian families | Preserve current accepted names and service identity. PLUGH remains PLUGH as the service/location name; Lion Bastion is the hull/platform family, not a replacement for PLUGH. |

Keep Peregrine, Javelina, Tapir, Moose, Merlin, Kestrel, Hawk, Hobby, Serval, Impala, Gazelle and Springbok. Keep the current app/project name L337 in this pack; the linked chat's alternative game titles were suggestions and do not authorise another product rename.

[NAME-CHANGES.md](NAME-CHANGES.md) contains all 18 proposed display/family changes and their reasons. SH IDs remain stable. The existing missing Peregrine Mk II number is not filled by renaming Mk III or breaking accepted identities. A new Mk II would require its own deliberate future design.

## Larvaal names

Keep `Larvaal` as origin and race, with the accepted functional epithets. Medusa is the exceptional finale name. Do not rename the Drone, Warship or Sovereign through a generic animal naming sweep. Use `primaryUse` to explain their jobs, not a new invented faction for every hull.

## Tithe Network names

Stephen's subsequent explicit instruction gives the rogue resource AI basic functional designations: **Tithe Scout**, **Tithe Utility**, **Tithe Transport**. These describe scouting/escort, resource transfer and bulk logistics. They are mission conversions of existing hull lineages, not animal families silently renamed everywhere. See [the exact variant contracts](TITHE.md).

Names and animal themes are design choices, not a trademark or copyright clearance claim. External illustrative reference images are not included as owned game assets.
