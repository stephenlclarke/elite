# Fourth energy-system tier without rewriting existing upgrades

The requested Elite Energy Unit supplies four times stock recharge after the existing Advanced/Naval tier. It needs a real owned component, sequential paid shop eligibility, fitting/cost projections, saved identity and secret maximum-hardware integration. Its stock capacity ratio remains one; fourfold capacity is not requested.

Appending this grade must preserve every existing template identity, immutable component origin, actual statistic and already-clamped absolute upgrade receipt. Earlier quotes must become stale against the changed definition. Existing Naval mission rewards remain rewards and must not downgrade an already stronger fitted unit. New prices and technology thresholds are project balance choices, separate from the user's fourfold-recharge requirement.
