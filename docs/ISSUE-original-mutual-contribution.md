# Original mutual relief contribution

## Motivation

G13 needs an actual voluntary 500 CR contribution to a named recipient and a successful-family clue based on the completed native debit receipt. Generic mission acceptance or a Lua completion flag cannot establish that the reserve received money. Existing station pledges require six credit/Food rows and refuse acceptance-event clue pins; the Original offer policy also conflicts with native pledge receipt cadence.

## Approved scope

At the actual Cedamar G01-S085 main orbital station, Donate exactly 500 CR to the Cedamar Orbital Relief Reserve through one credits/difficulty-one row. Cargo, berths, deposit, reward, deadline, refund and privileges are zero. Back transfers nothing. Decline consumes the offer without donating. A later hourly offer permits another fully charged contribution; the family's distinct PLUGH clue is retained once.

## Acceptance

Existing native pledge admission, debit, recipient history, completion and durable publication remain the authority. Only exact G13 gets a one-row declaration and the existing hourly cadence; other Original offers retain stable revisions and other pledge providers retain six credit/Food rows. Actual current native dock, source/digest, resources and sequence must match. Both clue award and journal validation require the exact completed native recipient receipt, 5000-native debit and zero settlement, not acceptance alone. Writer refusal conserves property, offer, receipt and clue.
