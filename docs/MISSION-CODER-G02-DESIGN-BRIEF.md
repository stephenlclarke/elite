## Required playable result

Implement both encrypted-data and sealed physical-package variants at each of difficulties 1, 2 and 3. The board must let the player identify and choose the difficulty and variant before confirmation. Acceptance issues one uniquely identified item, freezes the route and destination, and starts the deadline. The player flies to the destination's main orbital station and confirms its named contact handoff. Back changes nothing. The physical item uses exactly one real tonne of protected mission hold; encrypted data uses zero tonnes and still requires independently validated custody. Neither is a tradable market commodity, a narrative flag or another mission's interchangeable payload.

Freeze one non-origin destination reachable within three shortest-route jumps. Sort candidates by stable system ID. Use saved nonzero xorshift32 state, with zero mapped to `0x6D2B79F5`; perform the specified wrapping shifts in the specified order. Select by the recorded floor rule, not the current board's LCG draws. Retain route, origin, destination, variant, difficulty, generator revision/state and every item/offer/instance identity across saves, hull changes and content absence. No candidate or unsatisfied fit means no usable offer. Reopening or completing a job must not reroll it.

The frozen reward in credits is `base[difficulty - 1] + 300 * shortestRouteHops`, with bases 500, 750 and 1,000. Store integer decicredits, paying once at the actual destination handoff. There is no deposit, extra failure fee, bonus encounter or mission kill reward. Deadlines are acceptance-relative 172,800, 172,800 and 259,200 persistent project game-clock seconds. Confirmation exactly at the deadline succeeds; expiry occurs strictly after it. Menus and pause do not advance this deadline, while actual simulated flight, travel and services do. Do not weaken existing contracts' exclusive deadline semantics globally to implement this profile's inclusive boundary.

Failure or abandonment is terminal, releases only this job's outstanding custody and grants, and cannot pay success or resurrect the item. Physical item destruction must be driven by an actual supported native loss transition. Another mission's item, a forged event or a story counter cannot prove delivery or loss. If the current game lacks a required loss transition, identify the smallest source implementation needed; do not invent proof or silently drop that rule. Preserve unrelated cargo, equipment, bounties and mission state.
