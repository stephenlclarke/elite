# Station information and trading duplication

Screens 1, 2 and 7 repeat the same market table while requiring players to navigate between prices, buying and selling. Screens 8 and 9 repeat fitted laser information, obscuring their separate purposes.

Use one docked Market entry with visible Buy/Sell modes and explicit one-unit trade actions. Preserve flight views and existing cargo/economy/mission rules. Keep Commander Status concise and make Inventory the complete cargo and fittings reference. Legacy 1/2 keys should enter modes without adding redundant Back steps. Update controls, menus and contextual help together.

Acceptance includes pointer and keyboard transactions, no transaction on mode change, preserved selected commodity, read-only prices in flight, mission-cargo protection and readable compact layouts. See [implementation handoff](PR-station-market-ux.md).
