# M01: bounded vmmap diagnostic custody

## Problem

The retained whole Panel16 ASAN attempt exceeded the sampled RSS guard. The existing worker-only monitor cannot account for a sibling vmmap utility or controller/support overhead, and its ordinary process/cleanup helpers have waits outside the proposed diagnostic deadline. A wrapper around that monitor would therefore give incomplete resource and custody evidence.

The selected investigation is one memory-region summary for the exact verified prepared helper. It cannot establish allocation stacks, object ownership, the cause of RSS growth or a complete sanitizer qualification. Raw output may have no supported residency fields.

## Acceptance

- An exact closed diagnostic policy/session selects one unchanged complete 16-method ASAN cohort with one consumed launch. An ordinary session receives no diagnostic capability.
- The first worker-only RSS sample at or above 1.75 GiB can trigger one hash-pinned `/usr/bin/vmmap -summary -w <verified helper PID>` invocation. No replacement PID or retry is permitted.
- The sampled union counts controller, worker descendants, vmmap and explicitly owned support descendants once, including verified reparented identities. The 2.25 GiB aggregate guard and 2 GiB reserve remain fixed. Sampling is not an OS hard limit or physical-footprint measurement.
- Fair nonblocking output reads preserve raw bytes and units, with a combined 4 MiB vmmap cap. Capture has at most 10 seconds and cleanup has one window of at most 2 seconds, both inside the original cohort deadline.
- Uncertain cleanup retains the same live controller/executor/reservation leases in an inert phase. ACTIVE is persisted before the first spawn; UNKNOWN or unresolved ACTIVE blocks updated later admissions even after controller death. Post-phase identity/RSS is UNKNOWN, with only last-verified facts retained.
- Only an immutable independently reviewed resolution bound to the current record, nonce, attempt, controller, full owned identities, helper image binding, spawn intents and terminal evidence can release unresolved custody. Owner death, an ACK or deleting a lock is insufficient.
- Actual configured installed entrypoints and source bytes require an exact reviewed deployment proof and quiesced old paths. Controller-needing local/provisioning entrypoints are ineligible as listener jobs; actual NativeAuthority jobs use the read-only registry guard.
- Diagnostic evidence cannot become an ordinary PASS or sealed quality receipt, including when both commands exit zero. Unqualified residency is explicitly UNAVAILABLE.
