# feat(missions): add Lua missions, physical encounters and isolated trials

## Motivation and behavior

The owner requested a generic Lua mission engine on a new branch. The implementation adds authored scripted courier, real classic patrol humanoid passenger equipment-loan and generated contract and profiled skirmish and independent station-service and linked-patrol and exclusive assignment/follow-up examples with no mission-specific Swift, preserving the three bundled schema 1 campaigns.
