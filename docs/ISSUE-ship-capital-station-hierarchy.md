# Stations need a Capital parent and Station subtype

Verified FR054 states that stations are a subtype of capital ships. The reviewed catalogue instead allowed the Capital flag only on ship-kind vessels. Structural taxonomy must support Capital/Station without confusing that parent with the eight displayed functional types or changing existing docking, damage and purchase rules.

Keep the reviewed three capital vessels and seven eligible station/platform identities, with one collection entry per stable ID. Station subtype always requires actual docking and active-station indestructibility; the parent cannot impose destruction or count another completion. Preserve all source mechanics, people, cargo, appearance, archival specifications and the Liberator exception.
