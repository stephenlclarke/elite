# Ordinary NPC references across separate re-entry batches

The successful automated checkpoint `7ccb37b5f969bc84c43a3864b195fb93c379672f` did not test an important relationship case: A leaves the camera bubble; B later leaves and re-enters under a new physical identity; A then returns in a different admission batch. Original scene identities alone cannot recover A's intended B. A second source reviewer confirmed that defect.

The correction keeps original scene actor facts in `NPCState` and adds at most two explicitly typed bindings to each persisted snapshot: first aggressor and actor-targeted intention. `NPCLogicalIdentity` names the existing finite ledger entry by its first admission. It does not allocate IDs, change actor bits, or collect historical aliases. Capture establishes a binding only from known ordinary custody and preserves the same association while the target is unbound or terminal. A changed actual reference drops the old association. Protected and foreign facts retain their own namespaces.

Re-entry resolves against every current ledger binding, rather than only the current admission batch, and verifies an actual living ordinary ship. An unavailable, refused, dead or protected target cannot become a replacement ship. Its active intention is suspended; original desired intent remains in custody for a later legitimate re-entry. Player and station references retain their existing explicit visit rebinding. Immutable combat incidents and emission origins are not rewritten. Offscene movement, resources, finite cargo, high-water replay protection and system departure remain unchanged.

Nine actual runtime cases cover engage/flee asymmetric repeated re-entry, real save/load both while waiting and after the target returns, terminal death/cargo, player/station/foreign controls, actual population refusal followed by successful admission, changed actual reference, and protected replacement exclusion. Waiting is also exercised through a real tick to verify coasting without unintended steering. The first regression version reproduced 13 issues on archived pre-fix source. The final corrected source passed 42 focused methods across five suites. Independent source review confirmed the bounded binding correction and helper extraction resolved the identified defect.
