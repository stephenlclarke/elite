The timer alone never transfers people to the hold. Repeated partial/disabled EMP hits must not postpone the original recovery deadline. Original people and actual ejected/captured identities remain conserved across partial batches, recovery, reload and any later disable episode.

- Occupied pods remain protected against ordinary weapon/cutter damage. Collection requires actual fit, space, physical approach and a durable custody transition. Failed saving must leave the person and pod in their current post-flight state. Escaped occupants are not automatically reclassified as slaves.
- Apply drawing cues to white wireframe, coloured wireframe and shaded panels. White hull lines retain their drawing style; the separate threat/salvage cue carries red or orange.

- EMP implementation balance ratified by the Orchestrator: resolve N from the validated fitted energy component's canonical grade order (Basic 0, Intermediate 1, Advanced 2), representing accumulated sequential module stages. FR-017 now supersedes that terminal-disable policy; recovery and capture timing require reconciliation. Body hull integrity stays separate. Source implementation and validation remain queued.
