## Current design interpretations requiring implementation review

- Former FR-005 interpretation required capture after the escape window. FR-017/018 supersede that timing: actual salvage may capture remaining crew during the disabled window; randomly scheduled ejections determine who is still aboard. The timer alone never transfers people to the hold. Repeated partial/disabled EMP hits must not postpone the original recovery deadline. Original people and actual ejected/captured identities remain conserved across partial batches, recovery, reload and any later disable episode.
- FR-006 uses the existing salvage-targeting module and Fuel Scoops for automatic approach/collection. The existing rescue design reserves one tonne per sealed pod while retaining the occupant as a person. The user's wording confirms one pod as one salvage unit; one-tonne accounting is the existing project design and must remain distinct from five-tonne charter cabins.
- Occupied pods remain protected against ordinary weapon/cutter damage. Collection requires actual fit, space, physical approach and a durable custody transition. Failed saving must leave the person and pod in their current post-flight state. Escaped occupants are not automatically reclassified as slaves.
- Apply drawing cues to white wireframe, coloured wireframe and shaded panels. White hull lines retain their drawing style; the separate threat/salvage cue carries red or orange.

- EMP implementation balance ratified by the Orchestrator: resolve N from the validated fitted energy component's canonical grade order (Basic 0, Intermediate 1, Advanced 2), representing accumulated sequential module stages. Do not count physical fitting slots, stowed equipment, display labels, recharge ratios or upgrade receipts. Base pulse 32 native electrical units and base range 3000; apply factor 1 + 0.5N to both, yielding 32/48/64 pulse and 3000/4500/6000 reach. The base pulse and range factor are implementation balance choices; the user specified +50% power and increasing reach. Keep player cost 8, heat 18 and cooldown 1.5 seconds; target electrical reserve recharges through authoritative target time/resources until zero, while an actual disabled record remains disabled under the former design. FR-017 now supersedes that terminal-disable policy; recovery and capture timing require reconciliation. Body hull integrity stays separate. Source implementation and validation remain queued.

- FR-017/018 implementation defaults: each original surviving crew member gets a persisted ejection delay sampled once per actual disable episode from 0.5 to 45 seconds. Recovery occurs at original disable time plus 30 seconds if the hull remains live and crew remain aboard; partial capture, selection, failed saving or a superficial cut cannot indefinitely postpone it. Actual capture requires time strictly before that deadline; recovery wins at the deadline. These timing bounds and tie rules are project balance choices. Original ordinals remain stable even when ejection and partial captures interleave; later disable episodes schedule survivors only.
- Escape with held native people records an explicit original-ID custody loss by escape/abandonment in the same successful equipment-consumption/cargo-clear/dock transaction, distinguished from death. It does not claim rescue or invent a new world capsule/hull provider. Failed transactions preserve existing custody; historical source discharges never reverse.

- FR-019 queued balance default: splash radius is one third of resolved EMP reach (1000/1500/2000 native units by fitted energy grade), with linear attenuation max(0, 1 - distance/radius). The actual primary mesh contact determines the world-space pulse origin; primary is hit once at full power. Player cost remains provisionally 8 native energy units, independently of 32/48/64 pulse output. These numbers and linear falloff are project choices, not user-specified percentages or a physical energy-conservation claim. Per-victim eligibility, actual electrical drain and legal attribution are required, with whole-pulse preflight of every zero-power custody transition before committing cost or effects.

- FR-019 self-collateral interpretation: the player is eligible for the same target-centred splash when within range. This supersedes the preliminary self-exclusion proposal and provides an actual production path for FR-016 disabled-player emergency controls without inventing NPC EMP distribution. Player electrical reserve must be distinct from fatal hull energy; actual zero blocks engines/ordinary weapons while preserving real owned energy-bomb/escape-pod routes, and recovery uses the original 30-second episode. No NPC custody record is created for the player and no fake test-only disable flag proves acceptance. This area-policy choice was stated to the user; implementation is queued.

- FR-020 balance and compatibility: proposed shop chain is existing Intermediate 1500 CR, Advanced 4500 CR and Elite 13500 CR, with Advanced required before Elite; legacy naval reward acquisition remains supported. Elite extends canonical energy grade order to 3, so EMP becomes 80 pulse / 7500 reach / 2500 splash radius under the existing additive formula. Elite recharge is 4x, with 5.2x future terminal upgrade headroom; stock capacity ratio remains 1. Existing grade templates/origins, actual stats and applied absolute-effect receipts must remain unchanged. Current ComponentProgression validates stored absolute effects rather than replaying requested upgrades, so a speculative receipt-rewrite migration is not required absent other source evidence. Old capped components and stale quotes still require regression coverage. Prices, tech levels and terminal headroom are project balance choices.

- FR-021 queued fitting defaults: eight dedicated alien-module bays shared across the four types on playable ships, expressed as one documented project fitting policy rather than unsupported historical per-hull claims. Each real fitted module adds 10% of the resolved normal base for its own axis; effects stack additively and fold once after ordinary component grades/upgrades. Capacity and recharge remain independent. Shield modules affect front and rear values for that axis. Fitting/unfitting requires current PLUGH main-dock proof and an atomic owned-item transaction; storing or changing a hull must preserve items without refilling resources. These bay/bonus defaults are project choices. Actual salvaged provenance is required; old artefacts and XYZzy alone do not mint alien modules.
