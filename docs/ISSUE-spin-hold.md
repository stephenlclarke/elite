# Prototype deliberate touchpad spin and roll-speed hold

Matching a rotating docking entrance needs sustained roll without continuously dragging or holding a key. The requested prototype uses a circle to choose direction and start a predictable slow roll, then one finger to slow, two to hold and three to speed up. It needs explicit mode/HUD feedback, smooth stopping/reversal, rebased aiming and safe contact cancellation.
