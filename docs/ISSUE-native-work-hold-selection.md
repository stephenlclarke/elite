# Physical hold selection and extraction permission

Work targeting previously refused protected or claimed large parents before any hold could engage. Ordinary live capital vessels received only ship tracking, and rocks or large hulls without a specialty module disappeared from selection entirely. These decisions mixed safe physical positioning with permission to extract or collect property.

## Required behavior

A real fitted specialty module permits the existing physical hold controller for a catalogue capital vessel or a rock/large salvage parent. Native geometry, clearance, speed, acceleration, turning and energy constraints still govern movement. Active stations keep docking behavior. Without the module, ordinary tracking and its existing visibility limits remain available, with no work approach, automatic tool selection or hold.

Hold eligibility grants no cutting, crew capture, cargo transfer or collection. All existing native extraction, ownership, protection, mission, occupied-pod, aperture, capacity and publication checks remain authoritative.
