# FR055: implement The Guiding Light against an ordinary native provider

## Motivation

The approved project-authored mission needs to observe the existing year-round Santa convoy without spawning, equipping, healing, moving, reserving or replacing it. Generic courier deadlines, scripted NPCs and Boolean docking do not provide the required contact, route calibration, original computer provenance, original crew care or native convoy legality. An offer that advertises those recovery branches must remain unavailable until every required native operation exists.

Authority is the approved `elite-santa-mission-design-20261017.json` (SHA-256 `d7b8b3ad004f0aa67e9f34fa56af23018ba9fd3568c37bd0e774a01d0e502468`), the Root implementation ownership artifact, and the implementation contract plus its three clarified invariants. Stable logical provider/member identities survive scene rebinding; repeated requests and reviews retain only bounded state; betrayal voids every unpaid crew reward even when earned earlier.

## Required behavior

Carry one identified protected 1t relay from genuine Crimbo main-station acceptance to genuine Atisen main-station delivery. Obtain the original manifest through an explicit request and five continuous admitted physical seconds of real contact. Full dispatch requires three actual forward-route checkpoints and qualified escort time. Explicit urgent dispatch and authentic original core or crew-care recovery provide exclusive alternatives. Original crew care earns one optional commendation; selling original people or an unlawful responsible-player attack voids unpaid rewards. Returning the held relay closes custody for zero base reward. Genuine cargo loss closes failure only after any innocent earned crew payment can be settled; cash overflow never invents held cargo.
