# Visible celestial flight space and solar fuel scooping

## Problem

The local flight scene contains ships and a station but no reachable planet or sun. Cabin temperature never changes, and Fuel Scoops only collect cargo, leaving the expected solar fuel/overheating gameplay absent.

## Requested behaviour

Render actual moving celestial objects through every cockpit view; make their location discoverable. Fuel Scoops collect fuel near the sun while cruising toward it. Solar proximity heats the cabin, distance permits cooling, and overheating or a surface impact destroys the ship. Preserve fuel policies, missions and existing docked save restoration.

## Acceptance

Deterministic proximity, facing, equipment, rate, capacity, heat, cooling, death and world-motion tests; perspective/clipping/depth/camera rendering checks; native flight/scoop/retreat evidence; integrated CI and renderer-generated media refresh.
