# Historical ship-hull audit contact sheets

These seven unchanged PNGs belong to the 14 October 2026 [167-hull audit](../SHIP-HULL-AUDIT.json). That audit records the actual `Display.shipPortrait` GIF source, sampled renderer frames, silhouette comparison method and limitations. The contact sheets preserve its historical visual evidence and repair the audit's previously missing relative references.

They show the hulls at that audit checkpoint. Later hull, renderer and catalogue changes supersede those previews; these images do not establish current 171-hull fidelity, texture support, current fingerprints or release acceptance. Current showcase media must be regenerated through the game's renderer and checked separately. No PNG bytes were edited during release composition.

Rendered third-party models retain the terms in their individual definitions and [project notices](../../NOTICE.md). Inclusion as historical evidence does not relicense those designs.
