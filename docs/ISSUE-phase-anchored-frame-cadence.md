# Frame deadline drift after late wakes

Matched candidate profiles 497 and 498 on `b0e4108991293e6a04a85f8640a913563e2e6b4f` measured median draw intervals near 17.7 ms, about 56.45 reciprocal FPS, in compact 1024x1080 and fullscreen 3600x2260 views. Processing p95 was about 7.6/7.4 ms and draw p95 about 3.8/4.3 ms. Compact's application profile completed successfully with a separately recorded post-run harness error; it was not retried. These measurements do not establish physical scanout timing or attribute all latency to scheduling.

At the baseline, the maintained loop calculated every deadline from that iteration's actual frame start and slept with unspecified tolerance. A late wake therefore became the next time origin and permanently shifted subsequent deadlines. Overdue work yielded and could immediately start another iteration rather than waiting for a future cadence slot.

The integrated phase-anchored cadence uses explicit small sleep tolerance, skips missed slots, re-anchors on effective rate changes and preserves the dynamic 1...60 FPS cap, real elapsed simulation delta, MainActor isolation and cancellation. Matched after profiles 504/505 on development source `a71af8c7` measured compact/fullscreen median intervals of 16.655625/16.658250 ms against baseline 17.721416/17.705959 ms. The [checked-in measurement report](NATIVE-FRAME-PACING.md) records p95 values, protocol, exact source/executable identities and evidence hashes. The coherent after batch also includes geometry/save changes; no unique cadence attribution, physical scanout or every-gameplay 60 FPS claim is made. Mission stack repair, full CI and release gates remain outstanding.
