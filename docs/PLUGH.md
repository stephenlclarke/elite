# PLUGH secret ports and galaxy browsing

The Galactic Map has eight galaxy buttons and [ / ] browsing. Search, crosshair, selected system data and per-galaxy selection follow the viewed galaxy. O returns to the current galaxy and system. Docked charts 4/5 include full selected planet data and its deterministic generated globe; Fn-Up/Fn-Down scroll long facts and clues. The separate 6 data page is retained in flight. Browsing changes no commander location, fuel, cargo, market, mission or active route. H or N in the current galaxy explicitly selects a travel/route destination; a foreign galaxy preview cannot initiate travel or planning. Ordinary G galactic-hyperdrive travel remains separate and consumes its drive.

Every original seeded galaxy still has exactly the same 256 systems in the original order. One additional deterministic PLUGH system uses identity 256 in each galaxy, with separate galaxy identity and saved discovery bit. It starts hidden from charts, search, neighbour selection, route planning and auto-trade destinations. U opens station rumours while docked at a main station; a trader directs the player to that galaxy's original system 7. Asking there discovers only that galaxy's PLUGH and saves the discovery. Its distinct coordinates lie near that station within ordinary jump reach. Discovery does not teleport, grant cash or reveal other galaxies. Actual countdown, fuel/tank/nav limits, arrival, station and docking apply. A galactic jump from PLUGH arrives at the next galaxy's original system 7 without discovering its secret port.

PLUGH stocks all 17 commodities at 63 units and offers every supported paid or reward-only equipment item regardless of ordinary technology/reward gating. Fitting, tier, missile-rack, tank, cash and cargo limits still apply. There is no hull purchase. Naval/Capital hardware bought here does not grant combat rank or mission completion.

The documented native balancing default is a 25% discount, rounded once to the nearest 0.1 CR with a positive minimum for positive transactions. Buy and sell use the same local unit price, preventing an instant same-station profit. Fuel rounds once on the complete refill bill. Market, shop, navigation estimates and auto-trade share the policy; cash and stock debit normally, including XYZZY's finite balance.

Outside PLUGH, the original reward restrictions and ordinary market calculations remain unchanged. Older saves default to no discoveries; invalid masks or undiscovered secret destinations are rejected.
