# Ship data scope

All ship research remains in scope, including Frontier-derived ship capabilities, the 155 numeric refit profiles and the docked shipyard design. Mission exclusions do not remove ships, equipment capabilities, alien records, source mappings or provenance. Preserve the complete ship data when updating research catalogues or implementation filters.

The independently maintained local `docs/research/implementation-scope.md` controls mission implementation eligibility. Its v4 station-handoff policy replaces retained planetside stages with explicit station transactions while preserving separately excluded Frontier and Operations catalogues; it does not remove ship data. It does not mean every researched campaign is already playable. The current native Mission Gallery describes the two implemented BBC Executive naval missions: Constrictor Hunt and Thargoid Plans.

Shipyard comparison, compatible equipment transfer and persistent surplus storage remain research requirements for future shipyard implementation. This cockpit update does not replace or delete their data.
