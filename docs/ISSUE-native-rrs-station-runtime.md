# Native RRS docking and service authority

The retained Rescue Stations 1.5.4 research describes 364 waystations and eight separate headquarters. Geometry and a logical station ledger alone do not create native scene actors, prove a ship entered a real port, or authorize the documents mission board and original main-station hand-in.

Add checked population, real hull and swept-port admission, same-station launch, retained condition and destruction, and bounded save joins. Keep the original main station outside the 372-record RRS ledger. Native entry or validated save restoration must provide current service authority; saved Boolean flags, Lua facts and generic station roles cannot manufacture it.

Acceptance requires actual actor population, safe entry and exit, original-main compatibility, stale and forged service refusal, complete persistence and failed-write rollback, normal-stack tests, renderer evidence and the maintained CI gates. The documents mission itself remains a separate consumer. See [implementation and validation status](PR-native-rrs-station-runtime.md).
