# Fly with a held trackpad yoke and finger-count throttle

The game only accepts keyboard flight controls. The agreed trackpad mapping is two fingers to steer at constant speed, one to slow down and three to speed up. Removing all fingers must centre the yoke and preserve cruising speed. Holding off centre must keep turning without repeated swipes.
