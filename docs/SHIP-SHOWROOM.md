# Ship showroom and exchanges

Press **6** while docked. Browse with arrows, select with **Return**, press Return again to review a trade, change the active Ships/Specs pane with **Tab**, and go back one level with **Escape**. Click the visible actions for comparison, source notes, rotation, sorting or trade. Search accepts name, class, edition or stable SH ID; arrows choose a result, Return opens it, and Command-Delete or Clear filter resets the query. Source profile arrows are enabled only when multiple profiles exist.

**Sort: A-Z / Power** changes the order without losing your filter or selected ship. Power uses the native standard capability index documented in [ship definitions](SHIP-FILES.md). Both narrow and wide layouts keep the selection in view.

All 171 records have shaded rotating models. The 162 flyable adaptations can be bought at a main station; nine station/environment objects remain display-only. The prominent **Switch ship - review trade** action shows price, trade-in, net payment/credit and resulting cash. It opens a quote showing the selected hull price, 75% trade-in allowance, net cost or credit, resulting balance and fitting conflicts. Left/Right chooses **Back** or **Confirm trade**, and Return selects. Pointer controls do the same. Back is the default. Save and Save As are queued after earlier player input, so an immediate save includes the confirmed exchange or XYZZY refit. Cargo, fuel, paid equipment and mission property are never silently discarded. Resolve a displayed conflict before trading or select a compatible hull. Subsequent exchanges work from any owned ship.

**Currently flying** identifies the owned hull; the two previews are labelled Current ship and Selected ship. The comparison colour key sits directly beside the comparison headings, separate from ship descriptions and tradeoffs.

The primary comparison uses the game's actual mechanics and your carried equipment on both hulls. **BETTER / WORSE / SAME** appear in words and colours; `?` means unavailable. Fuel tanks, jump caps and unlimited supply/cargo are distinct. The selected comparison's handling, speed, acceleration, pools, regeneration, capacity and fitting limits are the values used after purchase. **Source notes** keeps original edition profiles and provenance separate from that player comparison.

Ship definitions and geometry live in individual TOMLs in [Resources/Ships](../Sources/EliteCore/Resources/Ships), loaded identically by SwiftPM and the packaged app. See [every ship's rotating GIF and specifications](SHIP-CATALOGUE.md), [design/source attribution](SHIP-PROVENANCE.md) and [schema, calibration, economics and regeneration](SHIP-FILES.md). Original designs are documented in the appendix; the app stays uncluttered. Imported geometry retains its original terms and notices. The approved detailed Cobra icon remains unchanged.

XYZZY activation restores the canonical Cobra Mk III with maximum hardware. **C: Switch to Cobra Mk III (free refit)** repeats this without buying a hull, granting more cash or running auto-trade again. Cargo, mission payloads, campaign progress and unique ships remain intact. The maximum refit replaces the current fitted weapons and hardware; if transferred cargo exceeds the Cobra hold, unlimited cargo is enabled to preserve it. Option 2 refits the currently owned hull.

Development hull exchanges retain portable equipment that has no compatible slot as owned storage, including rounds beyond the smaller hull's pylons. Compatible fitted items stay installed; already stored spares are not automatically fitted when moving back to a larger hull. Hull-bound items leave with the sold hull. Cargo, fuel, mission property, people and cabin-capacity refusals remain separate. This source change is not yet claimed for a published release.
