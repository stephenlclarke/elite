# Ship showroom and exchanges

Press **6** while docked. Browse with arrows, select with **Return**, change the active Ships/Specs pane with **Tab**, and go back one level with **Escape**. Click the visible actions for comparison, source notes, rotation, sorting or trade. Search accepts name, class, edition or stable SH ID; arrows choose a result, Return opens it, and Command-Delete or Clear filter resets the query. Source profile arrows are enabled only when multiple profiles exist.

**Sort: A-Z / Power** changes the order without losing your filter or selected ship. Power uses the native standard capability index documented in [ship definitions](SHIP-FILES.md). Both narrow and wide layouts keep the selection in view.

All 167 records have shaded rotating models. The 158 flyable adaptations can be bought at a main station; nine station/environment objects remain display-only. **Trade ship** opens a quote showing the selected hull price, 75% trade-in allowance, net cost or credit, resulting balance and fitting conflicts. Left/Right chooses **Back** or **Confirm trade**, and Return selects. Pointer controls do the same. Back is the default. Cargo, fuel, paid equipment and mission property are never silently discarded. Resolve a displayed conflict before trading or select a compatible hull. Subsequent exchanges work from any owned ship.

The primary comparison uses the game's actual mechanics and your carried equipment on both hulls. **BETTER / WORSE / SAME** appear in words and colours; `?` means unavailable. Fuel tanks, jump caps and unlimited supply/cargo are distinct. The selected comparison's handling, speed, acceleration, pools, regeneration, capacity and fitting limits are the values used after purchase. **Source notes** keeps original edition profiles and provenance separate from that player comparison.

Ship definitions and geometry live in individual TOMLs in [Resources/Ships](../Sources/EliteCore/Resources/Ships), loaded identically by SwiftPM and the packaged app. See [every ship's rotating GIF and specifications](SHIP-CATALOGUE.md), [design/source attribution](SHIP-PROVENANCE.md) and [schema, calibration, economics and regeneration](SHIP-FILES.md). Original designs are documented in the appendix; the app stays uncluttered. Imported geometry retains its original terms and notices. The approved detailed Cobra icon remains unchanged.
