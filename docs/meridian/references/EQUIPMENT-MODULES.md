## Applying upgrades

A compatible recovered upgrade changes the selected physical component and is consumed once. Its effect is a finite percentage of that component's immutable original statistics, added to current statistics. Two distinct compatible +10% items therefore add 20% of the origin for that axis; they do not multiply the already improved value. The same item cannot be replayed.

The component retains its identity, origin, accumulated improvements, features and receipts. Meeting every required threshold promotes its grade automatically, retaining excess progress; one application may cross multiple grades. An improvement to another axis can remain useful without completing the next grade. A new stock purchase is a separate owned item; a weaker purchase is stored rather than replacing stronger fitted statistics. One component occupies each shield/navigation/energy slot, while applied upgrades accumulate on that component.

New improvements stop at family terminal envelopes: shield capacity ratios 11.96 and recharge 1.3; navigation reach 27.3 LY with spool/fuel-cost ratios no lower than 0.7; energy capacity ratio 1.3 and recharge 5.2. Valid grandfathered statistics are preserved rather than clipped to these bounds. Fitting, capacity and custody rules still apply. A successful fit/promotion preserves current resource fractions and does not refill energy, shields, fuel or missiles.

## Alien attachments

Recovered alien attachments are separate physical items with four independent types: energy capacity, energy recharge, shield capacity and shield recharge. A stock attachment contributes +10% to its type. Contributions of the same type add together and multiply the resolved normal component statistics once; two stock capacity attachments give +20%, independently of recharge attachments. These attachments have no gameplay count limit and are separate from the ordinary component terminal envelopes.

Inventory offers Fit, Store and matching recovered upgrades only at an authenticated PLUGH main-station dock. Refitting costs 0 CR, grants no charge, and clamps charge to the resulting capacity. Applying a compatible recovered upgrade permanently increases that physical attachment's contribution; storing and refitting it does not reset the improvement or permit replaying its source. Current ship summaries retain fitted counts and stacked percentages for each type. The native alien fitting source exists; complete acceptance and publication remain separately tracked.
