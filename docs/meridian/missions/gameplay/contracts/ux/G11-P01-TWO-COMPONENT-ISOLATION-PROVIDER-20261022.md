Station hull damage cannot substitute. Losses persist; no payment or clue on failure. The G11.P02 settlement retains the accepted `760 + 105L + 80H` CR quote; P01 neither computes nor pays it.

**Existing substrate:** the Enron induction utility implements three separate repairs and crew-owned kits. Its native surface query and before/after-step measurement pattern are reusable techniques; its identities, repair flags, three-module state and receipts are not G11 evidence. `NativeUtilityState.swift` supplies W04 installation/reception types, not two external isolation operations. Generic `EquipmentMissionIssueContract.swift` supplies issue/return ownership machinery; it does not establish that an isolation programmer has actually been issued.

**Concrete decisions introduced here:** the original source, location, geometry, two feeder circuits, finite quantities and thresholds below. These fill previously unspecified implementation inputs; they are not claims about already accepted numbers or existing gameplay. No new general resource-network framework is required.

## Resource transition: isolation must change the producer

Each feeder owns **10,000 utility energy units** initially, plus an initially empty **10,000-unit receiving load**. These are bounded project-authored native utility accounts, not player energy, station shields, cash, stock or a promised global power grid. For each feeder independently:

`remainingSupply + deliveredLoad = 10000`, with both values finite and in `[0,10000]`.

The native producer transfers `min(dt * 1 unit/second, remainingSupply, loadHeadroom)` while its actual contact is **connected**, during attained local active simulation with both module bindings valid. It debits supply and credits that same module's load once. Its canonical contact state gates this real transfer; a separate mission `isolated` boolean is forbidden. Pausing, absence of an attained local simulation interval and unloaded source actors do not advance this locally frozen source. Re-entry resumes the same accounts without off-scene catch-up or refilling.

The operation is non-destructive opening of that feeder's internal disconnect. After its measured work and successful atomic publication, change the producer's contact from connected to **latched open**. Subsequent transfer attempts through that feeder must transfer zero despite positive supply and receiving headroom. Opening primary does not change reserve, and vice versa. There is no player close/reset operation. Destruction, EMP disablement, exhausted supply, a full load, culling or a missing actor is not an open contact and never produces isolation credit. Finite exhaustion is a real unavailable condition, not a reason to reset accounts.

This source is deliberately limited to its two native circuits. It does not disable the real station or change Enron, Tithe or other campaign infrastructure. A native reader must observe the actual producer's contact and account delta, not recompute success from a journal label, dialogue, elapsed timer or cached model value.

## Work, custody and independent verification

1. **Authenticate.** Require the actual one-tonne original programmer in current K06 loan custody: issue owner/resource/pack digest, issue operation, serial item UUID, owning commander, carrier hull and inventory revision must agree. Loan status remains issued, not returned/released/lost. The same issuer identity is retained for P02 return. A matching name, ordinary commodity, another loan or decoded boolean cannot authorize work. P01 neither issues nor consumes the programmer, changes its mass, grants tools or creates a second custody ledger.
2. **Bind and review.** Tab may select either authorized live module without granting targeting hardware or automatic flight. Use the existing contextual maintenance pattern: Return reviews; fresh Return confirms arming; Q/Escape interrupts. Display that Return is a programmer operation in this context. W/tool changes, manual combat fire, target/view changes, help/pause, lost custody, stale bindings, EMP disablement or hull change disarm it. Other targets retain normal weapon controls. G11 does not add a key or masquerade as a laser/cutter.
3. **Measure actual work.** The chosen envelope is the applicable six-metre external-face policy: first unobstructed forward mesh hit on this original module; outward face; surface range **50–150 m**, relative linear speed **at most 1 m/s**, centre aim **at most 2 degrees**, and **20 qualifying native seconds per module**. Both endpoints of each attained step must meet it with identical owner/tool/actor/visit/carrier/revision authority; accept intervals only in `(0,0.100001]` seconds. Retain partial work, but count neither gaps nor synthetic catch-up. A fresh pre-opening native interval must also show this feeder actually delivering positive energy with supply/headroom available; zero flow from starvation cannot qualify. Reuse the Enron measurement technique, not its hardcoded role query or receipt type.
4. **Open, then observe separately.** At 20 seconds and a fresh qualifying interval, propose the latched-open contact and a work-commit record together. Opening alone is not verified isolation. A later native resource-producer step, followed by an independently constructed live surface/meter observation, must show the same open contact, positive remaining supply, positive headroom and zero actual transfer. Accumulate **one qualifying native second after opening**, using the same bounded endpoint intervals. Reconfirm current identity/geometry/tool custody throughout. A new obstruction or loss stops verification; it cannot manufacture a reading. Both modules need their own work record and later observation; one receipt cannot stand for two.
5. **Publish evidence only.** Emit a typed immutable isolation receipt only after that later observation is published. Record source/module/role, authorization and issue operation, programmer serial, original logical actor and current native binding, geometry hash, work history digest, contact-transition operation/revision, before/after meter accounts, observation times/interval count and causal sequence. The live endpoint/meter constructors are private to the actual producer/query files. There is no Lua command accepting arbitrary “isolated” facts. Return eligibility for P02 is derived from two distinct current-source verified receipts plus the real programmer obligation; P01 cannot complete a mission or issue money/clues.

No extra fee, battery charge, repair kit, consumable ammunition or deadline is introduced. The existing held programmer is the tool; actual finite feeder energy is conserved independently. K06.P01 supplies the missing programmer definition/issue binding and one-tonne reservation through the existing ownership machinery. Until that native proof exists, admission/arming fails closed; no mock entitlement is a production fallback.

## Failure, publication and current-format state

Keep attained resource transfers and measured partial time with the physical simulation; a failed save must never refund energy already transferred. As with the existing native utility boundary, stage the final contact change/work record in the existing publication candidate. A refused writer leaves the contact connected and no committed work receipt, while preserving attained time/accounts for the next flush. Final opening requires another fresh qualifying interval. Post-opening verification may be retried without repeating the opening or recreating energy; only its successfully published observation becomes evidence.

Q, pause, load, cull/rebind and tool or hull changes clear transient review/arm authority. Preserve balances, latched contacts, partial histories and published observations. An actor destroyed before verification, destroyed source station required by the authorization, lost/returned programmer or invalidated permission blocks new work and cannot count as isolation. Record real loss; retain earlier genuine openings without paying or awarding anything. Any existing isolated effect persists after cancellation, escape or parent failure. K06 owns programmer loss/return; P02 owns job failure/settlement. Do not synthesize new tools, bodies, state transitions or receipts on restore.

One provider-only ScriptInstance, `l337.isolation-work-source/native-source`, owns `savedIsolationWork`. Retain it independently of the G11 board's parent terminal status. Fixed cardinalities are two modules and two energy-account pairs, at most 256 work segments per module, at most one opening and one verified-isolation receipt per module. Store the parent/authorization/issuer/tool binding, finite source reservation, first admissions, current logical bindings, accounts, contacts, partial histories and terminal loss. Do not persist a live review nonce, arm, publisher, query object or runtime epoch as reusable authority.

The new codec must strictly validate the leaf's shape, unique fixed roles/IDs, source/account conservation, bounded finite times/counts, monotonic non-overlapping work and later observation, acknowledged sequence bounds, exact geometry/owned-encounter joins and current K06 obligation. A missing field is not a default connected circuit; removed evidence is not a reset. Reject receipt substitution, duplicate roles, unknown fields, old-schema field smuggling, future times and erased losses. Use a fresh current normal/Alien format pair allocated by the central save owner at implementation; do not claim an unused number while C08/G4 work is active or add migrations. That owner must propagate recognition of the new envelope pair through existing feature validators, including mixed C08/G4 saves; this does not authorize the G11 author to rewrite their codec or state. Cold restore clears transient authority and requires fresh endpoints before further progress; already published valid isolation receipts remain valid.
