## Harm, interruption, receiving and retries

This is explicitly **non-firing** from acceptance until disengagement. Any actual player weapon discharge fails the attempt, including an actual practice-beacon hit; existing finite energy/ammunition/effects still occur. A press that emits nothing does not invent a discharge. ECM is allowed under its normal fitting/energy rules. An accepted player-attributed ordinary hit or actual collision also fails it while preserving every ordinary consequence. Reuse G4's real discharge/accepted-hit/committed-impact observers; do not infer harm from a key press or suppress the normal kernel. Defer beacon cleanup until that kernel has returned, as in T02. Merely being attacked cannot award an evasion receipt; unsafe observations earn nothing and normal survival rules apply.

Escape while this active flight exercise owns the practice context explicitly leaves **only T03**, with zero award and safe provider retirement; Escape in help or a review first retains normal close/back behaviour. Q only releases guidance. Before disengagement, leaving the native visit, docking, actual ship loss or a hull change interrupts the unfinished attempt without awarding success. Existing missions, property, law and save identity are not reset. An explicit Cancel Practice board action is available too; no timeout abandons it automatically.

On measured disengagement, seal its receipt, safely end this provider and mark **ready for Avenor reception**. This retirement is neither a kill nor terminal/clue. Retain original provider/phase proof; do not require the beacons still to be live at the station. An ended valid provider is historical evidence, not a runtime practice permission. The exercise's no-fire/no-harm scope ends at this sealed break-off; later ordinary actions retain ordinary law but cannot rewrite already completed manoeuvre evidence. Actual owner/carrier continuity still applies at receiving.

Use `l337.training/defensive-flight`, version `1.0.0`, with its real authored pack digest and native first-success binding. Successful receiving requires a genuine later Avenor dock sequence/time than acceptance and disengagement; a born-berthed/reloaded acceptance stamp cannot substitute. Atomically commit the normal zero-payment completion receipt and **`defensive-flight-family-trace`**; repeat Confirm/load adds neither reward nor clue. A refused writer leaves the live terminal/clue unchanged, with attained native progress retained for retry. Generic mission finish, timer or credits commands cannot bypass the marked native family.

Failure/abandonment preserves its reason and grants nothing. A fresh explicit acceptance may allocate a new T03 practice attempt through the existing provider/capacity policy, with new native identities; it cannot relabel ordinary traffic or reset the old receipt history. Do not automatically restart, mint resources or erase other missions. Starting T03 never hijacks an active T02 range; completed T02/T01/T04 source is unchanged.
