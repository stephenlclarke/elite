# The Shared Pulse

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

HOLT: More replies make this worse. Listen first.

A Larvaal maintenance organ and a human beacon exchange incompatible acknowledgement pulses. Each automatically repeats faster when it receives the other's reply, creating a hazardous feedback loop. The commander establishes independent listening references, restores a consenting repair organ and verifies either separated timing windows or a shared bounded handshake.

## Offer and reward

At Elaith in Galaxy 3, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 8,600 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The pulse before the reply.
