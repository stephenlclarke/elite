# Original mission families

Private proposal: fifteen repeatable objective families and four training exercises. Each has original dialogue, requirements, money rules, evidence and artwork. All required families remain available after postponed training or a failed job.

[Artwork gallery](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/elite/tree/6552b68f7a146ceb79b4376d33cc8afd3f987399) / [Current design TOML](original-families.toml)

## Shared contract

Use the normal station board, arrows, Confirm and Esc. Preview actual fitted hold/berths, consent, route, quoted money and any explicit clock before accepting. At most three service reservations may be active across both original and Wayfarer boards, using one capacity ledger. Offer identities remain stable until a real stock or reservation event.

Each first verified family terminal grants its own permanent zero-mass clue, atomically with its actual settlement. Donations qualify through their real recipient receipt; training through actual completed practice. Incidental payments, failure and cancellation qualify for none. Later jobs retain the same clue.

Unquoted deadlines do not exist. Armed flight-simulation budgets freeze on pause and restore the same remainder. Real cargo, living people, unique actors, consent and attributed kills remain authoritative. No free hull, legal reset, source-stock clone or real kill credit from simulated practice.
