# A Garden in Transit

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

VEIL: A seed catalogue is not a cargo price list. Record the conditions that let it remain alive.

An orbital seed archive is moving because its lease is ending. Two destinations want the same climate collection, while the growers insist the living archive must not be divided blindly. The commander compares real receiving environments, accompanies the seed tender and obtains either a distributed custody charter or a shared quarantine berth.

## Offer and reward

At Kurenash in Galaxy 7, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 6,100 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The dormant season.

### 2. Zorgadek: survey

### 3. Kurenash: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Custodian Tomas Veil.

Custodian Tomas Veil: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Vektaor: field-work

Test two isolated seed cradles with the loaned reference sampler.

ARLEN: Test capsules return to their registered owner. A copied inventory is not a new living collection.

### 5. Drelor: independent-witness

Escort the identified seed tender to its agreed quarantine berth.

VEIL: Its custodians remain aboard until actual reception confirms their collection.

### 6. Kurenash: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Custodian Tomas Veil: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Create distributed seed custody
- Establish a shared orbital archive

### 7. Vektaor: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Custodian Tomas Veil: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify two compatible climate receipts and their consented custody split.

VEIL: Split only the collection the growers agreed to share.

Gossip: A Garden in Transit: Create distributed seed custody.

**plan-b:** Verify the common quarantine lock and joint access charter.

ARLEN: A shared berth needs enforceable access for both settlements.

Gossip: A Garden in Transit: Establish a shared orbital archive.

### 8. Kurenash: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Custodian Tomas Veil: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Kurenash: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Custodian Tomas Veil: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 6100 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Custodian Tomas Veil: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. The archive contains a circular season boundary that encloses no named climate or world. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Completion clue

The dormant season: The archive contains a circular season boundary that encloses no named climate or world.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
