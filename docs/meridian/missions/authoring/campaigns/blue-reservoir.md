# The Blue Reservoir

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

LERR: Clean intake water says nothing about what the circulation loop keeps from returning.

An orbital water commons is losing dissolved oxygen while every intake remains clean. A new circulation pump has trapped part of the living filter behind a one-way lease boundary. The commander measures actual flow, opens consented bypasses and chooses distributed filter cradles or a jointly governed reservoir.

## Offer and reward

At Bornolom in Galaxy 4, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,300 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The dry current.

### 4. Bornalan: field-work

Open two measured bypass locks without releasing the living filter.

VEIL: The colony remains contained. A destroyed tank is neither a repair nor a rescued organism.

### 5. Dorbogan: independent-witness

Escort the water-commons inspection tender to the safe laboratory berth.

LERR: Its operators must observe the actual flow before accepting it.

### 6. Bornolom: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Engineer Cato Lerr: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Distribute the living filter among independent cradles
- Create a jointly supervised reservoir

### 7. Bornalan: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Engineer Cato Lerr: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify both containment receipts and balanced flow tests.

Gossip: The Blue Reservoir: Distribute the living filter among independent cradles.

**plan-b:** Verify common circulation limits and independent shutdown authority.

LERR: Shared water needs a physical limit as well as a charter.

Gossip: The Blue Reservoir: Create a jointly supervised reservoir.

### 8. Bornolom: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Engineer Cato Lerr: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Bornolom: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Engineer Cato Lerr: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7300 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Engineer Cato Lerr: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. The oldest flow recorder traces an inward curve through a channel that contains no water. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.
