# Open Measure

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

LERR: A certificate should describe a fit, not manufacture a monopoly.

Three independent workshops cannot exchange replacement parts because their calibration certificates use incompatible reference loads. A supplier profits by treating each disagreement as a new proprietary component. The commander measures actual tolerances, tests shared reference fittings and escorts a neutral technician before choosing public measurement standards or a witnessed interoperable service.

## Offer and reward

At Valtor in Galaxy 2, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,900 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The reference without a load.

### 8. Valtor: return-and-audit
