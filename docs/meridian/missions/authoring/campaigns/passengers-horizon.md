# The Passenger's Horizon

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

SOREL: A fine horizon is useless if a passenger cannot safely reach it.

A passenger cooperative sells a scenic orbital journey that excludes travellers whose cabin needs do not match its standard form. The commander tests real access and safe viewing positions, escorts a volunteer assessment tender and helps the travellers establish either adaptable shared cabins or individually guaranteed journey slots. Consent and actual fitted capacity determine the journey plan.

## Offer and reward

At Aelara in Galaxy 6, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,500 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The horizon without a world.

### 2. Izaeth: survey

### 3. Aelara: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Captain Orin Sorel.

Captain Orin Sorel: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Evanieri: field-work

Test two external boarding and viewing interfaces with the loaned access console.

DAIN: Use an empty test cradle first. People do not have to prove that an unsafe fitting hurts.

### 5. Noaliena: independent-witness

Escort the consenting assessment tender to its independently verified berth.

SOREL: Their actual reception is the evidence; a passenger count alone is not.

### 6. Aelara: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Captain Orin Sorel: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Provide adaptable shared journey cabins
- Guarantee individual accessible journey slots

### 7. Evanieri: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Captain Orin Sorel: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify compatible fittings and each traveller's independent consent.

DAIN: Shared access cannot erase an individual's needs.

Gossip: The Passenger's Horizon: Provide adaptable shared journey cabins.

**plan-b:** Verify reserved capacity and the safe receiving itinerary.

SOREL: A reservation includes every receiving point, not just departure.

Gossip: The Passenger's Horizon: Guarantee individual accessible journey slots.

### 8. Aelara: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Captain Orin Sorel: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Aelara: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Captain Orin Sorel: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7500 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Captain Orin Sorel: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. A viewing reference encloses an inward-curved horizon that belongs to none of the surveyed planets. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Completion clue

The horizon without a world: A viewing reference encloses an inward-curved horizon that belongs to none of the surveyed planets.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
