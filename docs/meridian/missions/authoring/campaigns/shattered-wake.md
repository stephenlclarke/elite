# The Shattered Wake

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

A station can stop a weapon volley. Can it survive an entire broken orbit?

An abandoned orbital mass driver above Aermiori has restarted during an automated survey. Its spoil stream intersects a fractured moonlet, sending a long train of large bodies and dense gravel towards Spindle Anchorage. The station can discharge the firepower of an ordinary ship, but successive heavy impacts can exhaust its shields before they recover. The commander measures the approaching train, shuts down the driver and helps build either a tug-deflection array or a fragment-catching screen. The commander can visit neighbouring systems with a verified warning and recruit their available tugs, interception patrols and evacuation craft. During the passage, the commander redirects marked threats and clears a route for rescue craft. Afterwards, survivors and the flight records are taken to Loalaila, where an inquiry determines what happened and how to protect the next orbital settlement.

## Offer and reward

At Loalaila in Galaxy 6, after visiting three systems, speak to Controller Edda Rell. No kill-count gate or prior campaign. The loaned coupler needs one tonne of hold; standard flight, targeting and weapons are sufficient.

Payment: 1,800 CR, once on successful completion. The completed relief and inquiry earns the base 1,800 CR. Add 6,000 CR if Spindle Anchorage survives, or 3,000 CR if the station is lost but all registered evacuees reach Shelter Dock; no bonus for other station-loss outcomes. All outcomes require the later relief and inquiry, not merely waiting for an explosion. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The curve before the fall.

## Briefing

RELL: Spindle Anchorage can absorb a ship's weapons all day. These are moving mountains and a wake of gravel. We need to change their paths before they share the same few seconds against the shields. Survey the outer watchers at Aermiori. We have time to prepare; I will not arm the operation until you confirm readiness.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Loalaila: accept

Accept the orbital emergency commission at Loalaila.

RELL: The shelter crews are already standing by. We need a reliable picture of the whole debris train, not a heroic shot at the biggest rock. Take the survey first; the station's outer orbit is still clear.

### 2. Aermiori: survey-train

Scan three outer watchers and compare the projected impact bands at Aermiori.

ORR: The lead body is not the whole threat. Behind it are several heavy pieces and a narrow gravel stream. Their paths meet the anchorage while the shields would still be recovering from the first impacts. These signed scans also let you warn Iraeth, Reilieli and Siamaeth; their emergency contacts may release additional ships before you arm the defence.

### 3. Aermiori: collect-coupler

Dock at Spindle Anchorage and collect a one-tonne impulse coupler.

PENN: One coupler will link either our tug anchors or the fragment screen. The hold needs one free tonne. We will keep it safely here until you can take it. The deployment hardware itself is already in orbit.

### 4. Aermiori: silence-driver

Shut down the abandoned mass driver and record the moonlet fracture boundary.

ORR: The driver was following an obsolete survey test embedded in its local controller. Its feed is now isolated. The material already in flight cannot be recalled; the fracture record tells us which surfaces will split under fire.

### 5. Aermiori: choose-defence

Choose tug deflection or a fragment-catching screen through the emergency radio contact.

PENN: Tug deflection shifts the heavy bodies, leaving the gravel for interception. The screen catches gravel, leaving the heavy bodies for us. Neither handles everything. Choose the work your ship can support; we will show the remaining threats before you arm the passage.

Choices:

- Deflect the heavy bodies with tug anchors
- Catch the gravel with a fragment screen

### 6. Aermiori: deploy-defence

Install the selected three-node defence and confirm readiness to begin the passage.

RELL: The survey, shelter craft and defence nodes are ready. Confirm starts the fifteen-minute passage. The clock keeps running while the game is active, even if you dock or leave; Pause stops it. You can still cancel before arming.

**tug-deflection:** Synchronise the three tug anchors, then install the coupler and confirm readiness.

PENN: The tug anchors can move the marked heavy bodies sideways. Watch the gravel bands that remain on course.

Gossip: The emergency crews used a coordinated tug-deflection array.

**fragment-screen:** Align the three fragment catchers, then install the coupler and confirm readiness.

PENN: The screen can catch the marked gravel bands. Its capacity is finite; the heavy bodies remain your priority.

Gossip: The emergency crews deployed a fragment-catching screen.

### 7. Aermiori: hold-the-approach

Help redirect the marked threats until the debris passage resolves; keep the evacuation corridor clear.

RELL: The passage is active. The HUD shows time to impact, combined load, shield reserve and station integrity. Fire that shatters a body near the station can leave every fragment on course. Help the marked tugs or clear threats whose projected paths you can actually change.

### 8. Aermiori: escort-rescue

Escort the registered relief tender to Shelter Dock with the survivors and incident records it recovered.

PENN: Our relief tender is sweeping the shelter corridor. It carries the people we actually recovered and the anchored event recorder. Bring it into Shelter Dock. If no one survived a section, the records still matter; the missing people are not replaced by new names.

### 9. Loalaila: deliver-relief-record

Dock at Loalaila and submit the relief manifest and recovered event record.

RELL: We will account for everyone by the rescue manifest, not by the number of launches on a scanner. The surviving station needs repairs; a lost station needs a permanent exclusion marker and support for its displaced crews.

### 10. Loalaila: complete-inquiry

Compare the saved trajectory record, driver shutdown and defence telemetry with Jalen Orr.

ORR: The debris kept the momentum it had before we arrived. The useful actions changed where it would strike. I have separated those records from a second curved trace that was present before the driver restarted. It does not belong to the spoil stream.

### 11. Loalaila: close-case

Confirm completion of the relief operation and inquiry; collect the base fee and the recorded outcome bonus.

RELL: The relief record and inquiry are complete. Your base fee is 1,800 CR. The audited station and evacuation outcome determines the additional payment. The real station status and casualties remain on the regional record.

ORR: This curved trace predates the debris. It is not evidence that anyone deliberately caused the disaster. I have copied it to your journal; PLUGH's AI may be able to place it beside your other traces.

**station-saved:** RELL: Spindle Anchorage survived the passage. The 6,000 CR protection bonus brings your total to 7,800 CR. Its crews still have a home and a berth.

**station-evacuated:** RELL: The anchorage is gone, but every registered evacuee is safe at Shelter Dock. The 3,000 CR evacuation bonus brings your total to 4,800 CR. Its wreck will remain on the chart.

**station-lost:** RELL: The anchorage was lost and the manifest records casualties. Your completed recovery and inquiry earns the 1,800 CR base fee. There is no protection or full-evacuation bonus. The inquiry will protect future settlements.

## Completion clue

The curve before the fall: Before the mass driver restarted, the orbital recorder traced a loop of inward-facing points around a quiet centre. The curve passes through the same absence described by the other collected traces.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use normal targeting and scans, contextual Confirm actions and the shared help controls. Esc cancels a dialog without choosing an operation or arming the timer.

The impulse coupler uses one tonne between collection and completed defence deployment. It is loaned without a fee and installed into the final node in one atomic transfer. No salvage, record or clue requires spare hold.

After the watcher survey, optional contacts at Iraeth, Reilieli and Siamaeth can dispatch available tugs, patrol ships and rescue haulers. The mission log shows routes, warning receipts, offers, departures and arrival estimates. Warn any subset before arming; late visits remain possible but do not pause an active passage.

Preparation has no deadline. Before arming, display the operation, all targets, 15-minute passage clock, shield reserve, impact load, structural integrity, rescue manifest and safe breakup range. Confirm at the final deployment starts the advertised clock; cancelling does not.

After arming, the active simulation clock continues during docking, radio dialogs and interstellar departure. Explicit game pause, automatic background pause and system sleep freeze it. Save/load resumes the same elapsed passage, impact queue and rescue manifest; it does not replay impacts or reset the station.

The station can be genuinely destroyed by the accumulated asteroid impacts. Rescue and the inquiry use Shelter Dock and Loalaila, so loss of Spindle Anchorage cannot remove the remaining objectives.

A lost rescue tender can be replaced by a new crew and hull. Persist survivors already safe at Shelter Dock. Do not recreate dead evacuees, clone a tender's passenger manifest or award an arrival for a culled ship.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Choose tug-deflection or fragment-screen once. The selected deploy-defence variant changes the mitigation available during the same debris passage. Afterwards, all station outcomes continue through rescue, relief and inquiry. The choice alone never guarantees survival.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.

## Station and debris rules

Ordinary single-ship weapon fire cannot penetrate the station shields or reduce structural integrity. Ship fire never counts as an asteroid impact.

Authoritative collisions by physical asteroids and fragments draw from a recharging shield reserve. Unabsorbed load damages structural integrity. Repeated impacts can destroy a station.

Independent normalised gameplay loads; physical impact-energy conversion and station-size budgets require runtime balancing.

A collision receipt combines encounter and collider identity; process it once. Splitting conserves the parent's mass, momentum and total kinetic-energy budget. Invalidate the parent and give children new identities; never count the parent and its children as separate intact masses.

## Optional warning tour

Show an optional Warn neighbouring systems objective with three labelled destinations, current offers and plotted routes. Any order is allowed; the player may warn some, all or none. Return-to-Aermiori is always visible. Use current normal navigation and contact controls.

Require confirmation at the registered local main-station emergency contact. Reserve each system's available response once, persist its decision and dispatch receipt, and show the actual offer before confirmation. A busy reserve may offer fewer ships or decline; revisiting or reloading cannot reroll availability or create another group.

Create the actual offered NPC ships under one dispatch identity. They depart from their home system, traverse their plotted route and assemble at Aermiori's outer watch with their remaining arrival estimate shown. They cannot affect the incident before arrival. Persist travel, arrival, loss and replacement-free group membership.

Tugs physically deflect eligible heavy bodies; fighters intercept eligible fragments; rescue haulers carry real registered evacuees to Shelter Dock. They use the same collision, fragmentation and passenger rules. Helpful fire cannot simply remove rock mass or damage the station.

The tour fits the untimed preparation phase before the player confirms final readiness. After arming, a late warning trip uses the same active passage clock; display the estimated help arrival against remaining time and warn that it may be too late. An offer or departure is not an arrival.

- **Iraeth:** Iraeth Mining Cooperative; up to 2 tug ships. MINING COOPERATIVE: Your watcher record confirms the strike corridor. We will check our active tow crews. The response screen shows the tugs we can release and their travel time; confirm to send the warning and reserve those crews.
- **Reilieli:** Reilieli Orbital Patrol; up to 3 interceptor ships. ORBITAL PATROL: A verified fragment train is an emergency across this cluster. We can divert the available patrol shown here to the outer watch. They will intercept small threats once they arrive, rather than firing at the station.
- **Siamaeth:** Siamaeth Civil Relief; up to 2 evacuation-hauler ships. CIVIL RELIEF: Our haulers can help with people and supplies, not the heavy rocks. Confirm the offered ships to send them to Shelter Dock. Every passenger they carry will appear on the same rescue manifest.

Warnings are optional and may be delivered in any order. An offered or departing fleet is not yet helping at the anchorage. The HUD and mission log distinguish travelling, arrived and lost ships.
