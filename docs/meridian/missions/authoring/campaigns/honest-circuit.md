# The Honest Circuit

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

VENN: A fast time means little if the clock and the route disagree.

A new flight certification circuit rewards apparent speed without accounting for unsafe close passes or offloaded fuel. The commander independently measures its beacons, completes a real receiving test and chooses an efficiency standard or a safety-margin standard. The player completes the circuit with their current commander and ship.

## Offer and reward

At Lonomon in Galaxy 4, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,000 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The tick outside the circuit.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Lonomon: accept

Accept The Honest Circuit at Lonomon.

Captain Elara Venn: A new flight certification circuit rewards apparent speed without accounting for unsafe close passes or offloaded fuel. Review the complete survey, loan and receiving requirements before confirming.

### 2. Mobarum: survey

Scan three circuit beacons and compare their independent timebases.

VENN: A fast time means little if the clock and the route disagree.

### 3. Lonomon: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Captain Elara Venn.

Captain Elara Venn: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Lobagom: field-work

Complete two published corridor tests with the reference timing standard.

RENN: Every limit is visible before the run. Pausing preserves the simulation clock.

### 5. Balmabol: independent-witness

Escort the consenting calibration tender through the same certified corridor.

VENN: The standard must work for a working tender as well as a fast player ship.

### 6. Lonomon: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Captain Elara Venn: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Certify measured route efficiency
- Certify a minimum safety margin

### 7. Lobagom: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Captain Elara Venn: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify fuel accounting and actual receiving time.

RENN: Efficiency includes the fuel actually used, not a removed manifest.

Gossip: The Honest Circuit: Certify measured route efficiency.

**plan-b:** Verify the closest-pass envelope and emergency abort test.

VENN: A safe abort is a valid measured result, not a punishment.

Gossip: The Honest Circuit: Certify a minimum safety margin.

### 8. Lonomon: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Captain Elara Venn: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Lonomon: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Captain Elara Venn: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7000 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Captain Elara Venn: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. A reference timebase records a curved phase mark between two valid circuit ticks. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Completion clue

The tick outside the circuit: A reference timebase records a curved phase mark between two valid circuit ticks.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
