# Mutual Shelter

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

TOREN: A promise of shelter is useful only if a ship can actually fit.

A repair mutual has insured more emergency shelter than its docks can physically provide. A legal salvage claim on a disabled hauler exposes the shortfall. The commander measures real capacity, protects the hauler's crew and helps members choose a pooled reserve or independently bonded shelter.

## Offer and reward

At Ashoes in Galaxy 5, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,100 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The ownerless reserve.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Ashoes: accept

Accept Mutual Shelter at Ashoes.

Nia Toren: A repair mutual has insured more emergency shelter than its docks can physically provide. Review the complete survey, loan and receiving requirements before confirming.

### 2. Voron: survey

Scan three emergency-dock capacity registers and their physical berth limits.

TOREN: A promise of shelter is useful only if a ship can actually fit.

### 3. Ashoes: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Nia Toren.

Nia Toren: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Ranues: field-work

Reserve two real emergency berths against the independently measured capacity ledger.

ARLEN: Reserve the space once. Selling a second receipt does not create another berth.

### 5. Peloen: independent-witness

Escort the disabled cooperative hauler to its verified repair berth.

ARLEN: Its crew keeps its title while the repairs and hearing take place.

### 6. Ashoes: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Nia Toren: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Pool member-funded emergency capacity
- Bond independent shelter obligations

### 7. Ranues: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Nia Toren: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify the real reserve ledger and member withdrawal limits.

TOREN: Publish what is covered before accepting anyone's contribution.

Gossip: Mutual Shelter: Pool member-funded emergency capacity.

**plan-b:** Verify each provider's berth bond and an actual receiving drill.

ARLEN: A bond needs a berth behind it, not another bond.

Gossip: Mutual Shelter: Bond independent shelter obligations.

### 8. Ashoes: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Nia Toren: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Ashoes: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Nia Toren: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7100 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Nia Toren: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. An early shelter ledger reserves an inward-shaped volume that has never been assigned a hull or creditor. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
