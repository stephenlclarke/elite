# Harbour Without Orders

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

RELL: The harbour is intact. Its doors are closed by three mutually invalid signatures.

A port's arrival allocator has lost its signing authority after a disputed maintenance transfer. No one is attacking the station, but every berth refuses every arriving ship. The commander establishes who can authorise safe approaches, brings in an independent traffic witness and tests either distributed berth authority or a temporary public harbour office.

## Offer and reward

At Gallodor in Galaxy 4, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,600 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The unsigned berth.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Gallodor: accept

Accept Harbour Without Orders at Gallodor.

Controller Edda Rell: A port's arrival allocator has lost its signing authority after a disputed maintenance transfer. Review the complete survey, loan and receiving requirements before confirming.

### 2. Ombolom: survey

Scan the three external arrival allocators and record their refusal certificates.

RELL: The harbour is intact. Its doors are closed by three mutually invalid signatures.

### 3. Gallodor: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Controller Edda Rell.

Controller Edda Rell: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Emdor: field-work

Install temporary safety limits at the two external approach relays.

VOSS: These limits prevent conflicting approaches while the operators decide who may sign.

### 5. Emron: independent-witness

Escort the neutral traffic witness to the emergency receiving berth.

RELL: Reception must identify our witness and the berth used. A ship disappearing near the station proves nothing.

### 6. Gallodor: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Controller Edda Rell: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Return authority to independent berth operators
- Create a time-limited public harbour office

### 7. Emdor: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Controller Edda Rell: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify two operator keys and a conflict-free simultaneous approach test.

VOSS: Each berth may sign its own approach, within the common exclusion envelope.

Gossip: Harbour Without Orders: Return authority to independent berth operators.

**plan-b:** Verify the public key register and its automatic authority expiry.

RELL: Emergency authority must come with a visible end.

Gossip: Harbour Without Orders: Create a time-limited public harbour office.

### 8. Gallodor: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Controller Edda Rell: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Gallodor: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Controller Edda Rell: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7600 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Controller Edda Rell: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. The allocator's oldest signed audit describes an empty central berth enclosed by a curved approach boundary. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Completion clue

The unsigned berth: The allocator's oldest signed audit describes an empty central berth enclosed by a curved approach boundary.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
