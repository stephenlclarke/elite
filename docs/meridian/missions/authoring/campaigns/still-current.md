# The Still Current

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

The harvesters are idle. The charge is still moving.

A mothballed orbital energy lattice near Karrakor reports no output, yet three independent utility meters show its charge reservoir growing. A maintenance tender is trapped outside an over-heated service corridor. Physicist Anja Kest suspects that conductive dust has bridged the collector isolation gaps, turning the dormant structure into an unintended capacitor. Salvage Chief Ruan Pell finds that uncrewed reclamation machinery is repeatedly reconnecting the bridges because it mistakes a stored commissioning instruction for a current work order. The commander must establish the real energy balance, rescue the technicians, position insulated baffles and revoke the obsolete work authority. The settlements then choose between a supervised community buffer and dismantling the collectors for reuse. Every step relies on measured charge, physical isolation and real receiving records.

## Offer and reward

At Valrator in Galaxy 2, after one completed ordinary trade, answer a utility survey notice. No combat-rank gate. The recovered charge cell and later baffle programmer each need one tonne of free hold, never both at once.

Payment: 8,400 CR, once on successful completion. The selected community buffer or completed material recovery remains in the local utility record. No personal ship curse, permanent equipment damage, legal-status reset or free hull. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The resting arc.

## Briefing

KEST: These three meters cannot all be wrong in the same way. We stopped buying power from the lattice years ago. Find where the charge goes before someone reconnects a corridor full of people.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Valrator: accept

Accept the dormant-lattice survey at Valrator.

KEST: We need measured values, not a rumour about haunted machinery. You can leave the survey at any safe berth; your ship will remain yours to control.

### 2. Karrakor: survey-current

Scan three utility meters around the Karrakor lattice.

The supply meter reads zero. The reservoir and return meters still record a growing potential difference. Their independent calibration signatures agree.

### 3. Karrakor: recover-cell

Recover the one-tonne insulated charge cell from the unmanned maintenance sled.

PELL: The sled is empty. Use the insulated service latch and preserve the cell; blowing up a random machine tells us nothing about the trapped charge.

### 4. Valrator: analyse-charge

Exchange the charge cell for Kest's baffle programmer at Valrator.

KEST: The cell stores charge across dust bridges. The reclamation controller keeps rebuilding them from an obsolete instruction. I will keep the cell and loan you this programmer in the same hold slot.

### 5. Karrakor: rescue-technicians

Escort the identified maintenance tender through the safe corridor to its reception berth.

PELL: The technicians are aboard this tender. Keep the clear corridor open until reception records their arrival. Do not enter the charged lattice to shorten the route.

### 6. Mortor: map-feedlines

Scan the three external feedlines at Mortor and identify the obsolete work authority.

All three collectors are connected to the same archived work order. Revoke that authority before any branch can be safe; no living crew has consented to its renewal.

### 7. Karrakor: place-baffles

Position three insulated baffles at the marked Karrakor work points.

The baffles split the work volume into visible safe windows. Watch normal cabin heat and withdraw between operations if needed. The programmer records each physical lock.

### 8. Valrator: choose-lattice

Choose the lattice's future with the local utility operators.

KEST: A supervised buffer can deliver stored power under local limits. Or we can empty the reservoir and reclaim the collectors. Both require the old work authority to be removed.

Choices:

- Commission a bounded community energy buffer
- Recover the stored energy and dismantle the collectors

### 9. Karrakor: isolate-collectors

Revoke the obsolete work order and apply the chosen collector settlement.

Only the marked collector modules are involved. Their signed work-order revocation and measured isolation are separate from the nearby station.

**community-buffer:** Revoke the archived work authority and test the locally bounded collector quota.

An order outside the consented quota is rejected. No reclaimed machine can reconnect a dust bridge by replaying the old job.

Gossip: The lattice collectors now obey locally tested utility limits.

**material-recovery:** Disable the archived work controller and verify all collector bus links are isolated.

The unmanned controller is isolated. Its receiving log proves the collectors cannot reconnect to the reservoir.

Gossip: The dormant collectors are isolated for controlled material recovery.

### 10. Mortor: settle-reservoir

Account for the full stored charge and complete the selected settlement at Mortor.

The displayed balance must reach an accounted state. Deliveries require a real receiving meter; safe discharge and recovered storage retain their own receipts.

**community-buffer:** Deliver 300 measured charge units to the local utility and prove the remaining 600 stay inside the bounded buffer.

The utility has received its 300 units. The buffer holds 600 and refuses an over-capacity order.

Gossip: A measured community reserve now supports the utility without autonomous reconnection.

**material-recovery:** Transfer all remaining charge to registered recovery storage and confirm the collectors' material delivery.

Recovery storage and the receiving yard account for the remaining energy and dismantled components. Nothing vanished into an inventory adjustment.

Gossip: The lattice is retired; its stored energy and materials have reached the local operators.

### 11. Valrator: return-programmer

Return the baffle programmer and verify the final energy balance at Valrator.

KEST: The meter identities, local receipts and reservoir balance agree. The work tool is returned; your readings remain with the utility record.

### 12. Valrator: close-case

Confirm the completed lattice settlement and collect the survey fee.

PELL: The technicians reached reception and the lattice is under its agreed control. Your survey fee is 8,400 CR. The recorded outcome remains part of this system.

KEST: This arc predates the lattice. It cannot be part of the dust bridge or its work order. I have copied the zero-charge phase record to your journal; PLUGH's AI may recognise it.

**community-buffer:** KEST: The buffer is supervised by its users. It cannot turn an old commissioning instruction into a claim on new power.

**material-recovery:** PELL: The collectors are safely retired and their material is received. The empty corridor can be used again.

## Completion clue

The resting arc: The lattice's oldest meter records an inward arc before any collector was built. The measured charge is zero, but the phase signature still encloses a quiet centre.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use normal targeting, scan and contextual Confirm. The survey is voluntary; Esc closes a briefing without accepting, placing a device or choosing a settlement. No additional keyboard binding.

The charge-cell exchange loans the one-tonne baffle programmer atomically. Keep that tool through both collector operations and return it at Valrator before final payment.

Lattice field exposure adds cabin heat only while the ship is physically inside a clearly marked hazard volume. The normal cabin-temperature HUD and an audible warning remain available. Leaving the volume permits normal cooling; the mission never hides instruments, fires weapons, buys cargo or redirects a jump.

The array starts with 900 measured charge units and no active generation source after isolation. Account for every routed, safely discharged or recovered unit. Display the remaining charge and the actual safe work windows. Moving a baffle is not a fresh energy source.

The maintenance tender and its technicians are real identified actors. Only its arrival at the designated safe berth completes rescue. If lost, preserve casualties and arrange an actual replacement retrieval before continuing; never resurrect a named individual or clone cargo.

Automated reclamation machines are unmanned service equipment, not station hulls. A patrol kill or destroyed station cannot supply isolation evidence. Scavengers may interfere, but combat is never the energy-balance or completion predicate.

Normal pause stops simulation heat and operations. Current-format saving preserves energy allocations, baffle positions, disabled work authority, tool custody and branch evidence. There is no hidden global deadline.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Choose community-buffer or material-recovery once at choose-lattice. Revoke obsolete work authority in both routes. Commit matching proofs at the collectors and reservoir; mixed evidence cannot complete the campaign. Both routes pay 8,400 CR and award the same clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.

## Energy and thermal rules

[Detailed lattice contract](STILL-CURRENT-CONTRACT.md)

Measured charge = remaining + physically delivered + safely discharged + recovered storage. No negative quantity, repeated transfer or unverified receiving endpoint.

A named geometric work volume supplies bounded simulation-time heat through the ordinary thermal system. It is never tied to a random GUI event or commander identity.

A current save restores the same meter identities, actual balances and recorded baffle placements. Culled scene meshes do not change logical control or charge.
