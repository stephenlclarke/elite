## The final mystery

The Unwritten Route receives all 64 source clues, then reveals its ordered PLUGH trail. Its own final reconstruction grants The convergent heart epilogue record; it cannot be a prerequisite for its own discovery. The selected Medusa geometry, legitimate player-kill requirement, later PLUGH reconstruction and paid purchase remain unchanged.

## Availability and saving

Training and all required base families remain available to an experienced commander who postponed them. Failed attempts may be retried with real replacement stock or actors; their failures do not award a clue. Preserve current-format evidence, custody, actual losses and once-only settlement/clue receipts. Missing optional paid packs never block the base mystery. No previous save/content format or external campaign alias is supported.
