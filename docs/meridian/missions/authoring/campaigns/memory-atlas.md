# The Memory Atlas

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

ORR: A beautiful memory is not a measured approach vector.

A navigation publisher has combined voluntary travel diaries with instrument surveys and now presents memories as exact route measurements. The commander revisits three disputed orbital landmarks, separates consented testimony from physical data and chooses an annotated public atlas or a custodian-held memory archive.

## Offer and reward

At Reris in Galaxy 1, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,200 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The place nobody remembers.

## Stages and dialogue

### 1. Reris: accept

Accept The Memory Atlas at Reris.

Agent Mara Vale: A navigation publisher has combined voluntary travel diaries with instrument surveys and now presents memories as exact route measurements. Review the complete survey, loan and receiving requirements before confirming.

### 2. Nedasen: survey

Record three orbital landmarks independently of the travel diaries.

ORR: A beautiful memory is not a measured approach vector.

### 3. Reris: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Agent Mara Vale.

Agent Mara Vale: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Cemalis: field-work

Compare two landmark instruments against the loaned reference watcher.

VALE: Keep consent attached to testimony and uncertainty attached to measurements.

### 5. Ledanor: independent-witness

Escort the consenting diary custodian to the archive receiving berth.

VALE: The custodian decides which memories become public.

### 6. Reris: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Agent Mara Vale: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Publish a separated evidence-and-memory atlas
- Keep the diaries with their custodians

### 7. Cemalis: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Agent Mara Vale: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify independent measurement labels and each contributor's consent.

ORR: Let readers see where testimony ends and the instrument begins.

Gossip: The Memory Atlas: Publish a separated evidence-and-memory atlas.

**plan-b:** Verify restricted custody and publish only measured hazard corrections.

VALE: Privacy does not require keeping an unsafe chart in circulation.

Gossip: The Memory Atlas: Keep the diaries with their custodians.

### 8. Reris: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Agent Mara Vale: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Reris: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Agent Mara Vale: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7200 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Agent Mara Vale: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. Three unrelated diaries contain the same inward curve around a place none of their authors visited. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

## Completion clue

The place nobody remembers: Three unrelated diaries contain the same inward curve around a place none of their authors visited.

Every successful terminal grants this same permanent, zero-mass clue. All commanders collect the same 64 clues before decoding the trail at PLUGH.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
