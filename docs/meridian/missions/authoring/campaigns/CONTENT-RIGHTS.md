# Original mission content rights

The private L337 proposal contains 39 original campaigns, 21 repeatable mission families and four tutorials. Every successful campaign and each family's first success contribute one of the 64 required PLUGH clues.

The proposed stories, dialogue, data, native artwork recipes and image masters are independently authored for this proposal. Text, data and recipes are designated for the project MIT licence as applicable; image-generation records retain the prompts and unchanged outputs. The proposed content contains no external mission package, script, dialogue or artwork.

[Authorship record](ORIGINALITY.md) / [Artwork provenance](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/elite/tree/6552b68f7a146ceb79b4376d33cc8afd3f987399) / [Complete original catalogue](ALL-MISSIONS.md).

Historical source identities, recorded licences and the unchanged installed-source fingerprints are kept in a separate audit record. They are excluded from the checked content bundle and are not runtime resources or aliases. No source notice has been removed or changed.

This is a private content proposal. Implementation, gameplay validation and whole-release rights review remain outstanding. Independent authorship does not itself guarantee non-infringement.
