# The Long Return

Private L337 proposal. Original story and design data; not installed or playable in the current app.

SOREL: Each crew remembers a different clock. Ask who actually received people, not who filed first.

A dispersed maintenance crew can return home only if three competing carriers honour the same transfer agreement. Each carrier has a plausible but incomplete account of an earlier missed rendezvous. The commander reconstructs the physical custody chain, escorts a voluntary reunion tender and negotiates a shared handoff or direct return.

## Offer and reward

At Zankakor in Galaxy 2, after one ordinary trade. No imported score gate or source campaign prerequisite. The reference loan needs one tonne of free hold.

Payment: 7,800 CR, once on successful completion. The selected verified settlement persists in local records. No free hull, unrelated legal reset or artificial combat kills. A permanent clue for The Unwritten Route: The passenger never booked.

### 2. Darvan: survey

### 3. Zankakor: collect-reference

Collect the identified one-tonne reference instrument from Coordinator Sera Dain.

Coordinator Sera Dain: This instrument is a loan. I retain it until your hold has room, and the final audit returns this exact serial identity.

### 4. Halridor: field-work

Test two neutral custody handoffs using the loaned receiver witness.

DAIN: These tests contain no passengers. Never make a real crew the bait for a missed rendezvous.

### 5. Elaris: independent-witness

Escort the named reunion tender to its consented receiving berth.

SOREL: Keep the surviving crew list honest. A substitute hull does not restore someone lost.

### 6. Zankakor: choose-settlement

Choose the verified settlement with the consenting operators.

Coordinator Sera Dain: Both choices preserve the independent evidence. Choose who controls the future work; closing this dialog selects neither.

Choices:

- Use a witnessed shared return handoff
- Give the reunion tender a direct return corridor

### 7. Halridor: apply-settlement

Verify both independent proofs for the selected settlement.

Coordinator Sera Dain: The selected plan must work in the physical system. A statement or unrelated kill cannot supply either proof.

**plan-a:** Verify both carrier signatures and the live crew reception.

DAIN: Shared responsibility must still identify who received each person.

Gossip: The Long Return: Use a witnessed shared return handoff.

**plan-b:** Verify the corridor reservation and home-berth arrival authority.

SOREL: A direct journey is safe only if its last berth is real.

Gossip: The Long Return: Give the reunion tender a direct return corridor.

### 8. Zankakor: return-and-audit

Return the reference instrument and confirm all physical receiving and settlement receipts.

Coordinator Sera Dain: The loan is returned. Check the actual observations, living witness reception and both selected settlement proofs before closing the case.

### 9. Zankakor: close-case

Confirm successful completion, the recorded settlement and the once-only fee.

Coordinator Sera Dain: The independent audit is complete. Your fee is 7800 CR. The operators retain the chosen settlement and their original records.

Coordinator Sera Dain: The audit has left one anomaly that belongs to neither side. The oldest rendezvous record reserves a curved empty space with no passenger, mass or destination. It is preserved in your journal for the AI at PLUGH.

The passenger never booked: The oldest rendezvous record reserves a curved empty space with no passenger, mass or destination.

## Interaction and recovery

Use the normal target and scan controls, contextual Confirm actions and mission contacts. Esc closes a dialog without accepting a transfer or choosing a branch. The mission log shows the current destination, target and next action. No special mission keyboard binding is introduced.

Use the existing mission log, targeting, scan, contextual Confirm and Esc. Preview all obligations before accepting; cancellation changes nothing.

Keep the reference instrument in reserved custody through the field work, witness reception and selected settlement. Returning it is part of the independent terminal audit.

Preserve actual crew and entity identities. Culling is not death or arrival. A lost unnamed tender may be replaced with a new consenting crew after real rescue handling; never resurrect a named casualty. A lost reference invalidates its old serial before a real replacement loan.

All objectives are space flight or space-station handoffs. Stations remain protected from single-ship weapons. No source world overrides, equipment budgets, hidden reputation grind or external storyline progress is required.

Persist each completed target. Recreate an uncompleted objective after load or entity loss; duplicate scans and incidental removal cannot complete it. Escort destruction returns to the escort briefing with a replacement launch and no reward.

Store the selected plan-a or plan-b identity once, separately from its authored display label. Apply only that branch at apply-settlement; both distinct physical and receiving proofs are required. Both legitimate endings share one final reward and one source clue.

Every campaign starts unaccepted with its current identity and zero progress. Persist current-format stage evidence, choices, mission-item custody, reward and clue receipts. Resume these records on load; replayed events cannot duplicate rewards. No previous campaign or save format is imported.
