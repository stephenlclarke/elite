# Original L337 mission authorship

The proposal contains 39 original campaigns, 21 repeatable families and four tutorials. These provide the same 64 distinct first-success clues for every new commander. Every legitimate campaign ending qualifies; repeated completions cannot substitute for another source. The Unwritten Route grants its own epilogue after reconstruction and is never required to unlock itself.

The stories, dialogue, characters, factions, locations, rewards, objectives, branches and current-format completion receipts are authored for L337. Signed Passage and The Guiding Light each have a full nine-stage campaign. The Still Current has twelve stages, Wayfarer Exchange fourteen, The Borrowed Voice ten, The Shattered Wake eleven and The Tithe of Worlds fourteen. These are complete campaign proposals with original requirements and outcomes.

Maintained sources include `generate.py`, the campaign `.authoring.json` files, `remaining-briefs.json`, `project-campaign-briefs.json`, `original-families.authoring.json` and `author-service-campaigns.py`. Source-research bookkeeping is not exported as player content. Generated definitions contain only current L337 identities and formats.

All campaigns and objective families have new briefing or stage artwork, dialogue and clues. The original recurring cast has new dialogue and nineteen portraits. Original equipment recipes, faction symbols, branch scenes and clue glyphs accompany the 59 original image masters. Exact prompts and generated outputs are recorded in the artwork source records. Approved hull geometry and the selected Medusa remain unchanged.

The authored text, data and native-art recipes are designated for the project MIT licence as applicable. Historical source identities, licence notices and exclusion evidence are retained in a separate audit record and excluded from the checked content bundle. This does not remove or rewrite any source notice.

The proposal is private. Runtime implementation, gameplay validation and whole-release rights review remain future work. The installed game, orchestrator and coder chats have not been changed by this proposal update. Independent authorship is not a guarantee of non-infringement.
