## Discovery and fabrication

No SH171 random, faction, wanted-list, ambient, scripted, misjump or XYZZY encounter before every required clue is decoded and all four trail objectives are completed in order. The only normal spawn is this quest's unique Medusa at the Ashor outer cradle. Redact its catalogue identity and design until the legitimate encounter.

Require actual destruction of the quest-instance Medusa credited to the player by the authoritative combat system, including the player's weapons. Targeting, culling, escape, NPC kills and generic SH171 kills do not grant the record. An uncredited loss permits a replacement encounter at the same unlocked checkpoint.

Automatically preserve a zero-mass reconstruction record on the valid kill. Full rendering becomes visible, but every purchase/fabrication path remains locked until a later PLUGH AI confirmation.

After reconstruction at any PLUGH station, permit ordinary paid Medusa purchase at PLUGH stations only. Retain the independent hull price and standard trade-in, capacity, fitting, confirmation and save rules. The quest grants fabrication access, not a free ship.

## Persistence and access

Permanent commander journal records; zero cargo mass, retained after escape, ship trade, load and pack upgrade.

The final required clue provides a normal navigation beacon for the local PLUGH station. Use existing PLUGH knowledge if already discovered. This reveals no Medusa location and does not enable XYZZY. Persist the revealed PLUGH entry position so it does not move on reload.

All commanders use the same current 64-clue base set. Every original campaign, repeatable family and tutorial contributes at its verified successful terminal, once per source. Future optional paid packs may add collectible evidence without becoming prerequisites for this base-game mystery.

Save current clue receipts, decoder and ordered trail evidence, encounter identity, player kill record and fabrication entitlement in the current format. Restore normal play from those records; duplicate events cannot grant repeated rewards. Every new commander starts with no mystery progress or Medusa entitlement.

XYZZY convenience settings do not bypass this quest's discovery or fabrication gates. Isolated developer test fixtures may arrange state explicitly without changing a real commander's progress.
