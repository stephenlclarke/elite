# Native paged wrecks and original cutting cycles

Fresh Thargoid wrecks and continued imported sections can use the existing paged world instead of ordinary lifetime pools. Three typed records retain physical wreck history, immutable fresh inspection and a linked current work cycle. Old schema-27 records retain their original encoding and history. A cold plan view authenticates current issuance without encoding synthetic historical work or spending old issuance credit as a new cutting budget.

## Implementation

The fresh reader uses the original ledger's sealed deterministic factory and the shared `SalvageSectionCycle` allocator. Current work holds one section and uses genuine mesh hit, surface, fitting, abandonment, protection and visit checks from the original cutter. A bounded current authority join seals the owner, hull, scene epoch, actor, exact section, immutable source and original cycle before hot reuse and cold publication. Immutable cached maps serve frame queries; full history joins and deterministic source reconstruction run at load or mutation boundaries. Native pending admission is indexed by system.

The actor supplies a synchronous value-path writer to the original pre-movement cutter stage. A successful publication commits the original emitted lots, allocator, cycle reset and actual admission together before normal fragment movement. A rejected writer leaves the completed hot budget and real source intact. Cold pose failure restores the complete previous Commander, hot cycle and observations. Wrecks retain actual current actor bindings, exact observed motion and irreversible death; missing bodies do not silently retire bindings. Parked wrecks re-enter at their actual retained pose without offscreen simulation. Versioned load rebuilds the deterministic site inventory directly from retained anchors and seed, without a new visit, clock change, physical admission or write. An exactly joined imported human EMP hull shares this motion and atomic rebinding authority; unrelated ordinary EMP archives retain their existing coast and recovery behavior.

Existing world lots, companion custody, original collection observations, native generation publication and PLUGH fitting are reused. Legacy inspection evidence and adopted original native plans remain immutable. Known pre-inspection work, damage, ledger revision and opaque global cursor stay sealed to the complete original ledger; missing material remains unknown until actual new work observes it. New physical operations start in their own incident namespace, and the current budget begins at the exact retained partial work or zero. A shared cold allocator proof rejects phantom cycles and releases above eight while preserving short, EMPTY, final-zero, discarded and death outcomes. A bounded section reconciliation discards only still-unissued reserved rows at the current observation time. The complete current actor census includes wrecks, reserved fragments, companions and legacy current custody, while immutable historical bindings remain audit evidence. The original UInt16 per-section serial domain, local actor admission, real cargo mass, scoop aperture, protection and finite resources remain enforced. No schema-28 activation, ordinary pool increase, history deletion or source/gear grant is added.

## Compatibility and remaining risks

Normal human wrecks retain their existing backend. Existing native and imported sections route by their exact permanent section identity; a native sibling cannot redirect a fresh human section. Fresh Thargoid plans have at most two lots, so they do not independently establish repeated-eight behavior. Human multi-batch and EMP tests establish compatibility only if executed successfully, not a new source bootstrap. The frozen format oracle intentionally covers the original plan/legacy-section fixture cases in a single bounded leaf; it does not claim an exhaustive historical archive corpus. The schema-27 page codec handles the additional typed records through its existing bounded canonical pages. The normalized world remains materialized in memory; this is not a streaming database. Disk, memory, I/O, exact numeric representability and local physical admission still have real failure paths.

The change affects shared cutter, pose, death, scene-save and target status consumers, so previous native collection/PLUGH and ordinary salvage checks must be requalified where their source dependencies changed.
