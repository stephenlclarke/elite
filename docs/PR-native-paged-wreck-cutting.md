# Native paged wrecks and original cutting cycles

Fresh Thargoid wrecks and continued imported sections can use the existing paged world instead of ordinary lifetime pools. Three typed records retain physical wreck history, immutable fresh inspection and a linked current work cycle. Old schema-27 records retain their original encoding and history. A cold plan view authenticates current issuance without encoding synthetic historical work or spending old issuance credit as a new cutting budget.

## Implementation

The fresh reader uses the original ledger's sealed deterministic factory and the shared `SalvageSectionCycle` allocator. Current work holds one section and uses genuine mesh hit, surface, fitting, abandonment, protection and visit checks from the original cutter. Immutable cached maps serve frame queries; full history joins and deterministic source reconstruction run at load or mutation boundaries. Native pending admission is indexed by system.

The actor supplies a synchronous value-path writer to the original pre-movement cutter stage. A successful publication commits the original emitted lots, allocator, cycle reset and actual admission together before normal fragment movement. A rejected writer leaves the completed hot budget and real source intact. Cold pose failure restores the complete previous Commander, hot cycle and observations. Wrecks retain actual current actor bindings, exact observed motion and irreversible death; missing bodies do not silently retire bindings. Parked wrecks re-enter at their actual retained pose without offscreen simulation.

Existing world lots, companion custody, original collection observations, native generation publication and PLUGH fitting are reused. Legacy inspection evidence remains immutable; unknown old work remains unknown in the original record, while the new current budget starts at the actual retained partial work or zero. The original UInt16 per-section serial domain, local actor admission, real cargo mass, scoop aperture, protection and finite resources remain enforced. No schema-28 activation, ordinary pool increase, history deletion or source/gear grant is added.

## Validation

Ten new methods contain fourteen cases. They cover all six full ordinary pools, genuine generated inspection and actor release, current save and no-write reload, writer refusal and retry without repeat cutting costs, common release-frame movement, imported partial mining/cutter work, old single-budget compatibility, death, physical cull/revisit missing/duplicate/rebound/moved scene actors and complete hot-session retention on refused save/dock/arrival/galactic boundaries. Source formatting and scoped Markdown/whitespace checks are performed before review. Compilation, discovery, runtime outcomes and coverage are not claimed.

## Compatibility and remaining risks

Normal human wrecks retain their existing backend. Existing native and imported sections route by their permanent original source identity. The schema-27 page codec handles the additional typed records through its existing bounded canonical pages. The normalized world remains materialized in memory; this is not a streaming database. Disk, memory, I/O, exact numeric representability and local physical admission still have real failure paths.

The change affects shared cutter, pose, death, scene-save and target status consumers, so previous native collection/PLUGH and ordinary salvage checks must be requalified where their source dependencies changed. Full `make ci`, renderer media generation and main-only SonarQube remain pending. Companion consumption, real AppKit alien-row proof, authentic Gallery acquisition and the broader feature/release list are not completed by this leaf.
