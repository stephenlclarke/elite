# EMP-disabled ships must stop firing and turn orange immediately

The user's latest instruction supersedes the initial policy that allowed an occupied disabled hostile to keep firing and remain red until its final crew member ejected. An accepted EMP must stop future ordinary NPC laser/missile emission and immediately show orange HUD/world cues, even with crew aboard. Existing emitted missiles remain independent live red threats. Aggression, original resources/cargo/legal custody and protected occupants must remain intact. See [implementation](PR-emp-weapon-disablement.md).
