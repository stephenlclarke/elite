# RRS protected documents assignment

Station Contracts consumes the native RRS board context at real waystation and headquarters docks. The headquarters board remains the approved project extension. Generic Lua contract boards retain their original main-station requirement. Base scenario 2 is the only enabled RRS mission profile.

The optional RRS contract ledger retains one cache entry per station, keyed by the actual station, whole persisted simulation hour and profile revision. A SHA-256 seed supplies a deterministic 53-bit draw; the source gate remains strictly below 0.5. Board generation, decline and acceptance are retained through the existing writer. Refresh and reload do not reroll a station's hour. The typed active slot is shared across RRS origins. Original project prose describes the terms; no source narrative, image or model is imported by this consumer.

Confirm checks the current sealed dock context, source descriptor, epoch, hour, legal/reputation gates, complete quote resources, active slot and living original-main destination. Acceptance freezes the genuine RRS receipt, source/project provenance, adapter revisions, original main identity and acceptance time. A unique protected digital packet exists only in the typed ledger: zero cargo tonnes and no equipment, pylon or cabin allocation. Trading, jettison and equipment storage have no packet entry to consume. Acceptance does not launch, refill or debit the ship.

The Station Contracts page exposes delivery and abandonment even when no new offer exists. Genuine original-main arrival and debrief Confirm record its immutable arrival receipt and once-only payment of 300 tenths of a credit. A terminal receipt removes active packet custody; completed and failed counts and reputation derive from the receipt history. The ordinary courier terminal observer also checks real system/galaxy departure and proved destruction of the frozen original main. Unavailable evidence leaves custody active and delivery disabled. An original-main tombstone cannot be replaced by a generic station search. Terminal publication uses the existing private candidate publisher; a failed writer keeps the active packet and retries rather than publishing a second receipt.

Save validation joins the ledger to the canonical equipment owner, authenticated native station records and original-main namespace. Before materializing board or job values, the custom decoder rejects more than 372 board keys or a known job count above 1024; incremental guards also bound decoders without a known count. It then checks deterministic board/packet identities, source revisions, accepted eligibility, chronological nonoverlapping assignments and terminal receipts. Transient quotes and sealed contexts are not serialized as authority. Native service facts and Boolean dock flags cannot confirm delivery. The existing retained-state durability trigger includes the RRS ledger, and painted-page proofs include current service epoch/hour, packet/assignment identity and original-main arrival identity.
