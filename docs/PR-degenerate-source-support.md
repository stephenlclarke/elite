# Retain original three-vertex collision supports

## Change

Prepare mixed original meshes without discarding their unresolved three-vertex source primitives. Preparation caches area resolution and support/conditioning flags, requires at least one certified surface in the mesh, and retains every primitive and all original component edges. A degenerate-only component cannot gain solid eligibility from edge incidence. The existing whole-component conditioning policy applies to every containment hit, including broad faces connected to an unresolved support.

## Conservative distance proof

For any finite axis, outward interval projections enclose all three original vertices. The positive gap between projection ranges divided by an upper bound on the axis length is a separation lower bound. Taking the maximum over arbitrary axes remains a lower bound; inaccurate approximate closest features affect tightness only. Point queries use ten bounded axes against the complete three-point convex support. Triangle-pair queries involving an unresolved support use 32 bounded axes against both complete three-point supports. Intersecting supports have overlapping ranges on every axis and cannot obtain positive separation. Filled nonzero interiors are retained even if an area/normal division cannot be certified.

The whole mesh remains the union of original primitives. Its distance is the minimum of primitive lower bounds, and the original maximum vertex radius encloses every convex support. The continuous linear/angular envelope, required clearance, strict numerical headroom and subdivision/query budgets are unchanged. Candidate axes that are nonfinite or cannot be normalised contribute zero, not safety. Coordinate axes ensure that resolved exterior gaps can still produce useful positive clearance.

For meshes or query triangles with unresolved support, BVH pruning uses outward-rounded box-distance lower bounds and an upper bound on the square of the current best lower bound. A discarded node therefore has true distance at least that best bound. A truly closer primitive cannot be pruned by this certificate. Ordinary traversal-order heuristics do not grant clearance; nodes and primitive queries retain their original charges.

Triangle queries visit the child with the nearer approximate AABB gap first; ties preserve the original left-first order. These values only choose visitation order and never replace the pruning certificate or change work charges. A two-leaf analytic regression retains all sixteen original primitives, including a zero support, and requires three useful exact-player stationary samples within the same 24-triangle/9-node budget, plus overlapping-support refusal and reversed source order. Fixed left-first traversal would spend sixteen triangle tests on the first sample before reaching the nearer leaf. This is a traversal optimization; measured improvement remains unproven until later runtime acceptance.

Support projections enclose the represented Double geometry. The existing pose conversions use two affine transforms, at most 36 elementary operations per vertex with validated near-orthonormal basis components. Their forward error is bounded by a small multiple of gamma(36) times the same radii/position magnitudes already present in the retained `1e-8` numerical headroom. Underflow is dominated by its minimum unit scale; overflow cannot produce an authorised finite separation proof. Thus no normal-conditioning error or new unchecked pose-error exemption is introduced. Pose/scale/translation and native acceptance still require genuine execution rather than assuming this source argument is a measured result.

## Transformed-query certification correction

Preparation still computes and caches the complete interval area certificate once. Repeated transformed-player construction can reuse a resolved, unconditioned original classification only if the represented transformed vertices are finite, the existing scalar area/scale routing checks remain well conditioned, and one selected outward interval cross-product component excludes zero. The approximate normal selects that component only; it grants no authority. Four outward coordinate differences, two outward products and one outward subtraction enclose the exact determinant of those represented vertices. A finite interval wholly above or below zero implies strictly nonzero area without constructing all three interval components, their squares or a square root. Every uncertain, near-collinear, previously conditioned, unresolved, collapsed or nonfinite case retains the full original interval-area construction and existing support fallback. Neither floating rigid-transform intent nor cached original resolution alone authorizes the fast path.

The new source regressions retain ordinary rotated/translated exact-player surface clearance, near-collinear exterior/contact checks and an actual large-translation collapse of an originally resolved player triangle. The collapsed case reaches charged triangle queries and still refuses with the retained numerical-headroom uncertainty. All earlier 179 declared cases remain intact. No primitive, pruning/ray/containment rule, pose headroom, clearance or work budget changes. This removes source-proven eager full interval-normal/length work from eligible transformed queries; selected-component cost and actual runtime improvement remain unmeasured, with compilation and execution pending independent acceptance.

## Ray and containment proof

Outward slab intervals enclose every possible intersection with the primitive's original vertex bounds. For an unresolved primitive, interval endpoints enclose the finite ray candidate segment. Fifteen fixed candidate axes can certify a positive separation between that enclosed segment and the full original support, proving a ray miss without any area division. Otherwise interval determinant/barycentric predicates must certify the outcome; a zero or unresolved determinant is a boundary/unknown result, never permission to skip the primitive. Alternate containment rays or the existing unproven refusal handle uncertainty.

All containment hits in a component with thin/small/unresolved supports remain conditioned. Distinct close broad-face crossings cannot be merged into a fictitious exterior. Exact original component edges and open-interior refusal are retained. A component with no resolved surface remains ineligible for solid containment, even if its incidence table is closed. No source face is removed and no closed flag is forced. Positive separation outside an original component bounding box is useful; unresolved interior or ray classifications may still refuse honestly.

## Larger-player dual-BVH traversal

For player BVHs with internal nodes (more than eight original primitives), traverse original player/target node pairs after both unchanged component-containment loops. Keep the original per-player-triangle path byte-for-byte for small player meshes, including the single-triangle exact-budget regression. No primitive or filled support is substituted or removed; leaf pairs call the same transformed `SweepTriangle` certification and original pair-distance function. Nonfinite represented leaf vertices refuse with `unproven`.

Each player-node box is transformed lazily within the query using outward interval operations in the exact `systemPoint` then `shipPoint` order: `(basis.x*x + basis.y*y) + basis.z*z`, world-position addition, target-position subtraction, then left-associated target-basis dot products. Outward rounding after every arithmetic operation encloses both exact results and rounded represented intermediates for every original vertex in the box. No ideal composite transform or unchecked floating corner transform is used. The transformed AABB encloses the convex support of every represented descendant primitive. A nonfinite/unknown enclosure becomes an unbounded box and cannot prune.

For every pair, a finite certified squared AABB-gap lower bound can prune only against the finite outward upper square of a finite current best primitive lower bound. Every omitted primitive therefore has true separation at least that best bound; taking the minimum of retained primitive lower bounds stays conservative. Infinity compared with infinity never authorizes pruning. Finite uncertain gaps retain descent. Approximate center gaps and original box extents only choose traversal/split order; ties retain deterministic left-first visitation.

Every popped pair charges two existing node visits before node/bounds/cache work, even for a cached node or pruned pair. This pays for both examinations and fixed bounded interval-transform/child-order work. Every leaf primitive pair charges one existing triangle test before cache lookup, first transformation/certification and exact comparison. No eager all-node/all-triangle pass runs. Query-local caches only retain charged visited player nodes/primitives; the depth-first pair stack grows with tree depths. All existing distance-query charges, containment/ray work, budget values, numerical headroom, continuous-motion envelopes, required clearances and refusal rules remain intact.

Seven new source methods add an analytic two-eight-face-leaf near/far witness under the same 192-pair budget for three stationary samples, reversed original source order, zero/sliver retention, actual 191-pair and one-node exhaustion, overlap/crossing, rotated/translated poses, actual represented collapse, overflow enclosure and concave-notch/solid-arm checks. The near leaves cost 64 pairs per sample; far-first source traversal must spend at least one extra eight-pair leaf before those same near comparisons. The analytic witness checks useful distance near 10 and charged work, with a conservative 96-node allowance rather than a guessed exact node count. It is a source-declared counterexample to unnecessary primitive work, not an executed benchmark. All earlier 182 source cases remain unchanged; combined newly authored source cases are 189.

Worst-case work remains quadratic in player/target primitives and node pairs, bounded by the unchanged budgets. Loose rotated boxes, overlap, interval overhead and cache memory can erase expected savings; containment may become dominant. No runtime improvement, all-hull completion or native comparison is claimed.
