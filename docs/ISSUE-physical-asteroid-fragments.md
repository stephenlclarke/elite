# Preserve physical mass after ordinary asteroid breakup

## Problem and approved scope

The approved L337 design requires all laser-shattered asteroid mass and momentum to remain dangerous, with strictly less than five percent of original intact mass collectible. MiningLedger.physicalHit previously tombstoned an ordinary weapon kill with no retained physical mass. Ore destruction and departure also erased payload records. Repeated descendant work must not create another original-mass budget.

This first bounded phase covers canonical ring-rock birth mass, current-format custody, finite shared salvage, actual parent motion and real native aggregate bodies. The coordinator explicitly permits a physical aggregate for unresolved fragments and dust. It does not close the full hazard feature or any campaign.

## Required invariants

- Native commodity units determine birth mass: minerals/alloys use tonnes, gold/platinum kilograms. Collision-space radius is not a metre-based density estimate.
- The original parent actor becomes the aggregate in place. No replacement allocation, forged actor or missing-mass transition is needed for breakup. Failure to reserve persistent custody retains the intact parent and resource history.
- One original mass budget covers partial mining work, all issued descendants, destroyed ore, collection, save/load and revisit. Strict integer bounds must remain below five percent, including exact multiples of twenty units.
- Actual admission receipts distinguish issued reservations from materialized canisters. Until first admission the physical parent retains reserved mass. Refused admission keeps both parent mass and pending custody.
- Destroyed breakup ore becomes noncollectible physical rubble; it cannot replenish the collectible budget. Departure/cull never deletes these descendants.

## Required next phase

Native swept debris impacts against player, ships and stations remain open. Existing GameplayRaycast and native ordnance consume the aggregate's solid mesh, but that is not a moving debris impact producer. See the exact follow-up seams in PR-physical-asteroid-fragments.md. No mission completion, station-destruction receipt, reward or narrative gate may substitute for real contact and resource damage.

## Phase-one review corrections

Independent source review found that removing a bound descendant's materialization marker could double count its mass in the parent, and that the pending-offer planner used the cargo quota even for asteroid rubble. Bound children and collected child identities must each retain their own materialization marker. Genuinely culled children remain materialized. Cargo and rubble must reserve their own population quotas as well as the shared ordinary budget, including outstanding offers.
