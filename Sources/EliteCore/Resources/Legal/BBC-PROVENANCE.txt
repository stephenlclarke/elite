BBC Elite reference material - rights retained

Elite was created by David Braben and Ian Bell.
Original 6502 Second Processor distribution identifies Acornsoft Limited,
1985. Mark Moxon supplies the annotated historical source reference.

Pinned reference:
https://github.com/markmoxon/elite-source-code-6502-second-processor/tree/9080bd09c814b43c9c82571493c74bfb447aa607

This project's MIT licence does not apply to retained original Elite
blueprint coordinates, narrative or designs, and is not a claim of
redistribution permission for the complete game.

Native uses include Constrictor and Thargon numerical blueprints in
NavalShips.swift and the Cobra blueprint coordinates in AppIcon.swift.
The original assembly source, ROMs and upstream commentary are not bundled
in the app. The implementation adds camera-axis reflection, native face
construction/triangulation, shading, projection and an original icon layout.
Those additions do not replace the original rights.
