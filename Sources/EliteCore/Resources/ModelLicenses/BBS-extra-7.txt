Z GrOovy Binary Basic Cobra Mk3 regular and Scuffed versions {1.0} 2-Jan-2014

*** Contents ***

- Cobra Mk III

The first goal of the binary basic shippack is to redux all existing canon ships from Elite/Oolite adding details to the models while keeping the general shape of the model shape basic and close to the early games.
I will be reduxing the color of these ships in the fashion I have done with the Eagles.

The second goal will be to add non canon ships that fit the Ooniverse and the Elite, but as an addon pack so you can keep both canon and non canon separate.

Thanks to Griff, this version has random colors, lasers that heat up red, engines that glow... the full monty. Griff was so kind to write this custom shader based on his Arc Elite set and especially adapted for this ship.
Without Griff, and his texture notes. This pack would not be so diverse, nor would it be released like it is now. Thank you my friend.

*** specifics ***

This is the BBS Cobra Mk III, which stock specs to maintain Oolite gameplay. It is just a model changeover and is randomly colored by shader, you will have to wait till you see a color scheme you like at your favorite shipyard.
The scuffed versions and the regular one are not that much different. Just a few hullplates removed for the scuffed version.

Have fun!

*** Dependables/Requirements ***

This oxp is standalone, as will be all next upcoming ships. I will be adding all ships to a single file though, so the next time a ship is released, the adder will again be in the zip. Please copy both files again to your addons folder, as I might have done updates on previous ships. 

*** Installation ***

Delete any previous files with the same name and install this one when updating to a newer version.
As always, copy the OXP to the addons directory and while starting up Oolite hold the SHIFT key until you see the spinning cobra.
