ADDITIONAL CORRESPONDING SOURCE ASSEMBLIES (28 RECORDS)

Black Monks (P04), Deposed (P09), Ionics (P18), Military Fiasco (P26),
Stealth (P36), Thargoid Wars (P41), Random Hits (P29), Staer9 no-shader
shipset (S9), Santa and Selezen Imperial Courier (IC).

Original authors, full credits, manifest evidence and licenses are preserved
in ModelLicenses; index.json maps every notice to its source archive path
and SHA256. The catalogue records the exact source archive SHA256s;
Each ship TOML records source definition, component identities,
shipdata hash and DAT file hashes. Source identifiers are provenance only;
runtime preview IDs use the xyzzy-showroom-SHxxx namespace. No source role,
mission variable, equipment identifier or script is activated. Stealth's
additional compatibility clauses are included verbatim in its notice.

Adaptations additionally resolve like_ship inheritance, legacy string and
dictionary subentities, component positions/quaternions and uniform source
scale. The complete assembly is normalized once for the native preview.
Oolite renditions are attributed in the TOMLs and source/design appendix;
they do not prove original Elite-A, BBC, Frontier or Elite Dangerous geometry.
The remaining 93 records use original MIT project interpretations. They are
not presented as historical geometry; their designs never establish specs.
